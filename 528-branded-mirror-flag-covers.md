# Branded Mirror Flag Covers

A mirror flag — a printed stretch cover that slips over a folded side mirror — is a trade tool rather than a consumer accessory. It is used to identify, advertise and price vehicles in dealerships, auction yards, workshops and at events.

Its value comes from repetition: dozens of vehicles in a row, each carrying the same mark, at eye level and at a distance.

## What it is used for

- **Dealer forecourts**, to mark a vehicle as sold, reserved, or as part of a promotion.
- **Auctions and remarketing**, to identify lots or display a sale message.
- **Workshops**, to indicate a vehicle's status in the queue.
- **Trade shows and launch events**, to brand a display line of vehicles.
- **Test-drive fleets**, to mark demonstrators.

In each case the requirement is the same: a cover that fits quickly, stays on a folded mirror, and shows a clear mark at a distance.

## Why the mirror

The folded side mirror is a convenient mounting point. It is at a consistent height, it projects from the vehicle, and it presents a surface that is visible from the front and from the side.

It is also safe to cover: unlike a mirror left exposed, a covered mirror is protected from knocks in a crowded yard.

## Material and construction

- **Stretch polyester knit**, which grips the mirror housing without fasteners and prints well.
- **Spandex blend**, for a wider size range and a tighter fit.
- **Single-layer construction**, which is adequate because the product is not weather protection.
- **Double-sided printing**, since the flag is seen from both sides.

The material must be thin enough to fold into a pocket and tough enough to survive being pulled on and off hundreds of times.

## Sizing

Mirror flags are usually sold in a small range rather than one size:

- **Cars and small SUVs**, which covers most forecourt stock.
- **Large SUVs and vans**, with wider housings.
- **Truck mirrors**, which need a distinctly larger size.

A flag that is too small will not pass over the housing; one that is too large will sag and turn in the wind.

## Print

- **Sublimation** for full-colour work, which suits photographic or multi-colour branding.
- **Screen printing** for one or two spot colours at volume.
- **Colour fastness**, since the flags live outdoors. A flag that fades within a season undermines the brand it carries.

Because the flag is read at a distance, the design should prioritise a large mark and high contrast over fine detail. Small text is wasted at forecourt distances.

## Operational use

- **Sets per site**, rather than single units, since yards use them in batches.
- **Colour coding**, so different messages can be distinguished at a glance.
- **Reusability**, because the flag is removed when a vehicle is sold and reused on the next one.
- **Washability**, since forecourt flags collect dust and handling marks.

## What to specify

- the mirror size range covered
- fabric composition and stretch
- single or double-sided print
- print method and colour fastness
- packing in sets
- expected reuse cycles before the elastic or print fails

## The commercial case

A mirror flag is a low-cost, high-visibility item that a dealer reorders as stock turns over. That makes repeatability more important than unit price: a consistent mark across a forecourt is the whole point, and a flag that prints differently on each order defeats it.
