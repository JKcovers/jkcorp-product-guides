# Outdoor Furniture Cover Buying Guide — Patio, Garden, and Poolside Protection

Outdoor furniture is expensive — a quality patio set costs $500-3,000. A furniture cover that costs $20-50 protects that investment from sun, rain, and bird droppings for years.

## Material Choices

- **Oxford with PU coating:** Waterproof, UV resistant, breathable. The standard choice for outdoor furniture covers.
- **PEVA:** 100% waterproof but degrades in 6-12 months of sun. Budget option for covered patios.
- **Ripstop polyester:** Lightweight but strong. Good for large sectional covers that need to be lightweight enough to put on solo.

## Sizing Tips

Measure the total width, depth, and height of your furniture set. Add 4-6 inches to each measurement for easy on/off. For L-shaped sectionals, measure each piece individually — you may need two covers.

## Essential Features

- **Ventilation flaps:** Prevent condensation and mildew
- **Adjustable drawstrings or click-close straps:** Keep the cover on in wind
- **Handles:** For easy removal by one person
- **Dark colors:** Hide dirt and mildew stains better than light colors
