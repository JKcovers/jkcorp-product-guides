# Pet Seat Cover Installation — 2-Minute Perfect Fit Every Time

Most pet seat cover complaints — sliding, gaps, and shifting — trace back to installation mistakes. Here's the correct 2-minute installation that keeps the cover in place for months.

## Step 1: Remove the Headrests (If Removable)

If your headrests are removable, take them off. This is the biggest installation mistake — covers installed with headrests in place never seat correctly around the posts.

## Step 2: Position the Cover

Drape the cover over the seatback, aligning the top edge with the top of the seat. The cover should extend past the seat bottom cushion toward the seatback bottom.

## Step 3: Reinstall Headrests Through the Holes

Put the headrests back, passing the posts through the cover's pre-cut holes. This locks the top of the cover in place.

## Step 4: Anchor the Seatback Straps

Reach behind the seat and connect the seatback straps around the seat's lower area (or clip them to the seat track/brackets if provided). This holds the cover against the seatback.

## Step 5: Secure the Bottom

Tuck the bottom edge under the seat cushion, or use the bottom straps if included. The cover should have zero visible slack.

## Step 6: Smooth and Check

Run your hand across the surface to smooth wrinkles. Check for gaps at the sides where paws or debris could slip through.

## Maintenance Between Rides

- Shake out fur after each ride
- Vacuum weekly
- Wash monthly (or after accidents)
- Re-check strap tension monthly — straps stretch over time
