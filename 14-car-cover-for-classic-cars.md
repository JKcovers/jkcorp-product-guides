# Car Covers for Classic Cars — Protecting Your Investment the Right Way

Classic cars deserve better protection than a generic car cover. The wrong cover can scratch decades-old paint, trap moisture that accelerates rust, or fit so poorly that it causes more harm than good. Here's how to choose the right cover for your classic.

## Soft Inner Lining Is Non-Negotiable

Modern clear coats are harder than vintage paint. A cover that's safe for a 2025 Honda can micro-scratch a 1965 Mustang's single-stage paint. The inner layer must be fleece or non-woven cotton — not polyester, not PEVA, not anything that feels rough to the touch. If you wouldn't wipe your car with the inner fabric, don't cover your car with it.

## Breathability Over Waterproofing

Classic cars are more rust-prone than modern vehicles. A fully waterproof but non-breathable cover traps condensation against the body, accelerating rust in door seams, trunk channels, and underbody panels. Choose a cover with a breathable middle layer and a waterproof outer shell — not a solid plastic sheet.

## Indoor vs Outdoor Storage

Indoor storage (garage, carport): A soft, breathable dust cover is sufficient. Multi-layer isn't necessary. Fleece-lined polyester or cotton blends work perfectly and cost less.

Outdoor storage (driveway, carport without walls): You need full weather protection. Multi-layer Oxford with 99% UV rejection, waterproof outer shell, and soft fleece inner is the minimum for outdoor classic car storage.

## Custom Fit Is Worth the Investment

Classic cars have unique body lines that universal covers can't accommodate. A cover designed for a generic "Large Sedan" won't fit a 1969 Charger properly. Spend the extra money on a semi-custom or custom cover sized to your exact make, model, and year. It's a small fraction of your car's value.

## Storage Tips

- Never cover a wet or dirty classic car. Wash and fully dry first.
- Use a car capsule or bubble for long-term storage in humid climates.
- Remove the cover monthly to inspect for moisture or pest issues.
- Wash the cover every 3-6 months.
