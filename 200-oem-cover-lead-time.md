# Lead Time Management for OEM Covers — Plan Orders Without Stress

Lead time is where import plans succeed or silently fail. A "shipped in 3 weeks" promise that becomes 6 weeks can wreck a seasonal launch. Understanding how OEM cover lead times are built lets you plan confidently — and negotiate honestly.

## Typical OEM Cover Lead Times

| Stage | Stock Design | Custom Design |
|-------|:--:|:--:|
| Sample production | 3-7 days | 7-15 days |
| Sample delivery (courier) | 3-7 days | 3-7 days |
| Bulk production | 15-25 days | 20-35 days |
| **Total from order to ship** | **~3-4 weeks** | **~5-7 weeks** |

## What Drives the Timeline

- **Fabric procurement** — stock fabric is ready; custom-woven or custom-color fabric adds 10-20 days
- **Printing setup** — logo screens and plates take 3-5 days before sewing starts
- **Production capacity** — peak seasons (Aug-Oct) stretch lead times
- **QC and packing** — final inspection and packaging add 3-5 days
- **Holidays** — Chinese New Year and National Day shift schedules for 2-4 weeks

## How to Manage Lead Time Like a Pro

### 1. Plan Backward from Your Deadline

Work back from your on-sale date: shipping + customs + inland transit + warehouse time. Add a 20% buffer. That is your required ship date — tell the factory it is a hard date.

### 2. Lock the Timeline in Writing

Get the confirmed schedule (sample → material → production → inspection → shipment) in the contract, with clear dates, not "about 4 weeks."

### 3. Ask for Milestone Updates

- Material arrival photos
- Production start photos
- Mid-production status
- Packing and loading confirmation

### 4. Split Large Orders

Splitting a 5,000-pc order into two shipments gets the first batch to market 2-3 weeks earlier — especially for seasonal products.

### 5. Order Off-Peak

Book production for January-June when capacity is open; expect pressure in Q3.

## Payment Milestones and Timing

Typical OEM payment structure: 30% deposit to start production, 70% balance before shipment (or against the shipping documents). Production does not start until the deposit arrives — factor banking time into your schedule.

## Reducing Lead Time

- Choose stock fabric and standard colors
- Approve samples and proofs on the first round
- Reply to questions within 24 hours
- Pre-agree packaging so no approval round is needed
