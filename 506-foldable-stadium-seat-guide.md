# Foldable Stadium Chair with Backrest Guide

A backrest turns a stadium cushion into a different product. The moment the cushion has to hold a seated adult leaning backwards, the load path runs through a frame, and the frame — not the foam — becomes the part that decides whether the product lasts.

## What the backrest changes

A flat pad is loaded in compression only. A seat with a backrest is loaded in compression and bending, and it has to resist the tendency to tip backwards when the sitter leans. That single change drives the choice of frame, hinge and seat depth.

It also changes the specification conversation. Buyers who compare a flat pad and a backrest chair on foam thickness alone are comparing the wrong component.

## Frame and hinge

Frames fall into two families:

- **Tubular steel frames** are the conventional choice. They are strong and inexpensive but heavier, and the coating quality decides whether they rust after a season of damp storage.
- **Aluminium frames** trade cost for weight. They suit products aimed at spectators who carry the chair to the ground and back.

The hinge is the component that fails first on cheap chairs. A riveted joint with a positive stop holds a set angle; a friction hinge that slowly sags leaves the sitter leaning further back each time. Ask how the hinge is locked and whether the locked angle is fixed or adjustable.

## Seat depth and backrest height

Seat depth has to clear the bleacher plank underneath. A chair with a deep seat used on a narrow bench tends to tip forward because the load sits ahead of the support, so the base needs either a wider footprint or a front lip that grips the plank.

Backrest height is a comfort-versus-pack-size decision. A taller backrest supports more of the spine but increases the folded profile.

## Folding, carrying and stability

Because the product is defined by being carried, the folded envelope and the weight matter as much as the open dimensions. Check:

- **Folded thickness** — this drives carton size and shipping cost.
- **Carry handle or strap** — padded, and positioned so the folded chair hangs level.
- **Folding action** — one-handed closing is a genuine selling point at a crowded exit.
- **Stability on a bench** — a chair that rocks on a bleacher gets returned regardless of how comfortable it is.

Ask the supplier for the rated static load and test it against the heaviest user the product is intended to serve.

## Customisation

Team and school programmes print the backrest panel, which is the largest flat surface on the chair. Confirm print placement relative to the frame tubes and the fold line, because artwork that crosses either will distort or crack. Some buyers add a stitched number or name panel for individual sales.

## Buying checklist

- frame material and coating suited to the storage conditions
- hinge type and whether the angle is locked or friction-held
- seat depth matched to the intended bench width
- folded size and weight for the retail channel
- rated load confirmed in writing, not assumed
