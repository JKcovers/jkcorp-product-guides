# Outdoor Sofa Cover Guide — Fit, Fabric and Features

An outdoor sofa is a major investment, and the fabric cushions on it are the first thing to fail. An outdoor sofa cover protects both the frame and the cushions — if you choose it correctly. This guide covers fitting, fabric, and the features that actually matter.

## Measuring Your Outdoor Sofa

- **Width:** measure across the widest point of the arms
- **Depth:** from the front edge of the seat to the back of the backrest
- **Height:** from the floor to the top of the backrest
- **Add 10-15 cm** to each dimension for a comfortable fit over cushions

## Cover Fabric Options

| Fabric | Waterproof | Breathable | Durability |
|--------|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 420D Oxford PU | Good | Good | Good |
| 600D Oxford PU | Excellent | Good | Excellent |
| PVC / vinyl | Excellent | Poor | Good |
| PEVA | Good | Poor | Fair |

For outdoor sofas, 600D Oxford with a breathable lining is the sweet spot — it sheds rain while letting condensation escape.

## Features That Matter

- **Full-length zipper or strap closures** — keeps the cover anchored in wind
- **Elastic hem** — fits snugly over the sofa base
- **Padded or fleece-lined top** — protects cushions from rubbing and scratching
- **Air vents** — prevents mildew on humid nights
- **Water-repellent zippers** — rain can seep through ordinary zippers

## Protecting Cushions Under the Cover

The biggest mistake is covering cushions that are still damp. Air-dry cushions fully, then cover. For extra protection, some owners place cushions upright or stack them under a dedicated cushion storage bag before covering the frame.

## Cleaning and Care

- Brush off leaves and debris before covering
- Wipe the cover clean with mild soap quarterly
- Check straps after storms
- In spring, wash and air-dry the cover before storing it

## How Long Should a Cover Last?

A quality 600D Oxford outdoor sofa cover lasts 3-5 seasons with basic care. Cheap PEVA covers often fail within one. The difference is the coating quality, seam tape, and UV stabilizers — not the color or style.

## Key Takeaways

- Measure width, depth, and height, then add 10-15 cm for a proper fit over cushions
- 600D Oxford with a breathable lining is the best balance of protection and airflow
- Cover only dry cushions — damp fabric under a cover is a mildew farm
