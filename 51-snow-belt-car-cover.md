# Car Cover for Snow Belt States — Surviving the Hardest Winter Conditions

If you live in Minnesota, Michigan, upstate New York, or anywhere that sees 50+ inches of snow annually, your car cover needs to be built for punishment. Here's what snow-belt protection demands.

## The Snow Belt Threat List

- **Snow load:** 12 inches of wet snow on a car roof can weigh 200+ lbs, stressing panel seams and sealant.
- **Freeze-thaw cycles:** Water freezes in micro-cracks, expands, and widens gaps. Repeat 30-40 times per winter.
- **Road salt mist:** Parked cars near roads collect salt-laden air that accelerates underbody corrosion.
- **Ice scraping:** Plastic scrapers leave micro-scratches that accumulate into visible damage over 4-5 winters.

## Minimum Specs for Snow Belt Covers

- 600D Oxford or heavier — lighter fabrics tear under snow load
- 5000mm waterproof rating — handles melting snow that pools on the cover
- Multi-layer with fleece inner — cold paint is brittle and scratch-prone
- Full under-body strap system — winter winds regularly exceed 30 mph
- Dark color — absorbs what little winter sun there is, helping melt light snow

## Usage Routine

- Brush heavy snow off the cover after each storm (use a soft broom, never a shovel)
- Wash the cover monthly to remove salt residue
- Let the cover dry fully once a week — take it off on a sunny day
