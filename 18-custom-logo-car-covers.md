# Custom Logo Car Covers — Why Every Dealership and Shop Should Offer Them

Branded car covers are one of the most underutilized marketing tools in the automotive industry. Every time a customer covers their car, your logo is displayed to their neighbors, passersby, and anyone in the parking lot. Here's why custom logo covers are worth the investment.

## Marketing ROI That Keeps Working

A custom car cover with your dealership or shop logo costs $3-8 more per unit than a blank cover. Over a 3-year lifespan, that cover is seen hundreds of times by dozens of people — in driveways, parking lots, car shows, and storage facilities. Cost per impression: fractions of a cent.

Compare that to digital ads ($2-5 CPM), billboards ($3-8 CPM), or direct mail ($500-1000 CPM). A branded cover is the most cost-effective outdoor advertising you can buy.

## What Customers Actually Want

Car owners who buy covers from dealerships want three things: protection for their investment, convenience, and a premium experience. A high-quality cover with your dealership's logo reinforces all three. It says "this dealership sells quality accessories" and "they stand behind their products."

## Customization Options

**Logo placement:** Roof center (most visible), front hood, or both.

**Printing methods:** Silkscreen (most durable, best for solid logos), embroidery (premium look, best for text logos), heat transfer (good for complex multi-color designs).

**Additional branding:** Custom storage bag with logo, custom packaging box, custom care instruction tag.

## Sizes to Stock

Most dealerships need 3-4 sizes to cover their inventory mix:

| Size | Fits | Typical % of Sales |

| Large | Large sedans, small SUVs | 30% |

## Profit Margin Opportunity

A quality OEM multi-layer cover costs $15-25 wholesale with logo. Retail at $80-120. That's a 300-500% margin on a product that requires zero shelf space and never expires. Even including free installation as a value-add, the margin is excellent.

1. Choose a manufacturer with ISO certification and 5+ years on Alibaba
2. Order samples in each size to check quality and logo placement
3. Start with 50-100 units in your best-selling size
4. Offer as an add-on at vehicle delivery
5. Display one on a showroom car so customers can see the quality
