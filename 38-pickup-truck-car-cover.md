# Car Cover for Pickup Trucks — Sizing Tiers and Fit Notes

Pickups are the hardest vehicles to fit with a cover. A sedan is one volume; a pickup is two — a tall cab and a flat bed — joined at a step that any cover has to bridge.

## The five universal size tiers

Universal covers are sized by overall length, bumper to bumper including the tailgate. The market divides into five tiers, coded T1 to T5 or PK-S to PK-XXL.

| Tier | Overall length | Pattern size (L × W × H) | Max cab | Representative models |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 (PK-S) | 195–206 in (495–523 cm) | 206 × 75 × 60 in (523 × 190 × 152 cm) | ≤140 in (355 cm) | Tacoma Regular/Access Cab 6' bed; Colorado/Canyon Extended Cab 6' bed; Frontier King Cab |
| T2 (PK-M) | 207–218 in (525–554 cm) | 218 × 78 × 60 in (554 × 198 × 152 cm) | ≤155 in (394 cm) | Tacoma Double Cab 5' bed; Ranger Crew Cab 5' bed; Colorado Crew Cab 5.2' bed; Jeep Gladiator; Hilux, D-Max |
| T3 (PK-L) | 219–232 in (556–589 cm) | 230 × 80 × 62 in (584 × 203 × 157 cm) | ≤155 in (394 cm) | F-150 Regular Cab 8' bed and SuperCab 6.5' bed; Silverado 1500 Double Cab 6.5' bed; RAM 1500 Quad Cab 6'4" bed |
| T4 (PK-XL) | 233–248 in (592–630 cm) | 245–248 × 80 × 65 in (622–630 × 203 × 165 cm) | ≤168 in (426 cm) | F-150 SuperCrew 5.5' bed; Silverado 1500 Crew Cab 5.8'; RAM 1500 Crew Cab 5'7"; Tundra CrewMax 5.5'; Titan Crew Cab |
| T5 (PK-XXL) | 249–268 in (632–680 cm) | 260–265 × 82 × 70 in (660–673 × 208 × 178 cm) | ≤175 in (445 cm) | F-150 SuperCrew 6.5' bed; Super Duty F-250/F-350 Crew Cab; Silverado 2500HD/3500HD Crew Cab; RAM 2500/3500 Crew Cab 8' |

## Cab-to-bed ratio: the second variable

Two trucks can share an overall length and still need different patterns, because length alone does not describe the shape. A regular cab pairs a short cab with a long bed, an extended cab sits in between, and a crew cab — the highest-volume configuration in North America — pairs a long cab with a short bed.

## Patterning a universal cover

Three tolerances make one pattern work across a tier:

**Cab-to-bed transition.** The junction between cab and bed is a dropped area. The cover needs a sloped drape with roughly 10 to 15 cm of slack across it, so rain and snow do not pool against the seams.

**Width at the A-pillar.** Universal pickup covers normally have no fixed mirror pockets, so the front section needs at least 80 inches (203 cm) of width to clear the mirrors folded or extended.

**Elastic hem.** Front and rear hems should use high-recovery elastic with around 15 percent stretch allowance, so the cover grips bumper and tailgate across different wheelbases.

## The simplified three-SKU range

Buyers limiting inventory can cover most real demand with three patterns: a T2 size at 218 × 78 × 60 inches for Tacoma, Ranger and Colorado; a T4 size at 245 × 80 × 65 inches for F-150, Silverado 1500 and RAM 1500; and a T5 size at 262 × 82 × 70 inches for F-250/350 and Silverado 2500HD.

## Beds, accessories and handling

A bed-mounted toolbox, ladder rack or headache rack breaks a standard pattern; the cover needs cut-outs or a raised section, and the accessory height and position must be supplied at order. Rear zippered panels let the bed be reached without removing the whole cover. Pickup covers are also heavier — a full-size truck cover can weigh 8 to 12 pounds — so grab handles make solo fitting practical.
