# Boat Cover for Fishing Kayaks — Protection for Paddle and Pedal Rigs

Fishing kayaks are expensive, feature-loaded boats — with rod holders, transducer mounts, pedal drives, and electronics — and they're usually stored outside, exposed to the sun that degrades their plastic hulls. A fishing kayak cover is the cheapest insurance you can buy for your rig. Here is the complete guide.

## Why Fishing Kayaks Need Covers

- **UV damage** — kayak plastic (HDPE) degrades and becomes brittle after years of sun exposure
- **Color fading** — UV bleaches hull colors permanently
- **Debris** — leaves, dirt, and bird droppings collect in the cockpit
- **Moisture** — standing water in the cockpit breeds mildew in seat foam
- **Rod holder openings** — debris enters through scupper holes and holders

## Types of Kayak Covers

### 1. Full Kayak Cover

- Covers the entire hull, deck, and cockpit
- Elastic hem fits around the kayak perimeter
- Best for long-term outdoor storage

### 2. Cockpit Cover Only

- Covers just the cockpit opening
- Keeps debris out of the seat area
- Lighter, cheaper, good for frequent use

### 3. Tarp-Style Protection

- Not a fitted cover — use with a frame to avoid water pooling
- Only for short-term protection

## What to Look For

- **UV-resistant fabric** — the cover's job is blocking UV; the fabric must be UV-stable itself
- **Water-repellent coating** — sheds rain while staying breathable
- **Elastic hem with straps** — holds the cover in wind
- **Paddle-holder clearance** — covers must fit around deck-mounted accessories
- **Light color** — reflects heat, prevents the cockpit becoming an oven

## Fit Considerations for Fishing Kayaks

- Measure length and width at the widest point (often the cockpit or bow)
- Account for protruding accessories: rod holders, transducer mounts, paddle holders, rudder
- Some covers have adjustable straps to cinch around accessory clearance
- If you leave rods rigged, consider a longer cover or remove the rods for storage

## Storage Setup

| Storage | Best Cover |
|---------|------------|
| Outside year-round | Full cover, UV-resistant, heavy fabric |
| Garage | Full cover or cockpit cover |
| On J-cradles/roof rack | Full cover with straps |
| In a shed | Cockpit cover or full cover |

### Positioning

- Store the kayak off the ground on racks or cradles — never flat on concrete
- Support it at the bulkheads, not the middle of the hull
- Cover with the cockpit facing down if using a cockpit cover, or fully covered

## Drainage and Ventilation

Fishing kayaks have scupper holes that let water drain — make sure the cover doesn't block drainage:

- Full covers should still allow airflow at the scuppers
- Never store a kayak with standing water under the cover
- In humid climates, prop the cover up slightly for airflow

## Maintenance

- Wash the cover with mild soap; rinse kayak hulls before covering
- Re-apply DWR treatment seasonally
- Inspect straps and elastic annually
- Check under the cover monthly in damp climates
