# Boat Cover Winter Storage — Preparing Your Cover for the Off-Season

The boat cover you stored in fall is the same cover that must protect your boat all winter — and it's usually stored wrong. A cover folded damp, compressed, or in a hot attic comes out in spring with mildew, creases, and broken seams. Boat cover winter storage done right doubles the cover's life. Here is the guide.

## Why Winter Is Hard on Covers

- **Snow load** — weight stresses seams and fabric
- **Ice** — frozen fabric cracks when handled
- **Freeze-thaw cycles** — trapped moisture expands and damages coatings
- **UV at altitude** — winter sun is intense even when cold
- **Wind** — winter storms stress straps and hems

## Before Storage: The Cover's Own Winter Prep

### 1. Clean Thoroughly

- Wash with mild boat soap and a soft brush
- Remove all salt, dirt, and bird droppings — they hold moisture
- Rinse completely — soap residue attracts dirt and mildew

### 2. Dry Completely

- Air dry in the shade — sun can bake in residue
- A damp cover in storage is a guaranteed mildew farm
- Dry both sides; check pockets and folds

### 3. Repair Before Storing

- Patch small tears — they grow under snow load
- Reinforce loose seams
- Replace worn elastic
- Repair now, not in spring when you need the cover

### 4. Re-Treat Water Repellency

- Apply DWR treatment to a clean, dry cover
- This restores water beading for winter rain and snow melt
- Do it before storage — treatment needs to cure

## Installing the Cover for Winter

1. Winterize the boat completely first
2. Install support poles — snow load is the #1 cover killer
3. Fit and secure the cover with all straps
4. Check drainage — water and snow must slide off
5. Position the boat so the cover slopes toward drainage

## The Monthly Winter Check

| Interval | Action |
|----------|--------|
| After each snowfall | Brush snow off gently — never with a shovel |
| Monthly | Check straps, poles, and hem tension |
| After wind storms | Inspect for tears and flapping damage |
| In thaws | Check for water pooling and ice buildup |

## Spring Removal and Cover Storage

### Removal

- Remove snow before uncovering — dragging snow across the hull scratches it
- Uncover on a dry day
- Let the boat air out before covering again

### Off-Season Cover Storage

The cover's off-season (summer) is when most covers get ruined:

- Wash and dry completely
- Fold loosely — tight creases weaken fabric
- Store in a breathable storage bag, not airtight plastic
- Keep in a cool, dry place — attics and damp basements damage covers
- Hang if possible — hanging preserves shape better than folding

## Common Winter Cover Mistakes

1. Storing the cover damp after spring removal
2. Skipping support poles — snow pools and collapses covers
3. Using a light cover for a heavy snow region
4. Tightening straps before freeze — contraction snaps them
5. Ignoring small tears until they're big ones

## When to Replace

- Fabric is thin or translucent in spots
- Water soaks through immediately
- Seams are splitting under light tension
- Mildew has penetrated the fibers
- Elastic is permanently stretched
