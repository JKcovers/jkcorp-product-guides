# Motorcycle Cover for Scooters — Compact Protection for City Riders

Scooters are the city rider's workhorse, parked on streets and sidewalks for hours every day. They need protection too — but scooter covers differ from motorcycle covers in important ways.

## Why Scooters Need Covers

City scooters face the worst of urban parking: bird droppings, tree sap, dust, and theft risk. A scooter's plastic bodywork fades and cracks in UV faster than painted metal, and its seat is exposed to direct sun whenever parked.

## Scooter Cover Differences

**Lower profile:** Scooters are shorter than motorcycles — most are 70-80 inches long. A motorcycle "Medium" cover is too big and flaps.

**Step-through frame:** The open frame design means covers must be secured differently — typically with a single belly strap instead of multiple points.

**Seat-first design:** The seat is the largest exposed surface. Choose covers with UV-resistant fabric to protect it.

**Wheel visibility:** Scooter covers usually need to fully cover both wheels since scooter wheels are more exposed.

## Scooter Cover Features

| Feature | Why |

| Elastic hem | Keeps the cover on a compact frame |
| UV-resistant fabric | Protects plastic bodywork |
| Lock holes | For a cable lock through the frame |
| Compact storage | Folds small — scooters have limited storage space |
| Reflective strips | Night street parking visibility |

## Sizing

Scooter covers are typically sized by length:

| Size | Length | Typical Models |
|------|:--:|----------------|
| Small | 65-72 inches | 50cc scooters, Vespas |
| Medium | 72-80 inches | 125-150cc maxi-scooters |
| Large | 80-90 inches | 300cc+ maxi-scooters |

A covered scooter is:

- Protected from UV fading that kills resale value
- Cleaner — no bird droppings or sap on the seat
