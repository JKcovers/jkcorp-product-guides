# Motorcycle Cover FAQ — 20 Answers Before You Buy

A motorcycle cover is a small purchase that decides how your bike looks after a season outdoors. But sizing, materials, and storage rules confuse many riders. This motorcycle cover FAQ answers the 20 most common questions in one place.

## The Top 20 Questions

**1. Does my motorcycle need a cover?**
Yes if it sits outdoors or in a shared garage — UV fades paint, rain corrodes chrome, dust scratches tank paint.

**2. Indoor or outdoor cover?**
Outdoor covers need waterproofing and UV resistance; indoor covers just block dust.

**3. What fabric should I choose?**
Outdoor: 300D-600D polyester or Oxford with water-resistant coating. Indoor: soft polyester or cotton-blend dust covers. Heated garages need breathable fabric.

**4. How do I measure my bike for a cover?**
Measure overall length, height (including mirrors), and width at the handlebars. Match against the cover size chart — sizes range from scooters to touring bikes.

**5. Will a cover scratch the paint?**
Only with a dirty bike or rough lining. Cover a clean bike, and choose a soft inner lining.

**6. Is the exhaust a problem?**
Yes. Wait for the exhaust to cool completely before covering — heat melts fabric and traps moisture.

**7. Waterproof or breathable?**
Both matter. A fully sealed cover traps condensation on the tank; choose water-resistant fabric with vents.

**8. How do I keep the cover from blowing off?**
Use covers with elastic hems, under-belly straps, or a lockable cable loop. Park with the bike against a wall for extra wind protection.

**9. Can I ride with the cover on?**
No. Covers are for stationary bikes — riding with one is dangerous and destroys the fabric.

**10. How long does a motorcycle cover last?**
2-4 years typically. UV and wind stress wear covers fastest; quality fabric and proper care extend life.

**11. Can I wash the cover?**
Yes — machine wash cold, gentle cycle, air dry. No fabric softener, no dryer heat.

**12. Does a cover prevent theft?**
No. Some covers have lock loops, but they only slow a thief, not stop one.

**13. Is it safe to cover a bike with a hot engine?**
No. Let the engine and exhaust cool first, or condensation forms under the cover.

**14. What about covering in winter storage?**
Use a breathable cover for long-term storage. Add a layer of protection against rodents — they nest under covers in cold months.

**15. Do I need to cover the windshield?**
Full-coverage covers include the windshield area. For tall windshields, check the cover's height rating.

**16. Can one cover fit multiple bikes?**
A universal cover fits different models loosely. For a good fit, buy a cover sized to your largest bike.

**17. What is a "half cover"?**
Half covers protect the seat, tank, and handlebars only — good for quick protection, but they leave the wheels and lower frame exposed.

**18. How do I store the cover?**
Dry it fully, fold loosely, and keep it in the included bag. Never store a damp cover — mildew ruins fabric fast.

**19. Do covers work for e-bikes and scooters?**
Yes, with the right size. E-bikes and scooters use the same cover types at smaller sizes.

**20. Is a cover worth it?**
For outdoor parking, absolutely. One cover protects more value than almost any other $30-60 purchase.

## Final Advice

Measure your bike, choose fabric by where you park, and always cover a cool, dry motorcycle. The right cover makes a bike look new for years.
