# Supercar Cover Guide — Protection for Six-Figure Paint

Supercars demand a different class of cover. The paint alone can cost more than a new sedan, the bodywork is low and wide, and the materials — carbon fiber, ceramic brakes, PPF-wrapped panels — react badly to the wrong fabric. This guide covers what a supercar cover must be.

## The Supercar Cover Requirements

**Ultra-soft inner lining.** Carbon fiber and ceramic-coated paint scratch easily. A 100% cotton or microfleece lining is mandatory; rough polyester is disqualifying.

**Tailored fit.** Supercar silhouettes are extreme: low noses, wide rear haunches, big wings. Generic covers bag over the shape, flutter in wind, and chafe. A vehicle-specific pattern is the only correct fit.

**PPF-safe.** Many supercars wear paint protection film. The cover must not grip, lift, or bubble film edges — soft linings and non-sticky inner surfaces are essential.

**Breathable, not just waterproof.** A non-breathable cover traps condensation against carbon and aluminum body panels, causing micro-corrosion under clear coat edges.

## Indoor vs Outdoor Supercar Storage

| Storage | Cover Type |
|---------|-----------|
| Heated garage | Thin breathable cotton or flannel dust cover |
| Unheated garage | Fleece-lined cover, breathable |
| Outdoor (avoid if possible) | Multi-layer waterproof, soft-lined, secured |

The ideal supercar home is a climate-controlled garage with a cotton cover. Outdoor storage is a compromise that requires a premium multi-layer cover.

## Handling a Supercar Cover

- Wash hands or wear gloves before handling — skin oils mark matte finishes
- Remove the cover by rolling, never dragging
- Store it in its bag, away from sunlight
- Inspect monthly for embedded grit — one trapped stone = one scratch

## The Price Logic

A quality supercar cover costs less than one paint correction session. For six-figure cars, this is not an accessory — it is insurance.

## Common Supercar Questions

**Can I use a cover with a car cover alarm?** Yes — many premium covers integrate or work alongside motion alarms; just ensure the fabric is not too thick for sensor sensitivity.

**Do covers damage ceramic-coated supercars?** No, if the cover is clean and soft-lined — see the ceramic coating guide for details.

**How often should I wash the cover?** Every 1-2 months for garage covers; more often if used outdoors. Grit is the enemy, not the fabric.

## Key Takeaways

- Soft lining, tailored fit, PPF-safe, breathable — the four supercar requirements
- Garage storage beats any outdoor cover arrangement
- Handle covers like you handle the car: clean hands, no dragging
