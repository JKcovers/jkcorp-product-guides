# Luggage and Trunk Covers — Protect Cargo, Cargo Area, and Privacy

Luggage covers serve three purposes: protect the cargo from weather, protect the vehicle's cargo area from scratches and dirt, and hide what's in your trunk from prying eyes. Here's what to know.

## The Three Types

**Roof luggage cover:** Protects luggage strapped to a roof rack from rain, road grime, and wind debris. Waterproof with elastic hems for a snug fit over bags and boxes.

**Trunk/cargo area liner:** A protective pad or liner that lines the cargo area — protecting it from pets, cargo, and spills. Waterproof backing is essential.

**Privacy cargo cover:** The retractable or fixed cover that hides trunk contents. OEM covers are factory-fit; universal versions use tension rods or brackets.

## Why Cover Roof Luggage

Roof cargo faces the most brutal conditions on any trip: highway wind, rain at 70 mph, UV exposure, and airborne debris. An unsecured or uncovered load can shift, degrade, or damage the vehicle's paint.

## Key Features for Roof Covers

- **Waterproof with reinforced seams** — rain at highway speed finds every seam
- **Adjustable elastic hem** — fits irregular shapes
- **Anti-flap design** — straps that cinch the cover to the load
- **UV resistance** — prevents fabric and cargo degradation
