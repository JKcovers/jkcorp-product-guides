# Windshield Protectors and Covers — Ice, Sun, and Hail Defense

Windshields face frost, ice scrapers, UV, and hail — and windshield replacement is expensive. Windshield protectors are cheap insurance with several useful forms.

## The Types of Windshield Protection

**Frost/ice cover (winter):** A fitted cover that shields the windshield from frost and ice overnight. Remove in the morning and drive off with a clear windshield — no scraping, no scratched glass.

**Sunshade (summer):** A reflective shield placed INSIDE against the glass, blocking UV and reducing interior heat.

**Hail protector (severe weather):** A padded cover designed to absorb hail impacts on the glass.

## Frost Covers — The Winter Essential

Winter drivers waste 10-20 minutes scraping ice. A frost cover eliminates it:

- Fitted elastic covers (stay in place overnight)
- Magnetic covers (attach to the roof edge)
- Features: soft inner lining (won't scratch glass), UV-resistant outer

**How to use:** Fit the cover dry the night before; if snow accumulates, brush it off; remove and store (shaking off ice) in the morning.

## The Scratch Danger

Ice scrapers leave micro-scratches that accumulate into visibility problems over years. Frost covers eliminate scraping entirely — protecting both your time and your glass.

## Sunshades and Heat

A reflective sunshade reduces interior temperature 15-25°F and blocks 99%+ UV — protecting the dashboard and preserving the windshield's own UV degradation.

## Hail Protectors

Padded windshield covers absorb small-hail impacts. For severe hail warnings, they're the best portable protection available.
