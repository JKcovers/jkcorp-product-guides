# Car Cover Warranty Guide — What's Covered and What's Not

Car cover warranties vary wildly — from "no warranty" to 5-year coverage. Understanding what manufacturers actually cover (and don't) helps you buy smarter and file claims correctly.

## What Warranties Typically Cover

- **Manufacturing defects:** Seams splitting, stitching failure, zipper breakage
- **Material defects:** Fabric that delaminates or tears along weave lines without cause
- **Hardware failure:** Broken buckles, straps, or elastic that fails prematurely

## What Warranties Do NOT Cover

- **UV degradation:** Normal fabric aging from sun exposure — even with a "UV warranty," slow fading is excluded
- **Improper use:** Machine drying, bleach, washing with fabric softener
- **Physical damage:** Tears from wind flap, animal damage, dragging on pavement
- **Normal wear:** Elastic losing stretch, coating wear over time
- **Mildew:** (result of improper storage/use)

## Warranty Terms by Quality Tier

| Cover Tier | Typical Warranty | What It Means |
|------------|:--:|------|
| Budget (PEVA) | 30-90 days | Defect coverage only |
| Mid-range | 1-2 years | Defects + some material failure |
| Premium | 3-5 years | Defects + material longevity |

## The Fine Print to Read

1. **"Warranty" vs "Guarantee":** A guarantee often means full refund; a warranty means repair/replacement only
2. **Prorated vs full:** Some warranties prorate the refund by usage time
3. **Proof of purchase:** Always keep your receipt
4. **OEM vs retailer:** The warranty runs through the seller, not the factory, unless specified

## How to File a Claim

1. Photograph the defect clearly
2. Keep your receipt and packaging
3. Contact the seller within the warranty window
4. Describe the failure mode (seam split, delamination, etc.)
5. Be ready to return the cover (shipping may be on you)

## The Bottom Line

A warranty is a quality signal, not protection from the elements. Buy from a manufacturer that stands behind defects — but assume the cover will need replacement in its rated lifespan regardless.
