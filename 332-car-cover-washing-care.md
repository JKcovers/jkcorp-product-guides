# Car Cover Washing and Care: Keep Your Cover Working

Car cover washing and care is the most skipped maintenance task among owners, and the most consequential. A dirty cover grinds grit into the paint it is supposed to protect, and a neglected waterproof coating lets water soak through. A clean, treated cover lasts 3-5 years instead of 1-2.

## When to Wash

- Every 2-3 months in normal use
- Monthly during dusty or pollen-heavy seasons
- Immediately after bird droppings, tree sap, or mud exposure
- Before long-term storage

## How to Wash a Car Cover

1. **Shake it out** to remove loose dust and debris
2. **Pre-soak** in cold water with a mild detergent for 15 minutes
3. **Hand wash or machine wash** on gentle, cold cycle only
4. **Do not use** bleach, fabric softener, or high-heat drying
5. **Air dry** completely; never store a damp cover

Check the care label first: coated covers (PU, PVC-backed) often need hand washing only, while uncoated polyester can tolerate a gentle machine cycle.

## Restoring Waterproofing

Washing strips water-repellent treatments over time. When water stops beading, re-treat:

- Use a spray-on fabric waterproofing product designed for outdoor textiles
- Apply to a clean, dry cover in a ventilated area
- Focus on the top panels and seams
- Allow 24 hours to cure before outdoor use

## Drying and Storage

- Hang the cover over a clean surface or use a drying rack; avoid direct scorching sun
- Fold into its storage bag only when fully dry
- Store in a cool, dry place; heat degrades coatings
- For long-term storage, add a moisture absorber in humid climates

## Common Care Mistakes

| Mistake | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Machine washing on hot | Shrinkage and coating damage |
| Using bleach | Fabric weakening and fading |
| Storing damp | Mildew growth and odor |
| Scrubbing stains hard | Abrasion and seam stress |
| Skipping re-treatment | Waterlogging and paint contact |

## Quick Takeaways

- Wash every 2-3 months, or immediately after sap, droppings, or mud exposure
- Gentle cycle, cold water, mild detergent: never bleach or fabric softener
- Re-treat waterproofing when water stops beading, roughly once a year
- Dry completely before folding, or the next use will smell and may mildew

## When to Replace

Replace the cover when the coating peels, water soaks through after re-treatment, or seams split. A failing cover holds moisture against the paint, which is worse than no cover.
