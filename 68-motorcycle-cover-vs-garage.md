# Motorcycle Cover vs Garage Storage — Which Protects Your Bike Better?

The age-old question: is a cover enough, or do you need a garage? The answer depends on your climate, storage duration, and budget. Here's an honest comparison.

## Garage Storage: The Gold Standard

A garage protects from rain, sun, theft, and temperature swings. The bike stays dry, clean, and warm — ideal for long-term storage and daily commuting.

**But:** not everyone has one. Apartment dwellers, city riders, and multi-bike households often can't fit everything inside. A garage also doesn't protect against dust accumulation, falling objects, or condensation in unheated garages.

## Outdoor Cover: The Practical Compromise

A quality multi-layer outdoor cover handles the big three — rain, UV, and dust — for $20-50. That's 5-10% of the cost of an extra garage bay.

**The trade-off:** the bike stays cold in winter, the cover needs periodic replacement (2-3 years), and a cover alone won't stop a determined thief.

## The Optimal Setup (Most Riders)

1. Store the bike in the garage when possible
2. Use an indoor cover even in the garage — it prevents dust, scratches, and condensation
3. If the bike must sit outside, use a waterproof outdoor cover with a belly strap and lock holes
