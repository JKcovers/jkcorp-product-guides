# Waterproof Luggage Cover — Protect Your Bags From the Elements

Checked luggage travels through rain, snow, and baggage-handling abuse. A waterproof luggage cover wraps your bag in a protective shell that keeps contents dry and the bag itself clean. This guide covers types, sizing, and durability.

## Why Use a Luggage Cover

- **Rain and snow** — wet baggage carts and open tarmac transfers soak soft-sided bags
- **Scuffs and scratches** — covers absorb the abrasion that destroys bag corners
- **Dirt and grime** — a cover keeps the bag clean between trips
- **Theft deterrence** — a cover hides brand labels and makes the bag less recognizable

## Types of Luggage Covers

| Type | Protection | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Stretch-fit spandex cover | Scuffs + light rain | Frequent flyers |
| Coated nylon cover | Rain + abrasion | Winter travel |
| Neoprene cover | Impact + water | Fragile or valuable bags |
| Anti-theft cover with lock | All of the above + tamper alarm | High-risk routes |

## Sizing

Luggage covers are sized by bag capacity (20, 24, 28 inches). Measure your bag's height, width, and depth — soft-sided bags that bulge need the next size up. A cover that is too tight strains the seams; one too loose catches in baggage machinery.

## Care and Durability

- Wipe the cover clean after each trip — dirt abrades the coating
- Reapply a water-repellent spray once a season for coated fabrics
- Check zippers and seams before long trips; replace the cover when the coating wears through
- Let a wet cover dry fully before folding it for storage

## Common Questions

**Will a cover fit over a fully packed bag?** Packed bags bulge. If your bag is packed to capacity, go one size up — a stretched cover strains at the seams and may tear on the belt.

**Do covers set off airport scanners?** No — luggage covers do not interfere with checked-bag screening, and you can leave them on at check-in.

**Can I wash a luggage cover?** Yes — hand wash in cold water with mild soap and air dry. Avoid machine washing, which breaks down the elastic and coating.

**Are covers worth it for carry-on bags?** Mostly for rain and scuffs on the overhead journey. For checked bags, covers are essential; for carry-ons, they are optional.

## The Golden Rule

**Size to the loaded bag, and re-treat the coating.** A waterproof luggage cover is only as waterproof as its coating maintenance.
