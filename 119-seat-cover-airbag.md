# Seat Cover Airbag Safety — The Feature You Must Never Compromise

Seat covers and airbags have a critical relationship: the wrong cover can prevent your side airbag from saving your life. This is the most important safety consideration in seat cover selection.

## How Side Airbags Work

Side airbags deploy from the seat's side bolster at speeds up to 200 mph. The seat cover sits between the airbag and your body. A cover without proper cutouts can:

- **Block deployment:** The airbag inflates against the cover instead of outward
- **Turn the airbag into a projectile:** A loose cover becomes a fabric missile
- **Rip unpredictably:** Inconsistent material behavior in a crash

## The Safe Cover Checklist

1. **Airbag cutouts:** The cover must have pre-cut, seam-sealed openings aligned with the airbag location
2. **Factory-style seams:** The cutout area should use breakaway stitching like the original seat
3. **Not too thick:** Avoid covers with thick padding over the airbag zone
4. **Proper fit:** A loose cover shifts over time and misaligns the cutout

## How to Verify

- Look for explicit "airbag compatible" labeling
- Check the cutout position matches your vehicle's airbag location (usually in the seat side bolster, marked "AIRBAG")
- Ask the manufacturer for the airbag test specification
- Never trim or modify the cover yourself — it will void safety

## Vehicles Without Side Airbags

Older vehicles without side airbags don't need cutouts — but verify your vehicle's airbag layout first. Front airbags don't involve seat covers.
