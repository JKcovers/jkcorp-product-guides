# Motorcycle Helmet Covers — Why Every Rider Needs One

A helmet is your most important — and most expensive — piece of riding gear. A dedicated helmet cover protects it from the elements, the sun, and prying eyes when you're off the bike.

## What Damages Helmets

**UV radiation:** Degrades the shell's protective resin. A helmet left on a bike's mirror in the sun for a season loses structural integrity faster than years of riding.

**Rain and moisture:** Seeps into vents and padding, promoting mildew and odor.

**Scratches:** Helmet shells are hard but not scratch-proof. Banging against a motorcycle cover buckle or a garage shelf leaves permanent marks.

**Theft:** An uncovered helmet on a parked bike is a target. A covered helmet is anonymous.

## Helmet Cover Types

**Rain cover:** Waterproof, packs to palm size, slips over the helmet on the bike or in the saddlebag. Essential for riders in wet climates.

**Storage bag (indoors):** Padded soft bag for carrying and protecting the helmet when off the bike.

**Locking cover:** Includes a loop or clip for a cable lock — secures the helmet to the bike while covering it.

## What to Look For

- **Reflective elements:** The helmet is already the most visible part of a rider; a reflective cover adds visibility when the helmet is off the bike.
- **Breathable mesh:** Prevents condensation inside the cover.
- **Durable material:** Oxford or PVC-coated fabric resists the same weather as your bike cover.
