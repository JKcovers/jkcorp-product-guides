# SUV and Sedan Trunk Covers

An SUV cargo area and a sedan trunk are different spaces, and a cover that works in one is usually wrong for the other. The SUV is a tall, open box with fold-down seat backs; the sedan is a deep well with a narrow opening and a floor that lifts.

Specifying a trunk cover means starting with which of the two it is for.

## The SUV cargo area

An SUV cargo area has characteristics that shape the product:

- **A large, flat floor** with a low load lip at the tailgate.
- **Fold-down seat backs**, which are usually carpeted and are part of the load space when folded.
- **Wheel arch intrusions** on both sides, narrowing the floor.
- **Tie-down points and rails**, often recessed.
- **Under-floor storage**, sometimes with a removable floor panel.
- **Side trim and a tailgate sill**, both easily scratched.

The seat backs are the part most often missed. When the seats are folded, cargo and dogs rest against them, so a cover that stops at the floor leaves the largest surface unprotected.

This is why SUV products split into two types: a **floor mat**, and a **full boot liner** that covers the floor, the seat backs and often the side walls.

## The sedan trunk

A sedan trunk is a different problem:

- **A deep well** with a high lip at the opening, so cargo is lifted over the sill.
- **A narrow opening** relative to the floor area, which limits what can be loaded.
- **A floor panel that lifts**, covering the spare wheel or battery.
- **A sill and bumper edge** that take the abrasion during loading.
- **Hinged lid arms** that intrude into the space when closed.

Here the priority is containment and loading protection rather than covering seat backs. A moulded tray with a raised lip is the usual answer, and it must allow the floor panel to be lifted without removing the mat.

## Coverage options

| Option | Best for |
|---|---|
| Flat floor mat | Light cargo, appearance |
| Moulded tray with lip | Spills, wet gear, tools |
| Full boot liner with seat-back panel | Dogs, bulky cargo, folded seats |
| Reversible quilted liner | Appearance with light use |
| Bumper or sill protector | Loading abrasion, both body styles |

For SUVs used with dogs, the seat-back panel matters as much as the floor, because a dog stands against it and claws at it on every journey.

## Fit points that matter

**SUV:** wheel arch width, seat-back angle, tailgate sill height, tie-down positions, under-floor hatch.

**Sedan:** trunk floor length and width, the position of the floor panel hinge, the height of the load sill, and clearance for the lid arms.

A mat that covers a tie-down or blocks a floor hatch will be cut or removed by the owner, which defeats the purpose.

## Materials

- **TPE or rubber** for utility, wet cargo and pets.
- **Carpet** where appearance matters and cargo is dry.
- **Quilted leatherette** for a premium look, with the seam caveat.
- **Foam-backed fabric** for light cushioning.

## What to ask a supplier

- whether the product is a floor mat or a full liner
- for SUVs, whether the seat backs are covered
- whether under-floor storage and tie-downs remain accessible
- how the mat is secured, since a loose liner slides
- material and odour behaviour in heat
- the vehicle coverage list
