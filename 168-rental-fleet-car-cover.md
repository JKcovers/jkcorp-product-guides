# Car Cover for Rental and Fleet Vehicles — The Untapped Protection

Rental fleets and company vehicles face unique protection challenges: high turnover, careless drivers, and constant outdoor parking. Yet most fleet managers never consider covers. Here's the case for changing that.

## The Fleet Cover Problem

Fleet vehicles depreciate faster than private cars because:
- Multiple drivers, no single owner's care
- Constant outdoor parking
- Higher mileage and abuse
- Resale happens sooner (2-4 years)

Paint damage is the most visible form of fleet depreciation — and the most preventable.

## Where Fleet Covers Pay Off

| Scenario | Without Cover | With Cover |
|----------|:--:|:--:|
| Snow belt fleet | Salt corrosion, paint damage | Protected |
| Sun belt fleet | UV fade, dash cracking | Protected |
| Airport/transit lots | Bird droppings, tree sap | Protected |
| Branded vehicles | Faded logos | Logos preserved |

## Fleet-Specific Cover Features

**Custom branding:** Fleet covers with your logo turn parked vehicles into mobile billboards — and make theft harder (covered cars are anonymous).

**Quick-install design:** If drivers won't use them, they don't help. One-person installation under 2 minutes.

**Durability:** Fleet covers get used daily — 600D Oxford or heavier.

**Durable straps:** Multiple anchor points for windy lots.

## The ROI Calculation

A $60-80 fleet cover protecting a $25,000 vehicle's paint and interior:
- Extends paint life 2-3 years
- Improves resale by $500-1,500 per vehicle
- Reduces detailing costs
- A 10x+ return on the cover investment
