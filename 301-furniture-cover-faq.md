# Furniture Cover FAQ — 20 Answers for Outdoor Furniture Owners

Outdoor furniture takes a beating from sun, rain, wind, and frost — and the right cover doubles its life. But furniture covers have their own sizing and material rules. This furniture cover FAQ answers the 20 questions buyers ask most.

## The Top 20 Questions

**1. Does outdoor furniture really need covers?**
Yes if it stays outside. Covers protect cushions from sun fading, rain rot, and bird droppings, and keep frames free of dirt and sap.

**2. Indoor or outdoor cover?**
Outdoor covers need water resistance, UV protection, and wind anchoring. Indoor covers just stop dust.

**3. What fabric is best for furniture covers?**
Polyester with a waterproof coating (often 600D Oxford) is the workhorse; premium buyers choose breathable, vented polyester.

**4. How do I measure furniture for a cover?**
Measure width, depth, and height at the widest points. Buy a cover slightly larger than the furniture — it must fit over armrests and backrests comfortably.

**5. Do covers fit chairs and sofas differently?**
Yes. Sofa covers are rectangular; chair covers are shorter and narrower. Many sets sell separate covers per piece.

**6. Waterproof or breathable?**
Both matter. Fully sealed covers trap condensation under cushions and cause mildew. Look for water-resistant fabric with vents.

**7. Can I cover wet furniture?**
No. Dry cushions and frames first — trapped moisture is the fastest way to ruin both.

**8. How do I keep covers from blowing off?**
Use covers with elastic hems, drawstrings, or built-in straps. For open areas, add bungee cords through the grommets.

**9. Do I need to remove cushions before covering?**
Ideally no — good covers fit over cushions. But if cushions stay damp, store them indoors or cover them separately.

**10. How long do furniture covers last?**
2-4 years outdoors. UV and wind shorten life; quality fabric and care extend it.

**11. Can I wash furniture covers?**
Yes — most are machine washable on cold, gentle. Air dry, no dryer heat.

**12. What about covers for umbrellas and tables?**
Different shapes: umbrella covers are tall and narrow; table covers are flat and wide. Buy per piece, not one-size-fits-all.

**13. Will a cover prevent rust on metal frames?**
It helps by keeping rain off, but covers do not stop existing rust. Treat rust before covering.

**14. Do covers protect wood furniture?**
Yes, especially teak and acacia — they shield wood from rain and UV. Use a breathable cover so trapped moisture does not warp the wood.

**15. What is the best cover for cushions alone?**
Cushion-specific covers fit snugly and protect fabric year-round. They are easier to remove than full furniture covers.

**16. Can covers be used indoors for storage?**
Yes — a lightweight dust cover works well for stored seasonal furniture in garages.

**17. How do I stop covers from fading?**
Buy UV-stabilized fabric. All covers fade eventually; quality fabric fades slowly.

**18. Do I need to cover furniture in winter?**
Yes in cold climates. Snow load and frost damage frames and cushions; a winter-rated cover handles both.

**19. How do I store covers between seasons?**
Clean, dry, and fold into the storage bag. Never store damp.

**20. Is it worth buying premium covers?**
For year-round outdoor furniture, yes. Premium fabric lasts twice as long as budget covers.

## Final Advice

Measure each piece, buy by shape and climate, use elastic or strap anchoring, and cover dry furniture. The right covers keep a patio set looking new for years.
