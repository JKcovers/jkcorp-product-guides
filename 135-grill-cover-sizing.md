# Grill Cover Sizing — Get the Right Fit for Your BBQ

A grill cover that doesn't fit is worse than no cover: too small leaves the base exposed to rain, too large flaps in the wind and collects water. Correct sizing takes two minutes.

## The Measurements

Measure your grill with side shelves DOWN (or extended, whichever is the wider configuration):

1. **Width:** Side-to-side at the widest point
2. **Depth:** Front-to-back at the deepest point
3. **Height:** Ground to the top of the closed lid

## The Fit Formula

Add 2-4 inches to each dimension for a proper fit:
- Enough to slide over easily
- Not so much that it billows in wind
- Full base coverage (the bottom of the grill is the first to rust)

## Common Grill Types and Sizes

| Grill Type | Typical Width | Typical Height | Cover Size |
|------------|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Portable/kettle | 18-22 in | 30-40 in | Small |
| Two-burner | 35-45 in | 40-45 in | Medium |
| Three-burner | 45-55 in | 45-50 in | Large |
| Four-burner + side burner | 55-65 in | 45-50 in | X-Large |
| Flat top griddle | 30-50 in | 30-40 in | Medium-Large |
| Pellet grill | 30-55 in | 45-55 in | Large-XL |

## Special Cases

- **With side burners:** Measure the full extended width
- **With smoker box:** Add depth
- **Island grills:** Custom-sized covers needed
- **Two grills side by side:** Consider one oversized cover

## The Drawstring Detail

A cover with an adjustable drawstring hem adapts to minor size differences and keeps the cover snug in wind. Look for this feature — it makes size mistakes more forgiving.
