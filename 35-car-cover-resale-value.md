# How Car Covers Protect Resale Value — The Numbers Don't Lie

Selling a car with faded paint costs you thousands. A car cover is one of the cheapest ways to preserve resale value. Here are the numbers.

## The Cost of Unprotected Parking

A car parked outdoors for 3-5 years without a cover typically needs paint correction ($500-2,000) or a full respray ($3,000-10,000) before sale. The car will sell for 10-15% less than a comparable vehicle with good paint — on a $25,000 car, that's $2,500-3,750.

## The Cost of Protection

A quality multi-layer outdoor car cover costs $50-150 and lasts 2-3 years. Even replacing it twice over 5 years costs $100-300 total — less than 10% of the cost of one paint correction.

## Other Resale Factors

- **Dashboard condition:** A cracked dashboard drops perceived value. UV-blocking covers prevent this.
- **Headlight clarity:** Yellowed headlights cost $150-300 to restore. Covers prevent UV yellowing.
- **Interior smell:** Mildew from trapped moisture creates odors that buyers notice immediately.

## The Bottom Line

A $100 cover that prevents $3,000 in paint and interior damage is a 30x return on investment. If you plan to sell your car within 5 years, a cover pays for itself many times over.
