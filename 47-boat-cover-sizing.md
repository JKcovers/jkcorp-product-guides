# Boat Cover Sizing Guide — Protect Your Investment in Any Water

Boat covers are the trickiest protective covers to size correctly. Windshields, T-tops, towers, outboard motors, and trailer configurations all affect fit. Here's how to get it right.

## Step 1: Measure Your Boat

For a trailerable cover, measure:
- **Centerline length:** Bow to stern along the center (not including swim platform)
- **Beam width:** Widest point of the hull
- **Height:** Gunwale to top of windshield or T-top

## Step 2: Type of Cover

- **Mooring covers:** Snaps directly to the boat, stays on in water. Custom-fit to your exact hull.
- **Trailerable covers:** Designed for highway speeds. Heavier fabric (600D+) with reinforced tie-down points.
- **Storage covers:** Lightweight, for indoor or covered dock storage. Breathable fabric prevents mildew.

## Step 3: Account for Accessories

Add 6-12 inches to your measurements for: outboard motors, swim platforms, bow rails, T-tops and towers, downriggers and rod holders.

## Material Choices

Marine-grade polyester with solution-dyed UV treatment lasts 5-7 years outdoors. This is the standard for serious boat protection. Acrylic blends offer better breathability for high-humidity storage. PVC-coated polyester is the heavy-duty option for extreme weather.
