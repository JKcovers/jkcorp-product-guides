# Boat Cover vs Shrink Wrap — The 5-Year Cost Comparison

Boat winterization comes down to two main choices: shrink wrap (single-use plastic) or a custom fabric cover (reusable). The 5-year cost difference is dramatic — and most owners make the decision without seeing the full math.

## The Upfront Cost

| Method | Typical Cost |
|--------|:--:|
| Shrink wrap (professional install) | $200-500/year |
| Shrink wrap (DIY kit) | $100-250/year |
| Custom fabric cover | $300-800 one-time |

## The 5-Year Total Cost

| Year | Shrink Wrap (Pro) | Shrink Wrap (DIY) | Custom Cover |

**5-year savings with a custom cover: $1,250 (vs pro wrap) or $375 (vs DIY)**

## Beyond Cost

| Factor | Shrink Wrap | Custom Cover |
|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Breathability | ❌ (needs vents) | ✅ Fabric breathes |
| Reusability | ❌ Single-use | ✅ 5-7 years |
| Environmental impact | ❌ Landfill | ✅ Reusable |
| Pest protection | ✅ Full seal | ⚠️ Good |
| DIY ability | ⚠️ Hard | ✅ Easy |
| UV protection | ✅ | ✅ 95%+ |
| Storage space needed | ❌ Discarded | ✅ Compact |

## The Real Decider

**Choose shrink wrap if:**
- Storing for one season only
- Boat is in extreme weather (full seal matters)
- You have a professional installer available at good rates

**Choose a custom cover if:**
- Storing for 3+ seasons
- You want DIY control
- Environmental impact matters to you
- You'll be covering annually

## The Hybrid Option

Some owners wrap in winter (complete seal) and use a fabric cover the rest of the year. Maximum protection, but no cost advantage — you're paying for both.
