# Fleet Seat Covers — The Business Case for Protecting Your Vehicle Interiors

For businesses running vehicle fleets — taxis, delivery vans, ride-share, company cars — seat covers are a direct ROI play. Here's the math and the buying guide.

## The Cost of Unprotected Fleet Seats

A fleet vehicle with worn, stained, torn seats sells for 10-20% less at disposal. On a $20,000 vehicle that's $2,000-4,000 lost. Interior wear also damages brand perception — customers notice dirty seats immediately.

## The Fleet Cover ROI

| Scenario | Cost |
|----------|------|
| New seat upholstery (per vehicle) | $800-2,000 |
| Seat repair (per seat) | $150-400 |
| Fleet seat covers (per vehicle) | $40-120 |
| Replacement cycle | 2-4 years |

A $60 cover protecting a $1,000 seat for 3 years = 5.5x ROI, not counting resale value preservation.

## Fleet-Specific Requirements

- **Durability:** Heavy-duty Oxford or vinyl rated for daily professional use
- **Easy cleaning:** Wipe-clean surfaces save labor costs
- **Universal-to-fit options:** Semi-custom covers reduce SKU complexity
- **Custom branding:** Fleet covers with your logo on seats double as branding
- **Airbag compatibility:** Non-negotiable, verify every model

## Implementation

1. **Start with a pilot:** Cover 10 vehicles, track cleaning costs and wear for 3 months
2. **Standardize:** One cover model per vehicle class reduces inventory
3. **Train drivers:** Proper installation extends cover life
4. **Schedule replacement:** 2-3 year cycle, inspect quarterly
