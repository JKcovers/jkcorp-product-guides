# Boat Winterization Full Checklist — Engine, Systems, and Cover

Winterization is the difference between a boat that starts in spring and a boat that needs an engine rebuild. A boat winterization full checklist covers every system — engine, fuel, plumbing, electrical, and finally the cover. Skip any step and you risk expensive damage. Here is the complete sequence.

## Why Winterization Matters

- Water left in the engine block freezes and cracks it
- Untreated fuel degrades, gumming carburetors and injectors
- Condensation inside the tank and engine promotes corrosion
- Trapped moisture in the cabin grows mildew all winter
- An uncovered boat suffers UV and snow damage on top of all that

## The Full Winterization Checklist

### 1. Engine (Outboard or Inboard)

- Flush the engine with fresh water (outboards)
- Drain the block and manifolds completely (inboards)
- Fog the cylinders with fogging oil
- Change the engine oil and filter
- Replace the lower unit gear oil — check for milky color (water intrusion)
- Grease all fittings

### 2. Fuel System

- Fill the tank to prevent condensation
- Add fuel stabilizer and run the engine to circulate it
- Drain or stabilize carburetors
- Label the tank with the fuel treatment date

### 3. Plumbing and Fresh Water

- Drain all water tanks, lines, and pumps
- Blow out lines with compressed air
- Add non-toxic RV antifreeze to pumps and traps
- Drain the livewell and wash down systems

### 4. Electrical

- Remove batteries and store them indoors on a maintainer
- Clean and grease all terminals
- Disconnect and store the battery charger if it stays aboard
- Inspect wiring for corrosion

### 5. Interior and Trailer

- Remove cushions, life jackets, and soft goods — store them indoors
- Leave hatches slightly open for airflow, or install solar vents
- Clean the interior thoroughly to avoid attracting pests
- Grease trailer bearings; check tire pressure

## Choosing the Winter Cover

| Cover Type | Snow Load | Best For |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| Custom fitted cover with support poles | Good | Trailered boats |
| Heavy tarp with frame | Good with poles | Larger boats |
| Shrink wrap | Excellent | Outdoor storage yards |
| Light cover | Poor | Indoor storage only |

### Key Requirements

- **Waterproof** — snow melt must not reach the boat
- **Breathable** — trapped moisture damages everything inside
- **Support poles** — prevent snow pooling and cover collapse
- **Reinforced seams** — seams fail first under snow load

## The Final Sequence

1. Finish all mechanical winterization
2. Clean and dry the boat completely
3. Install the cover support poles
4. Fit and secure the cover, checking for sagging
5. Note the storage location and date for spring planning

## Monthly Winter Checks

- After each snowfall, brush snow off the cover
- Check straps and pole positions monthly
- Look for signs of animals getting under the cover
- In mild spells, air out the cabin if accessible
