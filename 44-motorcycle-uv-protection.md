# Motorcycle Cover UV Protection — Save Your Paint and Seat from Sun Damage

A motorcycle's paint, seat, and plastics are all vulnerable to UV radiation. A quality cover with UV protection is the cheapest insurance for your bike's appearance and resale value.

## Where UV Damage Hits Hardest

- **Fairings and tank paint:** Horizontal surfaces face direct sunlight and fade 3x faster than vertical panels.
- **Seat:** Vinyl and leather crack within 2-3 years of outdoor parking without UV protection.
- **Dashboard and instruments:** Digital displays degrade in direct sun; analog gauges yellow and become hard to read.
- **Tires:** UV dries out rubber compounds, causing sidewall cracking that can lead to blowouts.

## UV Protection by Cover Material

Oxford fabric with UV coating blocks 90-95% of UV. Silver or light-colored covers reflect more sunlight. Dark covers absorb heat and can melt against hot exhaust components. A 99% UV rejection rating is the gold standard — look for the specific percentage, not vague "UV resistant" claims.

## Signs Your Cover's UV Protection Is Failing

- Color fading on the cover itself (the outer layer is sun-bleached)
- Cover feels brittle or thin in spots
- Water no longer beads on the surface
- Bike still feels hot to touch under the cover on sunny days
