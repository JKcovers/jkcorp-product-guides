# Pet Seat Cover for Cats — Yes, Cats Ride in Cars Too

Cat owners face a different challenge: cats hate cars, scratch everything, and escape through any gap. A cat-specific approach to seat protection is needed.

## The Cat Problem

Cats scratch. When stressed, a cat will claw the seat cover and any exposed surface. Their small size means they can slip between the cover and the door. And unlike most dogs, they don't settle — they explore, hide, and try to escape.

## The Best Cat Setup

**1. A carrier always.** Cats should NEVER ride loose in a car — they're a distraction and a crash hazard. A carrier is non-negotiable.

**2. A cover under the carrier.** The carrier's plastic base scratches seats. A waterproof cover protects the seat from the carrier and from accidents (stress causes cat accidents).

**3. Fleece or soft cover on top.** If you let the cat out at your destination (in a secure car), a soft quilted cover gives them grip and comfort — cats slide on slippery surfaces and panic.

## Features for Cat Owners

- **Scratch-resistant fabric:** Quilted polyester resists claw damage better than smooth surfaces
- **Full waterproofing:** Stress accidents happen
- **High sides:** A contained space feels safer for cats
- **Washability:** Cat scent builds up; wash frequently to reduce stress-marking

## Reducing Cat Stress

- Cover the carrier with a light cloth during the drive (reduces visual stress)
- Use a pheromone spray (Feliway) on the cover before travel
- Start with short trips
- Keep the carrier covered on the seat — under the cover for extra security
