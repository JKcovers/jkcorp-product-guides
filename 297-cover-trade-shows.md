# Cover Trade Shows and Exhibitions: Where the Deals Happen

Cover trade shows and exhibitions remain the fastest way to meet serious buyers face to face, compare competitors, and close distributor deals that email cannot. For protective cover manufacturers and importers, a few shows each year cover most of the industry's deal flow.

## The Major Shows

| Show | Location | Timing | Why Covers Attend |
|------|----------|--------|-------------------|
| Canton Fair | Guangzhou, China | April & October | The largest sourcing event; thousands of buyers, full product range |
| AAPEX | Las Vegas, USA | November | Automotive aftermarket buyers, including car accessories |
| SEMA | Las Vegas, USA | November | Performance and custom vehicle market; covers and accessories |
| Automechanika | Frankfurt / global rotation | Varies | Global automotive aftermarket; strong European buyer base |
| IBEX | Tampa, USA | October | Marine industry; boat cover and canvas suppliers |

## What to Do Before the Show

- Book meetings with 10-15 pre-qualified buyers, not just walk-ins
- Prepare a one-page capability sheet (products, MOQ, certifications, lead time)
- Bring physical samples: fabric swatches, hardware, and finished covers
- Set clear goals: X qualified leads, Y sample requests, Z quotes
- Print price lists with clear FOB terms

## What to Do at the Show

- **Lead capture:** QR-code digital catalogs plus a structured lead form
- **Product demo:** test fabric, water resistance, and hardware on the spot
- **Competitor scan:** photograph and price out competing booths
- **Buyer qualification:** ask about volume, market, and timeline before promising quotes

## After the Show: The Real Work

- Respond to every lead within 48 hours with a personalized quote
- Ship requested samples within 2 weeks
- Add leads to a nurture sequence: capability sheet, samples, quarterly news
- Track show ROI: leads converted per show, not just leads collected

## Digital Alternatives

For manufacturers who cannot travel, digital showrooms, Alibaba live rooms, and virtual factory tours serve the same qualification function at lower cost — combine both: meet at the show, follow up online.

## Budgeting for Shows

- Allocate booth, travel, and samples; freight alone can reach 20% of show cost
- Budget for post-show follow-up: samples, capability sheets, and visits
- Measure ROI per show: leads converted, not booths visited
- Rotate show attendance across regions to match buyer geography
- Share the booth with a complementary supplier to split costs when starting out
- Follow up every qualified lead twice: once at 48 hours, once at two weeks
