# SUV Car Cover Buying Guide: Size, Shape and Storage

SUVs bring extra considerations to cover buying: bigger dimensions, taller profiles, roof rails, and rear spoilers. An SUV car cover that fits a sedan will not fit an SUV, and a cheap universal cover flaps on a tall body. Here is what to check before buying an SUV cover.

## Why SUVs Are Different

- **Length and height:** most SUVs fall into the large (205-220 inches) or XL (220-240 inches) classes
- **Tall profile:** covers must drape lower and cinch tighter to resist wind
- **Roof rails and antennas:** need pockets or adjustment zones
- **Rear spoilers and wipers:** can snag the fabric during installation

## Measuring Your SUV

1. Overall length, bumper to bumper
2. Width including mirrors (folded and extended)
3. Height to the roof rail top, not the antenna
4. Wheelbase for strap positioning

Send these four numbers to the manufacturer for custom-fit recommendations; do not rely on the model name alone.

## Fabric Selection for SUVs

| Fabric | Why It Works |
|--------|--------------|
| 420D Oxford, coated | Balanced outdoor protection at moderate weight |
| 600D Oxford, coated | Stronger wind and abrasion resistance |
| Multi-layer with lining | Scratch-free soft inner layer on taller bodies |
| Breathable waterproof | Prevents condensation under a large surface area |

Larger covers weigh more: expect 5-10 lbs for a full-size SUV cover, more for premium multi-layer versions.

## Features That Matter

- **Roof rail clearance:** adjustable straps or cutouts for rails
- **Tie-down straps:** two or three straps keep the cover on tall bodies in wind
- **Mirror pockets:** standard on quality covers
- **Storage bag:** large enough for the folded cover

## Quick Takeaways

- SUVs live in the large and XL classes; always measure height to the roof rails
- Tall bodies need tie-down straps and tighter hems to resist wind
- Choose soft-lined multi-layer fabric so the large cover never scratches paint
- Install front-to-rear and check roof rail clearance before tightening
- Expect 5-10 lbs of weight for a full-size SUV cover, more for premium layers
- Keep the storage bag accessible; a heavy SUV cover needs a proper folding routine

## Installation Tips

- Install from the front, working toward the rear
- Pull the cover over the roof rail zone carefully to avoid snags
- Cinch straps from the center outward
- Check mirror pockets sit fully over the mirrors before tightening
