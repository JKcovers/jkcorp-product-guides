# Golf Cart Cover Buying Guide — Size, Fabric and Features

A golf cart is a significant investment, whether it's a street-legal cart or a yard workhorse. Left uncovered, rain pools on the seats and sun cracks the dash. A golf cart cover protects the cart and keeps it showroom-ready. Here is the complete buying guide.

## Why Golf Carts Need Covers

- **Seats crack** under prolonged UV exposure
- **Electrical components** corrode in rain
- **Dashboards fade** and become brittle
- **Batteries** are exposed to temperature swings
- **Resale value drops** fast on weathered carts

## Golf Cart Cover Sizing

Golf carts come in two dominant footprints:

| Cart Type | Approximate Size |
|-----------|------------------|
| 2-seat | 92" L x 48" W x 68" H |
| 4-seat / lifted | 110" L x 50" W x 74" H |

Measure your cart's length, width, and height — including mirrors, rear seats, and accessories. Add 10-15 cm for a comfortable fit. Many covers offer a "lifted cart" variant with extra height.

## Features to Prioritize

- **Wind straps with buckles** — carts sit in open areas
- **Fleece or soft lining** — protects the windshield and dash
- **Access panels** — covers with zippered doors for storage access
- **UV protection** — critical for seats and dashboards
- **Reflective accents** — helps locate the cart at dusk

## Storage and Care

- Cover a dry cart — seats hold moisture
- Secure all straps before windstorms
- Clean the cover quarterly with mild soap
- Check for wear at the corners and windshield contact points
- Remove snow with a broom to prevent fabric collapse

## Indoor vs Outdoor

- **Garage storage:** a breathable dust cover suffices
- **Outdoor storage:** full waterproof cover with straps
- **Coastal areas:** choose UV-stabilized fabric; salt spray eats ordinary coatings

## Key Takeaways

- Measure length, width, and height including mirrors and accessories — lifted carts need extra height
- Wind straps are essential for open-area parking
- UV protection is the top priority for seats, dashboards, and plastics
