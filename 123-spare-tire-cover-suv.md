# Spare Tire Covers for SUVs and Jeeps — Protection Plus Personality

The rear-mounted spare tire is a defining feature of many SUVs, Jeeps, and off-roaders. A tire cover protects it — and gives you a canvas for personality, branding, or vehicle identification.

## Why Mounted Spares Need Protection

A rear-mounted spare faces direct sun all day, every day. UV radiation drys out rubber compounds, causing cracking and dry rot that can make the spare unusable. Road spray, dirt, and airborne debris accelerate the damage. A cover blocks 99% of UV and protects the tire's integrity.

## The Double Function

Beyond protection, covers serve as:
- **Branding:** Custom-printed covers for fleets, dealerships, and brands
- **Identification:** Vehicle/owner names for off-road groups
- **Style:** Camo, skulls, mountain scenes, solid colors
- **Privacy:** Hides tire condition and brand

## Material Options

**Vinyl (standard):** Waterproof, wipe-clean, most design options. The classic choice.

**Oxford fabric:** Softer, breathable, heat-resistant. Better for extreme climates.

**Canvas:** Heavyweight, premium feel, retro look.

## Sizing

Size by tire diameter. Check the tire sidewall (e.g., 265/75R16 ≈ 32" diameter) or measure the mounted tire's overall height. Covers list compatible tire sizes — when between sizes, go up.
