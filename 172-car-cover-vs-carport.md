# Car Cover vs Carport — Cost and Protection Compared

Car owners face a real choice: build a carport or buy a good cover. Both protect from weather. The difference is cost, permanence, and what each actually protects against. Here's the honest comparison.

## The Protection Comparison

| Threat | Carport | Quality Cover |
|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Rain | ✅ | ✅ |
| UV | ⚠️ (partial shade) | ✅ (99% rejection) |
| Bird droppings | ✅ (if roof covers) | ✅ |
| Tree sap | ✅ | ✅ |
| Dust | ⚠️ (blows in) | ✅ |
| Wind-blown debris | ⚠️ | ⚠️ |
| Theft visibility | ❌ (visible) | ✅ (hidden) |
| Hail | ⚠️ (if solid roof) | ⚠️ (padded only) |
| Condensation | ⚠️ (open sides help) | ⚠️ (breathable only) |

## The Cost Comparison

| Option | Upfront Cost | Maintenance | Lifespan |
|--------|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| DIY carport kit | $800-2,500 | Low | 10-20 years |
| Pro-built carport | $3,000-8,000 | Low | 20+ years |
| Premium cover | $50-150 | Replace every 3-4 years | 3-4 years |

## The 5-Year Cost

| Option | 5-Year Cost |
|--------|:--:|
| DIY carport | $800-2,500 |
| Premium cover (1.5 replacements) | $75-225 |

A cover delivers 80% of a carport's protection at under 10% of the cost.

## The Real Differences

**Carport advantages:** Permanent protection, no setup time, protects while you drive in/out, adds property value.

**Cover advantages:** Portable (moves with the car), far cheaper, protects anywhere (work, travel), hides the car from thieves.

## The Verdict

- **Long-term home parking:** A carport is the better long-term investment if you own the property
- **Renters, travelers, multiple locations:** A cover wins — portable protection
- **Most owners:** A cover is the smarter first purchase; add a carport later if you stay put
