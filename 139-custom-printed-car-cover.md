# Custom Printed Car Covers — Branding and Personalization Options

Custom-printed car covers have moved from novelty to serious business. From corporate fleet branding to personalized gifts, full-print covers are one of the fastest-growing segments in protective covers.

## The Printing Technologies

**Sublimation printing (full-print):** Dyes are transferred into the fabric fibers at high temperature. The result is a full-color, edge-to-edge design that won't crack or peel — and won't fade as fast as surface prints. Best for full-vehicle graphics and photo-realistic designs.

**Silkscreen (logo):** The classic method for solid-color logos. Most durable, best for single or few colors. Perfect for OEM brand logos.

**Heat transfer (vinyl):** Cut vinyl applied with heat. Good for names, numbers, and simple graphics. Common for motorsport and personalization.

**Embroidery:** Premium stitched logos. Best for indoor covers and luxury positioning.

## Print Quality Factors

- **Resolution:** Full-print requires 300dpi+ artwork
- **Color matching:** Pantone matching for brand accuracy
- **Print placement:** Logo on hood, roof, or full-body
- **UV protection over print:** Printed designs need a UV-resistant coating to last outdoors

## The OEM Process

1. Submit artwork (AI, PSD, PDF, or 300dpi images)
2. Receive digital proof for approval
3. Approve pre-production sample
4. Bulk production (25-35 days for full-print)
5. QC: print alignment, color accuracy, seam quality
