# Car Cover Folding and Removal — The Technique That Prevents Scratches

How you remove and fold your car cover directly affects your paint's condition. The wrong technique drags grit across the clear coat; the right one keeps the cover clean and the paint flawless.

## The Removal Problem

Most owners grab the cover by one edge and drag it across the hood to pull it off. This is the #1 self-inflicted scratch source — the fabric picks up dust and grit as it drags, acting like sandpaper against the paint.

## The Correct Removal Method

**Step 1 — Loosen the rear first:** Stand at the rear of the car, pull the elastic hem up and over the trunk edge.

**Step 2 — Fold as you walk forward:** Instead of dragging, fold the cover in accordion sections over itself as you walk toward the front. The cover never drags across the paint — it lifts and folds.

**Step 3 — Front last:** When you reach the front, fold the last section over. The cover is now a neat bundle.

**Step 4 — Bag it:** Slide the bundle into the storage bag. No dragging, no grit transfer.

## The Accordion Fold Detail

Folding in 18-24 inch sections (matching the car's width) prevents sharp creases that weaken fabric. Consistent folds also make the cover faster to unfold next time.

## When the Cover Is Wet

A wet cover should NEVER go straight into the bag — mildew develops within days. Instead:
1. Drape it over a railing or the car roof to dry
2. Return in 30-60 minutes and fold clean
3. If you must store it wet, air it out as soon as possible

## The Rule

Never drag. Always lift and fold. This single habit extends both paint life and cover life.
