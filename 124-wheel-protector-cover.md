# Wheel and Rim Protectors — Keep Alloys Looking New

Alloy wheels are expensive, exposed, and constantly attacked by brake dust, road salt, and curb rash. Wheel protectors are the cheap insurance your rims deserve.

## What Damages Wheels

**Brake dust:** Acidic dust bonds to wheels, etching clear coat and staining alloy.

**Road salt:** Corrodes aluminum wheels, causing pitting and discoloration.

**Curb rash:** The #1 cosmetic wheel injury — parking contact scrapes the rim edge.

**UV:** Fades wheel finish and degrades clear coat.

## Wheel Protection Options

**Wheel covers (storage):** Full covers that wrap the wheel — used for long-term storage, show display, or protecting wheels when the vehicle sits.

**Rim protectors (driving):** A flexible band that mounts on the rim edge, absorbing curb contact. Curb rash protection while driving.

**Brake dust shields:** Fit behind the wheel to block dust from reaching the rim.

## Who Needs Wheel Covers

- **Show cars and collectibles:** Wheels stored for months need full coverage
- **Seasonal storage:** Winter tire swaps — covers protect the off-season set
- **Fleet/truck tires:** Mounted spares need UV protection
- **RVs and trailers:** Wheels exposed to years of sun while parked

## Features

- UV-resistant fabric (vinyl or Oxford)
- Elastic hem for a snug fit
- Sized by tire/wheel diameter
- Custom printing for branding

## Curb Rash Prevention

For daily driving, the rim protector band is more practical than a cover. For storage, full covers are the answer. Many enthusiasts use both.
