# Car Cover FAQ — Top 20 Questions Answered

Buying a car cover raises more questions than almost any other auto accessory. Sizes, fabrics, waterproofing, indoor vs outdoor — the details decide whether a cover protects or damages. This car cover FAQ answers the 20 questions buyers ask most, in plain language.

## The Top 20 Questions

**1. Do I really need a car cover?**
If your car sits outside, under trees, or in a dusty garage, yes. Covers protect paint from UV, bird droppings, tree sap, dust, and scratches. A sealed-garage car may only need a light dust cover.

**2. Indoor or outdoor cover?**
Outdoor covers need waterproofing, UV resistance, and wind anchoring; indoor covers only block dust.

**3. What fabric is best?**
For outdoor all-weather use, 300-600D polyester with a water-resistant coating is the workhorse; premium buyers choose multi-layer Oxford or breathable polypropylene.

**4. How do I know what size to buy?**
Measure the car's length, width, and height, then check the cover's size chart. If between sizes, size up — a slightly loose cover beats one that cannot close.

**5. Are universal covers OK?**
For occasional use, yes. For daily outdoor parking, a semi-custom or custom-fit cover fits better, flaps less, and lasts longer.

**6. Will a cover scratch my paint?**
Only if it is dirty or has a rough lining. Choose a soft inner lining and always cover a clean car.

**7. Waterproof or breathable?**
Both, in balance: sealed waterproof covers trap condensation; fully breathable covers leak in heavy rain.

**8. Can I leave it on all winter?**
Yes, with a cover rated for snow and wind, but check every few weeks for moisture and re-secure straps after storms.

**9. How long does a cover last?**
2-5 years depending on fabric, climate, and care. UV is the biggest killer — covers in sunny climates wear faster.

**10. Can I wash my cover?**
Yes. Machine wash cold on gentle, air dry. Avoid fabric softeners and high heat.

**11. Does a cover prevent theft?**
Not really. Some covers add locks, but no cover stops a determined thief.

**12. Are covers bad for paint in humid climates?**
Only if the cover traps moisture. Breathable covers manage humidity; sealed covers cause mildew.

**13. What about hail?**
A padded or quilted cover absorbs small hail; for severe hail, park under cover.

**14. Should I cover a wet car?**
Never. Always dry the car first — trapped moisture is the #1 cause of damage under covers.

**15. Do mirror pockets matter?**
Yes — they fit better and reduce seam stress. If no pockets, pad the mirrors first.

**16. What is the difference between 420D and 600D?**
Denier measures thread weight. 600D is heavier and more tear-resistant — better for outdoor and windy conditions.

**17. Can I use a car cover on a motorcycle?**
Different shape, different needs. Buy a cover designed for motorcycles instead.

**18. How do I store the cover when not in use?**
Clean and dry it first, then fold loosely or use the storage bag. Never store it damp.

**19. Do covers affect the paint under hot sun?**
A quality cover reflects heat and blocks UV — it protects paint. A cheap dark cover can trap heat and speed aging.

**20. Is a cover worth the money?**
Yes for outdoor parkers: repainting or paint correction costs far more than a quality cover.

## Final Advice

Buy for your climate and parking, not the lowest price. Check fabric specs, choose the right size, and cover a clean car.
