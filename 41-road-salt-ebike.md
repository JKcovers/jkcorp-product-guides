# How Road Salt Destroys Ebike Batteries — And How a Cover Prevents It

Winter road salt is an ebike battery's worst enemy. Here's the chemistry and how a cover stops it.

## The Chemistry Problem

Road salt (sodium chloride) dissolves in water to create an electrolyte solution that accelerates metal corrosion. When salt-laden spray hits your battery connectors, the corrosion process begins immediately — even faster than plain water rust.

## Where Damage Happens

- **Battery connectors:** The most vulnerable point. Corroded connectors cause intermittent power loss and eventually complete failure.
- **Charging port:** Salt crystals can bridge contacts and cause short circuits during charging.
- **Battery housing seams:** Micro-gaps in the plastic housing let salt solution seep in over time.

## How a Cover Helps

A waterproof battery cover creates a physical barrier between salt spray and your battery. After a winter ride, the cover has salt residue on the outside — but the battery underneath is dry. Rinse the cover (not the battery) after salty rides.

## Winter Care Routine

1. Rinse the cover weekly to remove salt buildup
2. Inspect battery connectors monthly for any white/green residue (early corrosion signs)
3. Apply dielectric grease to connectors before winter for extra protection
4. Bring the battery indoors for charging — never charge a frozen battery
