# Car Cover Sizing for Multiple Vehicles — One Size Doesn't Fit All

If you own more than one car, you can't just buy two of the same cover unless they're identical models. Here's how to manage covers for a multi-car household.

## Universal vs Dedicated Approach

Universal covers save money if your cars are in the same size class (e.g., two mid-size sedans). One cover fits both. If your vehicles span different classes — a sedan and an SUV — you'll need separate covers. A universal SUV cover is too large for a sedan and will flap in the wind. A sedan cover won't stretch over an SUV.

## Color-Coding System

If you buy the same cover model in the same color, you'll mix them up constantly. Most OEM manufacturers offer custom colors at no extra charge. Order your sedan cover in silver and the SUV cover in blue. Color-coded storage bags complete the system.

## Storage for Multi-Car Homes

With 3+ covers in rotation, storage gets chaotic. Dedicate a wall hook or shelf in the garage for each cover's bag. Label hooks by vehicle ("Camry", "Highlander", "Miata"). Fold covers accordion-style before bagging — it's faster than rolling and prevents creases.

## Budget for Replacement Cycles

More covers = more replacement cycles. If one cover lasts 3 years and you have three cars, budget for one new cover per year on average. Buying OEM wholesale saves 40-60% versus retail, making multi-car cover ownership much more affordable.
