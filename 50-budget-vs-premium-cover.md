# Budget vs Premium Car Covers — Where to Spend and Where to Save

Not every car cover purchase requires a top-tier multi-layer composite. Here's a practical guide to matching your budget to your actual needs.

## When Budget Covers ($20-40) Make Sense

- **Indoor garage storage:** You don't need waterproofing or extreme UV protection. A basic PEVA or lightweight polyester cover keeps dust off and costs the least.
- **Temporary outdoor use:** Using a cover for a few weeks while your garage is being renovated? A budget cover works for short-term protection.
- **Second vehicle or project car:** A car that's worth $3,000 doesn't need a $150 cover. Scale your protection investment to your vehicle's value.

## When Mid-Range Covers ($50-80) Are the Sweet Spot

- **Year-round outdoor parking in mild climates:** 420D Oxford with basic waterproof coating handles rain, sun, and wind without breaking the bank.
- **Daily drivers:** Mid-range covers balance cost, durability, and protection for cars you use every day.

## When Premium Covers ($100-150+) Are Worth It

- **Extreme weather regions:** Heavy snow, desert sun, coastal salt require 600D+ Oxford with multi-layer construction.
- **New or luxury vehicles:** A $150 cover protecting a $50,000 car's paint is a 0.3% investment.
- **Long-term outdoor storage:** If the car won't move for 3+ months, invest in the best cover you can afford.
