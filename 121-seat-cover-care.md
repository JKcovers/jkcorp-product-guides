# Seat Cover Care Guide — Keep Your Covers Looking New

Seat covers are a maintenance-friendly upgrade — but they need their own care routine. Here's how to keep fabric, neoprene, and leather covers looking fresh.

## Daily Habits

- Shake out crumbs and dust weekly
- Wipe spills immediately — the faster, the easier
- Keep sharp objects (keys, tools) out of pockets over the seats

## Fabric and Polyester Covers

**Machine wash (removable covers):**
- Remove and wash monthly (or after stains)
- Cold water, gentle cycle, mild detergent
- Air dry — never machine dry

**Vacuum:** For fixed covers, vacuum with a brush attachment weekly.

## Neoprene Covers

- Wipe with mild soap and water — neoprene resists stains
- Machine wash gentle if heavily soiled
- Air dry; never heat

## Leather and Leatherette Covers

- Wipe with a damp cloth weekly
- Apply leather conditioner every 3-4 months
- Avoid direct sun exposure (cracks leather)
- Never use ammonia or alcohol-based cleaners

## Stain-Specific Treatment

| Stain | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| Coffee | Blot, then mild soap solution |
| Grease | Dish soap, let sit 10 min, rinse |
| Ink | Rubbing alcohol, blot |
| Mud | Let dry, brush off, then wash |

## When to Replace

- Fabric pilling or tearing at seams
- Foam backing disintegrating
- Stains that won't come out (embedded odor)
- Stretched-out fit that bunches
