# Outdoor Heater Cover Guide — Protect Your Patio Heater Year-Round

Patio heaters are an investment. A single winter of rain, frost and wind can rust the burner, corrode the reflector and kill a heater that should have lasted a decade. An outdoor heater cover keeps it running and looking new.

## Why Your Patio Heater Needs a Cover

- **Rust and corrosion prevention** — moisture attacks the burner and gas fittings
- **Ignition protection** — keeps water out of the piezo starter and control valves
- **Wind and debris defense** — stops leaves and insects clogging the burner guard
- **Cleaner operation** — a covered heater burns more efficiently with clean air intake

## Types of Patio Heaters and Their Covers

| Heater type | Cover shape | Key point |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Freestanding propane | Tall cylinder | Height and base diameter are critical |
| Tabletop | Short, wide | Must fit over the reflector |
| Wall-mounted | Contoured | Look for a dedicated shape, not universal |
| Electric | Slim, low | Waterproofing still matters outdoors |

## Measuring Your Heater

Measure the **height from the base to the top of the reflector**, and the **widest diameter across the reflector or base**. Most covers are sized by height (e.g., 6 ft, 7 ft, 8 ft). Add a few centimeters so the cover sits fully over the unit without stretching.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford with PU coating** — the standard for all-season heater covers
- **Elastic hem or drawstring** — prevents wind from lifting the cover and scratching the reflector
- **Waterproof taped seams** — keeps rain from seeping through stitching
- **Breathable inner lining** — reduces condensation, which can rust the burner from inside

## Care and Safety Notes

1. Always let the heater cool completely before covering
2. Turn off and disconnect the propane tank before long-term covering
3. Check the cover monthly for water pooling and brush off snow
4. Clean the cover with mild soap and water in spring

## Key Takeaways

- Measure total height and widest diameter, then size up slightly
- 600D Oxford with elastic hem suits most climates
- Never cover a warm heater — let it cool first
- A covered patio heater lasts roughly twice as long
