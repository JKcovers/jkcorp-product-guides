# Side Mirror Covers — Protecting the Most Exposed Part of Your Car

Side mirrors protrude beyond the vehicle body, making them the first thing to hit parking poles, garage door frames, and other cars. Mirror covers offer targeted protection.

## Why Mirrors Take the Brunt

Side mirrors stick out 6-10 inches past the body. In tight parking, they're the collision point 90% of the time. A hit breaks the mirror housing, motor, or heating element — replacement costs $100-600 depending on the vehicle.

## The Types of Mirror Protection

**Storage mirror covers:** Fabric covers that protect mirrors during storage or car-cover use. They work with full car covers — mirror pockets reduce the baggy fabric at the widest point.

**Driving protection:** Some covers absorb light impacts, but no fabric cover protects against serious collisions — that's what the parking fold feature is for.

**Garage/parking protectors:** Foam padding applied to the mirror for close-quarters parking (common in narrow garages).

## Mirror Covers vs Car Cover Mirror Pockets

If you use a full car cover, the mirror pockets ARE the protection — pre-shaped pockets eliminate loose fabric that flaps and scratches. Standalone mirror covers are for cars stored without a full cover.

## Features

- **Fleece inner lining:** Never scratch the mirror housing
- **Elastic hem:** Secure fit
- **UV-resistant outer:** Protect the housing finish
- **Compatible with car covers:** Some attach under the full cover

## When to Use

- Long-term outdoor storage without a full cover
- As an extra layer with a car cover in harsh climates
- Protection against UV and bird droppings on the housing
