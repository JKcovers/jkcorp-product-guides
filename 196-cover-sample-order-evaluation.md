# Sample Orders for Covers — How to Evaluate Before Committing

A sample order is the cheapest insurance in cover importing. One $30-80 sample can prevent a $30,000 mistake. But only if you evaluate it correctly. Here is how to order, inspect, and judge cover samples like a professional buyer.

## Why Samples Matter for Covers

Covers are judged by feel and finish — fabric hand, stitch quality, zipper smoothness, print accuracy. Photos and videos compress all of that. A physical sample lets you and your customers test the product before a bulk order.

## Types of Sample Orders

| Sample Type | Purpose | Typical Cost |
|-------------|---------|--------------|
| Stock sample | Evaluate an existing product | Free-30 USD |
| Customized sample | Logo, size, or color on stock design | 30-80 USD |
| Pre-production sample | Exact bulk specs before mass production | Covered by order or 30-80 USD |

## What to Evaluate on a Cover Sample

### 1. Fabric and Feel

- Weight and texture match the spec (420D vs 600D Oxford feel very different)
- Waterproofing: pour water on the surface and check the back
- Breathability: does vapor escape or pool?

### 2. Workmanship

- Stitch density and straightness
- Seam strength at stress points (pull gently)
- Zippers: open and close them 20 times
- Elastic hem tension and stitching

### 3. Print and Logo

- Placement matches the approved proof
- Colors match your brand guidelines
- Print durability: rub the surface — does ink transfer?

### 4. Size and Fit

- Measure all dimensions against the size chart
- If possible, test-fit on the actual vehicle or furniture
- Check the size label accuracy

### 5. Packaging

- Polybag quality and sealing
- Hang tag, barcode, and instructions included
- Carton size and weight for freight calculation

## The Sample Feedback Loop

Good sample evaluation is a conversation, not a verdict. Send the factory a structured list:

1. What passes
2. What needs adjustment (with photos)
3. Exact tolerance required
4. Request a revised sample or confirmation
