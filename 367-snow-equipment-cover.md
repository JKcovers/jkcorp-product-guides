# Snow Equipment Cover Guide — Protect Shovels, Throwers and Winter Tools

Shovels, snow throwers and winter gear spend nine months of the year in storage, where rust and dust quietly destroy them. A snow equipment cover keeps winter tools sharp, dry and ready for the first storm.

## Why Cover Snow Equipment

- **Rust prevention** — moisture on blades and augers corrodes metal fast
- **Fuel and oil protection** — keeps debris out of engine components on snow throwers
- **Edge preservation** — protects shovel blades and scraper edges from dings
- **Organized storage** — shovels, brooms and scrapers stay together in one place
- **Winter readiness** — equipment works first try when the snow arrives

## What to Cover

| Item | Cover style |
|------|-------------|
| Snow shovel | Sleeve or bag for the blade; full-length bag for the handle |
| Snow thrower | Full machine cover, sized to width and height |
| Snow blower accessories | Storage bag for chute tools, gloves and ice scrapers |
| Roof rake | Long, tubular storage sleeve |

## Sizing Your Snow Thrower Cover

Measure the **width across the auger housing**, the **total height to the top of the handlebar**, and the **length from chute to rear**. Snow thrower covers are sold by width class (e.g., 24 in, 28 in, 32 in) — match your machine's width and size up slightly for the chute. Shovel sleeves just need the blade width plus a few inches.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — durable against shed damp and occasional outdoor storage
- **Waterproof construction** — important if gear lives on a porch or cart
- **Elastic hem or drawstring** — secures the cover in windy conditions
- **Reinforced bottom** — resists abrasion from concrete floors

## Storage Best Practices

1. Clean and dry all equipment before covering — salt and ice residue cause rust
2. Drain fuel from snow throwers or add stabilizer
3. Store covers in a dry place when not in season
4. Hang shovels blade-up under their covers to protect edges

## Key Takeaways

- Match snow thrower covers to auger width and size up for the chute
- 600D Oxford with elastic hem is the all-season standard
- Always dry and clean gear before covering
- Covered equipment starts and cuts better when winter returns
