# Long-Distance Motorcycle Touring — Packing and Protecting Your Bike

A motorcycle cover is the last thing on most riders' minds before a long tour. It should be the first. Here's how touring riders protect their bikes between hotel nights and campsites.

## Why Touring Needs a Different Cover

On tour, your bike faces a new environment every night: hotel parking lots, campsites, ferry decks, and rented garages. The cover must be compact enough to pack and quick enough to deploy after a long day's ride.

## The Touring Cover Checklist

**Weight under 2 lbs:** Every ounce counts in luggage. A 4-lb storage cover is dead weight for 23 hours a day.

**Compresses to bottle size:** Must fit in a saddlebag or tail bag pocket.

**Heat-resistant panels:** After a full day of riding, the exhaust and engine are hot for 30-60 minutes. Covering immediately with a non-heat-resistant cover risks melting.

**Quick-deploy design:** Elastic hem + one belly strap. No bungee tetris after an 8-hour day.

## Protecting the Bike Between Rides

- **Hotel parking:** Full cover + disc lock. The cover hides the bike and deters casual theft.
- **Campsite:** Cover against dew and insects — a fleece-lined lightweight cover works, or the same touring cover.
- **Ferry:** The deck environment is salty and wet. Use the full waterproof cover if it's a long crossing.

## Packing the Cover

- Always pack the cover where you can reach it without unpacking luggage (outer pocket)
- If the cover is wet from morning dew, dry it over the bike for 10-15 minutes before packing
- Carry a stuff sack backup — covers get damp and need separate storage
