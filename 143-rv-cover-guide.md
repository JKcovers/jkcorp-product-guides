# RV Cover Guide — Protect Your Motorhome Through Every Season

An RV is one of the largest purchases most families make — and it spends most of its life parked outdoors. An RV cover protects that investment from sun, snow, and weather damage year-round.

## Why RVs Need Covers

RVs sit idle for weeks or months between trips. During that time they face: UV degradation of the fiberglass and roof sealant, roof leaks from freeze-thaw cycles, snow load stress, and fading decals. A cover significantly slows all of this.

## The RV Cover Specs

| Feature | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Material | Heavy-duty Oxford (600D+) or woven polypropylene |
| Waterproofing | 3000mm+ |
| UV resistance | 95%+ rejection |
| Roof protection | Multiple-layer construction at the roof panels |
| Wind protection | Full under-body straps + ladder access zippers |

## The Ladder and Access Problem

RV covers need zippered access panels for:
- Entry door
- Ladder (for roof access)
- Side compartments
- Electrical hookup points

Without these, you're removing the cover every time you need the RV. Quality covers include 2-4 access zippers.

## Sizing

RV covers are sized by the rig's length and height class:

| RV Type | Length Range | Typical Cover |
|---------|:--:|:--:|
| Class B camper van | 16-20 ft | Small |
| Class C motorhome | 20-30 ft | Medium |
| Class A motorhome | 25-40 ft | Large-XL |
| Fifth wheel | 20-40 ft | Large-XL |
| Travel trailer | 16-35 ft | Medium-XL |

## The Storage Decision

- **Covered storage (roofed):** A breathable dust cover suffices
- **Open storage:** Full weather cover required
- **Snow regions:** Heavy-duty cover + periodic snow removal

## Common Mistakes

- Sizing by brand instead of length
- Ignoring the roof (the most vulnerable part — ensure roof reinforcement)
- Skipping access zippers
- Tightening straps too much (damages gutters and seals)
