# Motorcycle Cover Materials — Oxford vs Polyester vs Nylon

Choosing the wrong motorcycle cover material means replacing it twice as often. Here's a quick comparison of the three most common options.

## Oxford Fabric: The All-Weather Champion

Woven polyester with a PU or PVC waterproof coating. Available in 210D, 420D, and 600D densities.

**Best for:** Year-round outdoor parking in any climate. A 420D Oxford cover lasts 2-3 years outdoors.

## Polyester with PVC Backing: The Value Option

Standard polyester with a waterproof PVC layer bonded to the inside. Slightly less breathable than Oxford but adequately waterproof.

**Best for:** Covered parking, carports, occasional outdoor use. Costs 20-30% less than Oxford.

## Nylon: The Touring Specialist

Lightweight and highly packable. Nylon covers fold down to the size of a water bottle.

**Best for:** Touring riders who need a compact cover for overnight stops. Not for long-term outdoor storage — nylon degrades faster in UV than Oxford.
