# Truck Car Cover Guide: Full Coverage for Pickups

Trucks need a different cover approach than cars: longer wheelbases, cab-plus-bed proportions, and accessories like bed caps and racks change how covers fit. This truck car cover guide covers full-vehicle covers, cab-only options, and the specs that keep a cover on a pickup in wind.

## Truck Cover Styles

| Style | Coverage | Best For |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Full-length cover | Entire truck, cab to tailgate | Outdoor storage, full protection |
| Cab-only cover | Cab and windshield | Quick cover, cab interior protection |
| Bed cover / tonneau | Bed area only | Cargo protection (see tonneau guide) |
| Custom fit per model | Tailored to one truck model | Best fit and wind behavior |

Full-length covers dominate outdoor storage; cab covers suit owners who park inside but want cab shade.

## Measuring a Pickup

- **Overall length:** bumper to bumper; extended cabs and long beds push trucks into XXL classes
- **Height:** measure to the highest point; bed caps and toppers add height
- **Width with mirrors:** mirrors on trucks are wide; measure extended
- **Bed configuration:** note if the bed is standard, long, or with a cap/rack

## Fabric and Construction

Heavy-duty fabrics are the norm: 420D to 600D Oxford with coatings, plus reinforced hems. Trucks sit outside more and face wind from all angles, so look for:

- Double-stitched seams along the hem
- Multiple tie-down straps (front, middle, rear)
- Elastic hem plus grommet points
- UV-stabilized fabric for sun exposure

## Accessories and Fit

- **Bed caps and toppers:** need taller patterns or adjustable panels
- **Roof racks and tool boxes:** measure and allow clearance
- **Antennas:** removable or pocketed
- **Towing mirrors:** wider pockets required on heavy-duty models

## Quick Takeaways

- Full-length covers protect the whole truck; cab-only covers suit indoor parking
- Measure overall length, height to the highest point, and mirror width
- Heavy-duty 420D-600D fabric with multiple straps is the minimum for pickups
- Bed caps, racks, and towing mirrors change the pattern; measure with them installed
- Check strap tension after storms; large truck surfaces catch wind hard

## Wind Performance

Trucks present a large surface to wind, so strap systems matter more than on cars. Cinch straps at the hood, cab, and tailgate, and check tension after storms. In very windy regions, consider a cover with an extra center strap.
