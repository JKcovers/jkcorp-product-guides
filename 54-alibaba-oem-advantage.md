If you're sourcing car covers for your business, Alibaba offers advantages that domestic wholesale can't match. Here are five reasons to buy OEM direct.

## 1. Factory-Direct Pricing

Skip the importer, distributor, and retailer markups. A multi-layer Oxford cover that retails for $80-120 costs $15-25 direct from the factory. That's 40-60% savings per unit.

## 2. Full Customization Control

Domestic wholesalers sell what they have in stock. OEM factories build what you want. Custom logo, custom colors (up to 323 options from some manufacturers), custom sizing for specific vehicle models, custom packaging with your brand.

## 3. Quality Consistency

When you buy from the same factory repeatedly, they learn your quality standards. Domestic wholesalers frequently switch suppliers, leading to inconsistent quality batch-to-batch.

## 4. Small Order Flexibility

Most Alibaba car cover factories accept MOQ of 50-100 pieces. For a startup or small business testing a new market, that's manageable. Domestic wholesale minimums are often much higher.

## 5. Trade Assurance Protection

Alibaba's escrow system holds your payment until you confirm receipt and satisfaction. This is stronger buyer protection than most domestic wholesale arrangements.
