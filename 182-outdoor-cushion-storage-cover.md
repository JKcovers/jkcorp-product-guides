# Outdoor Cushion Storage — Covers vs Bringing Them Indoors

Outdoor cushions are the most expensive replaceable part of patio furniture, yet they are usually the least protected. When the season ends you face a choice: store cushions indoors, or keep them outside under covers. Here is the honest comparison.

## The Case for Covers

| Advantage | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Convenience | Cushions stay where they are used |
| Space | No indoor storage needed |
| Furniture protection | Covers protect the frame too |
| Quick access | Sunny days are not blocked by storage logistics |

## The Case for Indoor Storage

| Advantage | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Maximum lifespan | Climate-controlled air beats any fabric |
| No fabric wear | Covers rub cushions over months |
| Pest-free | No nests or chewing damage |
| Zero condensation | The #1 cushion killer is trapped moisture |

## The Hybrid Approach (Recommended)

1. **In-season:** keep cushions outdoors under a breathable, waterproof cushion cover
2. **Off-season (3+ months):** move cushions indoors; use covers on the bare frames
3. **In-between seasons:** use a heavy-duty waterproof cover with ventilation

## What to Look for in a Cushion Cover

- **Waterproof coating** (PU or TPU) to shed rain
- **Breathable backer** to release condensation
- **Full zipper or drawstring closure** so covers stay on
- **UV-stabilized fabric** so covers themselves survive summer

## Storage Mistakes That Kill Cushions

- **Storing wet:** always air-dry cushions before covering or storing
- **Sealing airtight:** even waterproof covers need airflow; trapped moisture = mildew
- **Skipping the cover on frames:** bare frames rust and crack
- **Stacking heavy loads:** foam compresses permanently

## When Covers Alone Are Enough

In mild, dry climates (or under a covered patio), a good cushion cover alone protects cushions all year. The deciding factor is humidity, not temperature.

## Key Takeaways

- Covers are the best in-season solution; indoor storage wins for long off-season months
- The hybrid approach — breathable waterproof covers in-season, indoor storage in winter — maximizes cushion life
- Trapped moisture, not cold, is the number one killer of outdoor cushions
- Always dry cushions completely before covering, and never seal them airtight
