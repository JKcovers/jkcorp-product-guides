# Car Cover Mirror Pockets and Antenna Design — The Fit Details That Matter

Most buyers compare car covers by fabric and price, but the difference between a cover that lasts and one that fails is often in the details: mirror pockets, antenna accommodation, and seam placement. These small features decide whether the cover fits tight or flaps loose.

## Why Mirror Pockets Matter

Side mirrors are the most fragile protruding part of a car. A cover without pockets:

- Stretches fabric over the mirror, wearing thin spots that tear first
- Leaves the mirror exposed at the seam, letting in rain and dust
- Pushes the whole cover out of position, creating loose fabric over the doors

Proper mirror pockets are reinforced boxed corners that cup the mirror completely. The cover stays aligned, and the mirror is fully sealed.

## Antenna Options

| Antenna Type | Cover Solution |
|--------------|----------------|
| Fixed mast | Padded sleeve or reinforced opening |
| Screw-off | Removable cover with internal grommet |
| Shark fin | Stretch fabric section that conforms |
| None | Standard smooth roof panel |

Fixed antennas are the classic tear point. Covers should either have a padded antenna sleeve that protects the mast from bending or a grommeted opening that lets the antenna pass through cleanly.

## Other Fit Details to Check

- **Reinforced corners** — the first place covers wear out
- **Double-stitched seams** — single stitching fails under wind load
- **Elastic hem** — keeps the cover taut against the bumper line
- **Grommets** — allow cable locks for wind security
- **Wind straps** — for exposed parking

## The Checklist Before You Buy

1. Does the size chart include your car's mirror width?
2. Is there an antenna provision that matches your setup?
3. Are mirror pockets boxed and reinforced, not just stitched?
4. Are stress points (corners, seams) double-stitched?

## Quick Install Tips

- Pull the cover over the roof first, then seat each mirror pocket — never the reverse, which strains the fabric
- Check that both mirror pockets sit fully seated before tightening straps
- If the cover has an antenna opening, route the antenna before settling the roof panel
- After install, walk around the car: the hem should sit below the wheel centers all the way around

## Common Fit Questions

**Will a cover without mirror pockets still fit?** It will stretch over the mirrors, but that stretch point is where covers tear first — and the mirror stays half-exposed.

**Can I add mirror pockets to an existing cover?** A tailor can retrofit them, but factory-reinforced pockets are stronger than any aftermarket stitching.

**Do mirrors need pockets on indoor covers too?** Yes — garage covers rub against mirrors every time they are installed, and un-pocketed fabric wears through quickly.

## Key Takeaways

- Mirror pockets and antenna provisions decide fit quality more than fabric weight
- Boxed, reinforced mirror pockets prevent the most common tear points
- Match the antenna solution to your actual antenna type
