# SUV Pet Seat Covers — Sizing and Setup for Tall Vehicles

SUVs and crossovers are the most popular dog-hauling vehicles in the world. Their tall seats, flat floors, and fold-flat designs create specific cover needs.

## SUV-Specific Challenges

**Tall seatbacks:** Standard covers sized for sedans don't cover SUV seatbacks fully. Choose XL or SUV-specific covers.

**Fold-flat seats:** If you fold the rear seats flat, you need a cover that converts — many SUV covers extend to cover the folded cargo area.

**Cargo access:** Dogs often ride in the cargo area, not the rear seat. Consider a cargo liner instead of (or in addition to) a seat cover.

## The SUV Setup Options

**Option A: Rear seat cover**
- Protects the second-row seats
- Best for dogs that sit on the seat
- XL sizing for tall seatbacks

**Option B: Cargo area liner**
- Protects the cargo floor and seatbacks when folded flat
- Raised edges contain mud and water
- Best for dogs that ride in the back

**Option C: Full coverage (seat cover + cargo liner)**
- The complete SUV dog setup
- Use a cargo liner with raised edges + a seat cover for the second row

## Features for SUV Dog Owners

- **Extra-long straps:** SUV seatbacks are taller; straps must reach
- **Split-folding compatibility:** Covers that accommodate 60/40 split seats
- **Waterproof with raised edges:** SUVs are adventure vehicles — mud and water containment matters
- **Cargo-area hooks:** Some liners use OEM tie-down points
