## Customization Capabilities

- **Materials:** Non-woven, PEVA, polyester, 210D-600D Oxford, SMMS/SFS composite, gold velvet, 250gsm PVC
- **Colors:** 323 standard palette + Pantone matching
- **Printing:** Silkscreen, sublimation (full-print), embroidery, heat transfer
- **Sizing:** Universal, semi-custom (dimension-based), full custom (model template)
- **Packaging:** Custom poly bags, branded boxes, hang tags

## Production Process

1. Template selection or new template creation
2. Material & color confirmation (samples available)
3. Digital proof approval (printed designs)
4. Pre-production sample (1 unit, credited to order)
5. Bulk production: 15-25 days standard, 25-35 days custom
6. QC: seam tension, strap strength, print accuracy
7. Shipping: sea (bulk) or air (samples)
