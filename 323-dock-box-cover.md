# Dock Box Cover — Protection That Extends Dock Box Life

A dock box stores life jackets, lines, and electronics — and sits directly in sun, salt spray, and rain. The lid seals, but the box body fades, cracks, and corrodes over time. A dock box cover is a simple upgrade that extends the box's life by years. Here is the guide.

## What a Dock Box Cover Protects

- **The lid seal** — the most common failure point; UV hardens and cracks the gasket
- **The plastic or fiberglass body** — UV causes fading, chalking, and embrittlement
- **The lock and hinges** — salt corrodes hardware quickly
- **The contents** — a failing seal lets rain into electronics and life jackets

## Choosing the Right Cover

**Size:** measure the box's length, width, and height, including the lid's curvature. The cover should fit like a fitted sheet — snug over the top, with an elastic hem or straps that reach under the lid rim.

**Material:** marine-grade fabric with a UV-resistant coating. Look for:

- Elastic hem for a secure fit
- UV-stabilized fabric (marine acrylic or coated polyester)
- Vent panels to release trapped moisture
- Straps for high-wind docks

## Fitting and Care

1. Wipe the box dry before covering — trapped moisture under the cover is worse than rain on the box.
2. Seat the cover over the lid, centered.
3. Tuck the elastic hem under the lid rim on all four sides.
4. Secure any straps under the box or around the lid handles.
5. Clean the cover with mild soap and fresh water twice a season to maintain its water resistance.

## Common Questions

**Will a cover fit over a locked box?** Yes — most covers clear the latch and keyhole. Check that the fabric does not bind the latch handle, and consider a cover with a zippered access panel for the lock.

**Do I need a cover if the box is under a dock canopy?** A canopy stops direct rain, but salt spray and UV still reach the box. A cover adds protection where the canopy cannot reach the sides.

**How long do covers last in salt air?** Marine environments are harsh — expect 2-3 seasons. Rinse the cover with fresh water monthly to slow fabric and hardware degradation.

**Can I cover a wet box?** Only if you want mildew. Wipe the lid and box dry first; trapped moisture under a cover is worse than open air.

## The Golden Rule

**Cover a dry box, and let the cover breathe.** The best dock box cover in the world cannot save a box that is sealed wet underneath it.
