# How to Clean Your Motorcycle Cover — Step-by-Step

A dirty motorcycle cover does more than look bad — the accumulated grit acts like sandpaper against your bike's paint. Cleaning it regularly extends both the cover's life and your bike's finish.

## How Often to Clean

**Outdoor covers:** Every 2-3 months. Road dust, bird droppings, and tree sap degrade the fabric and waterproofing.

**Indoor covers:** Every 6 months. Indoor dust is less aggressive but still builds up.

**After extreme conditions:** Wash immediately after salt exposure (coastal or winter roads) — salt crystals accelerate fabric degradation.

## Machine Washing (Recommended)

1. Shake out loose dirt and debris
2. Pre-treat bird droppings or sap spots with mild soap
3. Machine wash on gentle/cold cycle
4. Use mild detergent — never bleach or fabric softener
5. Air dry only — never machine dry (heat destroys waterproof coatings and shrinks elastic)

## Hand Washing (For Delicate Covers)

For fleece-lined or heat-sensitive covers:
1. Fill a tub with cold water + mild detergent
2. Submerge and gently agitate for 5 minutes
3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water
4. Squeeze (don't wring) and hang to dry

## Storage After Cleaning

- Ensure the cover is 100% dry before folding
- Store in its carry bag
- If storing for the season, add a silica gel packet to absorb residual moisture
