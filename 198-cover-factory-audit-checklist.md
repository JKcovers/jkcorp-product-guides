# Cover Factory Audit Checklist — What to Verify Before You Commit

A factory audit is a snapshot of a supplier's capability — and a mirror for its honesty. Before placing a cover OEM order, run through this checklist to verify the factory can actually deliver what it promises.

## 1. Legal and Compliance Basics

- Valid business license matching the registered name
- Export qualification (import/export license)
- ISO 9001 quality management certification (verify, don't assume)
- Consistent company name across Alibaba, contracts, and bank documents

## 2. Production Capability

| Checkpoint | What to Verify |
|------------|----------------|
| Cutting equipment | Fabric cutting machines or manual cutting quality |
| Sewing capacity | Number of machines and workers |
| Printing/embroidery | In-house or outsourced? Print quality samples |
| Fabric sources | Where does fabric come from? Stock or custom-woven? |
| Capacity | Monthly output vs your order size — no overbooking |

## 3. Quality Control System

- Incoming material inspection records
- In-line QC at each sewing stage
- Final AQL inspection before packing
- QC reports available to buyers (photos/videos)
- Defect tracking and corrective action process

## 4. Sample and Development Ability

- Speed of sample production (7-15 days is healthy)
- Digital proofs and approval workflow
- Ability to develop custom sizes and features
- Prototype quality on first attempt

## 5. Order Management

- MOQ flexibility and transparent pricing tiers
- Lead time commitment in writing
- Milestone communication (material, production, shipment)
- Change management — what happens when specs change

## 6. The Physical Visit

If you visit (or use a third-party audit):

- Walk the production floor — is it organized?
- Check fabric storage — dry, clean, labeled?
- Talk to workers about their QC duties
- Look at finished goods and packing area
- Ask to see order boards and shipping history

## Red Flags

- Factory refuses a video call or live tour
- Different names on license, signage, and documents
- No QC records available
- Quotes that change when you ask for details
- Extremely low prices with no explanation (cheap fabric + thin coating)

## Audit Frequency

- **First order:** full audit before confirming
- **Repeat orders:** light check — QC reports, capacity confirmation
- **Annually:** re-verify certifications and financial stability
