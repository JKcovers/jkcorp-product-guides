# Pet Seat Cover for Pickup Trucks — Options for Crew and Extended Cabs

Pickup owners love their dogs — and their trucks. The problem is the rear bench of a pickup: it is often narrower than a car seat, has odd angles around the wheel wells, and gets used for everything from hauling gear to carrying muddy paws. A pet seat cover for pickup trucks needs to handle all of it.

## Crew Cab vs Extended Cab: Measure First

| Cab type | Rear seat shape | Cover fit |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Crew cab | Full-size bench, close to car-like | Standard bench cover usually fits |
| Extended cab | Short bench, tight foot space | Compact cover; check seat-back height |
| Double cab | Mid-size bench | Measure width before ordering |

Measure the seat width between the doors and the seat-back height at the center position. Pickup rear seats sit more upright than car seats, so a cover designed for cars may ride up or leave the top of the seat exposed.

## Features That Matter on a Pickup

### 1. Secure Anchoring

Pickup rear seats often have bulky seat belts and LATCH points that interfere with clips. Look for covers with:

- Headrest straps that adjust high
- Seat anchors that grip firmly
- Side flaps that tuck into the door trim

### 2. Tough Fabric

Pickups are working vehicles. Choose a heavy-duty fabric (600D polyester or Oxford) that resists claws, mud, and gear sliding across it.

### 3. Waterproof Backing

Your dog will climb in from a muddy trail, a lake swim, or a rainy worksite. A waterproof backing layer protects the seat foam permanently.

## The Worksite Factor

Many pickup owners carry both a dog and tools or cargo in the cab. Consider:

- **Fold-down center section** — lets the seat convert to a flat work surface
- **Detachable panels** — remove and wash the dog section without washing the whole cover
- **Fender/side protectors** — dogs lean against the door panels when riding

## Installation Tips

1. Remove the headrests, slip the cover straps over the posts, and reinstall
2. Anchor the bottom edge between the seat base and backrest
3. Tuck side flaps into the door gaps
4. Secure the front edge — pickup seats are upright, so gravity pulls covers forward

## Cleaning

Pickup covers collect mud, not just hair. Rinse the cover with a hose before machine washing, then air dry. Avoid high-heat drying — it degrades the waterproof backing.

## When to Upgrade

If your dog rides daily, goes off-road, or swims regularly, treat the cover as a consumable and replace it every 2-3 years. A cheap cover that shifts and leaks will cost more in seat cleaning than a good cover costs upfront.
