# Garage Condensation Management — Protect Cars and Tools

A garage can be the worst place to store a car if condensation is out of control. Warm, humid air meets cold concrete walls, and water appears on the car, the floor, and your tools — all winter long. Garage condensation management is a system problem, not a one-time fix. Here is how to solve it.

## Where Garage Condensation Comes From

- **Temperature differential** — warm air hits cold surfaces (concrete, metal, windows) and condenses
- **Humidity sources** — wet cars entering the garage, dryers, open doors, groundwater
- **Poor ventilation** — trapped moisture has nowhere to go
- **Cold spots** — uninsulated walls and single-pane windows create dew points

## The Condensation Damage Checklist

- Water droplets on the car paint and glass
- Rust forming on tools, shelves, and metal fixtures
- Mildew smell in the garage
- Damp cardboard boxes and softwood floors
- Corrosion on exposed car components

## The 5-Step Management System

### 1. Control the Humidity Source

| Source | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Wet car entering | Dry the car before garaging |
| Open doors in humid weather | Keep closed when humidity is high |
| Clothes dryer vent | Vent to outside, not the garage |
| Groundwater | Seal floor cracks; use a vapor barrier |

### 2. Improve Ventilation

- Install ridge or gable vents that allow cross airflow
- A small exhaust fan with a humidity sensor runs only when needed
- In cold climates, ventilate during the driest part of the day — not at night when humidity peaks

### 3. Manage Temperature

- Insulate walls and the garage door — this reduces cold surfaces that cause condensation
- A small space heater on a thermostat (kept above freezing, not hot) prevents the coldest condensation
- Radiant floor heating is the premium solution but rarely retrofitted

### 4. Use a Dehumidifier

In humid regions, a dehumidifier with a condensate drain is the single most effective tool. Run it at 45-55% relative humidity. This protects the car, the cover, and everything else in the garage.

### 5. Cover the Car Properly

Even with management, condensation will occasionally reach the car. The right car cover is the last line of defense:

- **Breathable cover** — allows moisture to escape instead of trapping it
- **Soft inner lining** — prevents condensation from touching the paint directly
- **Cover removed weekly** — let the paint and cover dry out completely

## A Note on "Garage Covers"

Some buyers think a sealed "garage cover" protects better indoors. The opposite is true in condensation-prone garages: a sealed cover traps the moisture that forms on the car. Indoors, the priority is breathability, not waterproofing. A fleece-lined breathable cover performs best.

## Seasonal Adjustments

- **Spring/Fall** — humidity swings are widest; monitor weekly
- **Winter** — cold surfaces + heated garage = heavy condensation; run the dehumidifier
- **Summer** — keep the garage closed during humid days, ventilate at night
