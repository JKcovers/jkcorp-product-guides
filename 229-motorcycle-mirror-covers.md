# Motorcycle Mirror Covers — Keep Mirrors Scratch-Free in Storage

Motorcycle mirrors are the most vulnerable body parts on any bike. They stick out beyond the handlebars, sit at elbow height in a garage, and collect dust, scratches, and bird droppings during storage. A motorcycle mirror cover is a small investment that keeps mirrors clear and scratch-free — which matters because replacement mirrors are surprisingly expensive and often hard to match.

## Why Mirrors Need Covers

- Mirrors are the widest point of the bike — first to hit walls and other bikes
- Dust settles on glass and scratches it during wiping
- Chrome mirror stems corrode in humid storage
- UV dulls plastic mirror housings over time
- Water spots etch into mirror glass if left to dry

## Types of Mirror Covers

| Type | Material | Protection Level |
|------|----------|------------------|
| Padded bag | Neoprene/fleece | Best — scratch and bump protection |
| Fitted sleeve | Oxford fabric | Good — dust and UV |
| Plastic pouch | Vinyl | Fair — dust only |
| Bar-mounted bag | Combined with bar cover | Good — all-in-one |

## What to Look For

1. **Soft inner lining** — fleece or micro-fiber protects the glass
2. **Snug fit** — a loose cover rubs the mirror and causes the scratch it is meant to prevent
3. **Water resistance** — keeps moisture off the stem and housing
4. **Secure closure** — elastic or drawstring so it stays on in wind
5. **UV protection** — stops housing fading and chrome pitting

## When to Use Them

- **Garage storage** — protects against dust, tools, and accidental bumps
- **Shared parking** — other vehicles and pedestrians hit mirrors daily
- **Touring** — hotel parking lots are full of hazards
- **After washing** — a cover keeps water spots off freshly cleaned mirrors
- **Trailer transport** — protects against vibration and strap contact

## Fitting Tips

Clean the mirror glass before fitting — a cover over a dusty mirror turns the dust into a scouring pad. Fit the cover over the glass and the stem, and make sure the closure sits below the mirror head so water cannot pool on the glass. For chrome stems, apply a light coat of metal protectant before covering.

## Pairing with a Full Cover

For long-term storage, mirror covers work as a second layer under a full motorcycle cover. This matters for bikes parked close to walls or other vehicles, and for touring riders who carry covers in a tank bag. The combination protects the mirrors even if the main cover is lifted or shifted.

## Maintenance

Wash mirror covers with the rest of your motorcycle accessories — machine-washable neoprene and oxford covers clean up easily. Re-apply a DWR treatment to fabric covers each season. Replace a cover when the lining wears thin or the elastic loses tension; a loose cover does more harm than good.

## The Bottom Line

Mirror covers cost little, pack small, and protect the most exposed parts of the bike. Add them to any storage or touring setup.
