# Jet Ski Trailer Cover — The Complete Buying Guide

A jet ski trailer cover protects two investments at once: the personal watercraft and the trailer that hauls it. Most owners cover the ski but forget the trailer — and the trailer is what takes the worst beating from road debris, UV, and salt. This guide covers what a jet ski trailer cover does, how to choose one, and how to use it properly.

## Why Your Jet Ski Trailer Needs a Cover

- Road salt and grime corrode trailer frames and wiring within seasons
- UV degrades tires, rollers, and bunk carpet
- Debris and bird droppings stain galvanized and painted surfaces
- Trailer bearings and hubs are exposed to moisture that causes rust
- A covered trailer looks more professional and lasts significantly longer

## What to Look For

### Material

- **600D Oxford polyester** with PU coating — the standard for jet ski trailer covers
- **Marine-grade canvas** — heavier, more durable, best for year-round outdoor storage
- **PVC-backed materials** — fully waterproof but check breathability for humidity

### Design Features

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Elastic hem | Keeps the cover tight at highway speeds |
| Grommets | Allow tie-down straps for high-wind security |
| Ventilation | Prevents condensation from forming under the cover |
| Cutouts/straps for bunks | Fits around the trailer frame without flapping |
| Storage bag | Makes covering/uncovering quick and easy |

### Size

Jet ski trailers come in single, double, and tri-axle configurations. Measure the trailer's length and width at the widest point — the fenders usually. Order a cover with at least a few inches of give, never an exact match.

## How to Use a Jet Ski Trailer Cover

1. **Clean the trailer first** — dirt trapped under the cover scratches paint
2. **Secure all tie-downs** — loose fabric flaps and abrades the frame
3. **Tighten the elastic hem** so the cover doesn't balloon at speed
4. **Check after the first drive** — straps settle and need re-tensioning

## Trailer Cover vs. Boat Cover

A jet ski trailer cover is not interchangeable with a ski cover:

- **Ski cover** — protects fiberglass, soft interior, and the seat
- **Trailer cover** — covers frame, tires, rollers, and wiring

Owners who trailer long distances need both. The trailer cover also protects the bunks — wet bunks transfer moisture to the hull all season.

## Maintenance

- Wash with mild soap and water; never machine wash
- Re-apply water-repellent treatment seasonally
- Inspect seams and elastic after each season
- Store dry — a damp cover mildews and rots
