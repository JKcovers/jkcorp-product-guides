# Kayak Cover Guide — Storage and Protection for Your Kayak

Kayaks are expensive, and UV light is their worst enemy. Polyethylene hulls fade and weaken, while dust and grit scratch the surface during storage. A kayak cover protects the hull during transport, storage and off-season months.

## Why Your Kayak Needs a Cover

- **UV protection** — the sun's rays fade and degrade polyethylene and fiberglass
- **Scratch prevention** — covers block dust, grit and accidental knocks
- **Bird and debris defense** — no more cleaning droppings off the deck
- **Trailer protection** — covers reduce wind noise and road grime during transport

## Types of Kayak Covers

| Cover type | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Full cockpit cover | Protects the cockpit and deck from sun and rain |
| Hull / full-length cover | Covers the entire kayak for long-term storage |
| Storage bag | Soft-sided protection for indoor stacking |
| Trailer cover | Multi-kayak cover for open trailers |

## Sizing Your Kayak Cover

Measure your kayak's **overall length** (e.g., 10 ft, 12 ft, 14 ft) and the **beam width** at the widest point. Full-length covers are sold by length class. Cockpit covers need the **cockpit rim dimensions** — measure the length and width of the opening. Add a few inches for a comfortable fit over hatches and fittings.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — rugged all-season choice for outdoor storage
- **UV-stabilized fabric** — essential for sunny regions
- **Elastic hem or shock cord** — holds the cover tight against wind
- **Ventilation** — reduces trapped moisture and mildew on the hull
- **Straps or buckles** — keep the cover on during trailer transport

## Storage Tips

1. Rinse the kayak with fresh water and dry it before covering
2. Loosen straps slightly for indoor storage to avoid pressure marks
3. Store the kayak off the ground on racks — covers protect, racks support
4. Clean the cover with mild soap and water each season

## Key Takeaways

- Match full-length covers to overall kayak length and beam width
- 600D Oxford with straps suits outdoor and trailer use
- Always store a clean, dry kayak
- A covered kayak keeps its color and hull strength for years
