# Fleet Car Covers — Why Businesses Should Buy in Bulk

If your business operates a fleet of vehicles — rental cars, delivery vans, service trucks, or executive sedans — fleet car covers are one of the highest-ROI investments you can make. Here's the business case.

## The Cost of Not Covering

Fleet vehicles parked outdoors depreciate faster than covered vehicles in three measurable ways:

**Paint degradation:** A vehicle's exterior condition directly affects resale value. Fleet vehicles sold at auction with faded or oxidized paint typically sell for 8-15% less than comparable vehicles with good paint. On a $25,000 vehicle, that's $2,000-3,750 lost at resale.

**Cleaning costs:** Uncovered fleet vehicles need washing 2-3x more often. At $15-25 per wash, a 50-vehicle fleet spends $1,500-3,750/month on exterior cleaning. Covers cut that by half or more.

**Brand perception:** A fleet of clean, well-maintained vehicles reflects positively on your business. Dirty, faded vehicles parked at your facility tell customers you cut corners.

## Fleet Cover Features

**Bulk pricing:** OEM manufacturers offer significant discounts for fleet orders. Per-unit costs can drop 30-50% for orders of 100+ units.

**Custom branding:** Fleet covers with your company logo turn parked vehicles into 24/7 billboards. Cost per impression is near zero.

**Durability:** Fleet covers must withstand frequent use — on and off multiple times per day. Heavy-duty multi-layer Oxford with reinforced seams is the minimum standard.

**Quick-release design:** Fleet drivers won't use covers that are a hassle. Look for designs with single-person installation under 2 minutes.

## Implementation Tips

1. Start with a pilot program: Order covers for 10 vehicles and track cleaning cost savings and driver feedback for 3 months.
2. Assign covers to specific vehicles by VIN to track lifespan.
3. Train drivers on proper installation and removal (the accordion fold method).
4. Schedule quarterly cover inspections and cleaning.
5. Replace covers showing wear before they damage paint.
