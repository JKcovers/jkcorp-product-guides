# Convertible Car Cover With Roll Bars — Fit Considerations

Convertibles with integrated or aftermarket roll bars need special cover attention. The roll bar structure sits above the beltline and changes the car's profile — a cover that ignores it will fit badly and rub.

## Why Roll Bars Complicate Covers

Roll bars rise 6-12 inches above the seat beltline. A standard convertible cover, sized for the smooth contour, will:
- Pull tight over the bar, stressing seams
- Leave the bar exposed to UV (the most visible part of the car)
- Flap against the bar and abrade both cover and bar finish

## Cover Options

**Option 1 — Roll bar cutouts:** A custom cover with shaped openings that fit over the bar. The bar is covered by its own small boot or pad. This is the cleanest solution for fixed roll bars.

**Option 2 — Full-height cover:** A cover sized to include the roll bar height, draping over the bar entirely. Simpler, but the extra fabric over the bar can flap.

**Option 3 — Bar pads + standard cover:** Cover the bar with a padded roll bar protector, then use a standard cover. The pad takes the abrasion.

## Measuring for a Roll Bar Cover

| Measurement | How |
|-------------|-----|
| Bar height above beltline | Ground to bar top, minus ground to beltline |
| Bar width | Side to side at the widest point |
| Bar angle | Fixed vs. hooped (angled) bars differ |

## Features That Help

- **Reinforced bar sections:** Extra fabric or padding where the cover crosses the bar
- **Adjustable straps over the bar:** Prevents the cover lifting off the bar area
- **Soft inner lining:** Protects the bar's finish from abrasion

## The Warning

Never use a cover that sits ON the roll bar without reinforcement — the bar will rub through the fabric within months, and the fabric can scratch the bar's finish.
