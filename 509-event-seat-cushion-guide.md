# Stadium Cushion for Outdoor Events and Festivals

Festival seating is a different problem from stadium seating. A stadium cushion is designed for a known bench with a fixed profile; an event cushion has to work on grass, gravel, a hay bale, a temporary grandstand or the ground itself, for a spectator who will carry it in a bag for a whole day.

That changes the specification, and it changes which features earn their cost.

## The surface is unknown, so the base must be assumed wet

At an outdoor event the cushion spends the day on damp ground. The single most important specification is the base: it should be a coated or laminated synthetic that does not wick moisture up into the seating surface.

A cushion with an absorbent base will be cold and damp by the second set, and once an open-cell foam core is saturated it stays heavy. Closed-cell foam plus a water-resistant base is the combination that keeps a cushion usable across a multi-day event.

## Portability drives every other decision

At events, the cushion competes with everything else the spectator is carrying. Three features decide whether it gets used:

- **Packed size** — a cushion that rolls or folds small enough to attach to a bag travels; a rigid one is left at home.
- **Weight** — closed-cell foam at a moderate thickness gives the best support per gram for this use.
- **A carry point** — a handle, a strap or a loop that lets it hang from a bag rather than be held.

An integrated handle costs very little to add and is consistently one of the most valued features in this channel.

## Attachment when there is no bench

Stadium cushions rely on a sleeve or loops that grip a plank. At an event there may be no plank at all, so attachment has to work both ways:

- For temporary grandstands, elastic loops or a sleeve still apply.
- For ground seating, the cushion needs a base that resists damp without slipping and does not require fixing.
- A hook-and-loop strap that stows flat inside the cushion covers both cases without adding bulk.

Specifying an attachment that only works on a bench limits the product to half its potential market.

## Durability across a season

Event cushions are used intensively for a short period, then stored. That pattern favours:

- abrasion-resistant base fabric, because ground contact is constant
- strengthened stitching at the handle and any strap anchor
- print that survives folding and being sat on, not just handling
- a covering that can be wiped clean, since event use includes food and drink

## Branding for sponsors and organisers

Event cushions are frequently sponsor-funded and given away rather than sold, which shifts the priority towards a large, legible print area at low unit cost. Large solid-colour artwork prints economically; fine photographic detail costs more and reads less well at the distance a sponsor cares about.

Confirm the print method against the artwork before quoting, because the method choice can change the unit cost more than the cushion specification does.
