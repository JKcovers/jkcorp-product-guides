# Cover Cleaning Mistakes — Top 10 That Shorten Cover Life

Cleaning a car cover seems simple — until a wrong wash strips the waterproofing, shrinks the fabric, or embrittles the coating. These 10 cover cleaning mistakes are the most common ways owners shorten their covers' lives while trying to care for them.

## Mistake 1: Using Hot Water

Hot water shrinks polyester and damages coatings. What fits today may no longer fit after a hot wash.

**Fix:** always wash cold — 30°C or below.

## Mistake 2: Machine Drying

Dryer heat is the fastest way to destroy a cover: it shrinks fabric, melts coatings, and breaks elastic.

**Fix:** air dry only, ideally in the shade. Never use a dryer.

## Mistake 3: Using Fabric Softener

Softener coats the fabric, blocks breathability, and reduces water repellency. The cover stops working as designed.

**Fix:** skip softener entirely — a gentle detergent is enough.

## Mistake 4: Using Bleach or Harsh Chemicals

Bleach eats fabric, fades color, and destroys waterproof coatings. Chemical cleaners can also leave residues that damage paint.

**Fix:** use a mild, neutral detergent formulated for technical fabrics.

## Mistake 5: Scrubbing With Brushes

Hard scrubbing grinds dirt into the weave and abrades the coating. Aggressive brushing does more harm than the dirt itself.

**Fix:** rinse first, then use a soft sponge or cloth with gentle pressure.

## Mistake 6: Washing Too Often

Every wash wears the fabric and strips treatments. Over-washing shortens life faster than dirt does.

**Fix:** wash only when visibly dirty; between washes, shake out and spot-clean.

## Mistake 7: Washing With Other Items

Zippers, buckles, and hooks from other laundry snag and tear the cover fabric.

**Fix:** wash the cover alone, with straps fastened and zippers closed.

## Mistake 8: Ignoring the Care Label

Every fabric has specific washing rules. Following generic advice instead of the label voids warranties and damages fabric.

**Fix:** read the label first — follow its temperature, detergent, and drying instructions.

## Mistake 9: Not Rinsing Thoroughly

Detergent residue attracts dirt, reduces repellency, and can yellow the fabric. A quick rinse cycle is not enough.

**Fix:** run an extra rinse cycle to remove all detergent.

## Mistake 10: Re-Treating While Wet or Dirty

Applying waterproofing spray to a dirty or damp cover locks in dirt and fails to bond to the fabric.

**Fix:** clean and dry the cover thoroughly, then apply the treatment evenly.

## The Golden Rule

**Cold water, gentle detergent, air dry, no softener, no heat.** Clean covers protect better and last years longer.
