# Cover Wholesale Pricing Strategy — Understand the Numbers Before You Buy

Wholesale cover pricing looks like a single number, but it is really a stack: fabric cost, labor, packaging, overhead, and margin — each one negotiable. Buyers who understand the stack negotiate better and avoid paying retail markups in disguise. Here is the pricing strategy guide.

## What Makes Up a Cover's Wholesale Price

| Component | Share (typical) | Notes |
|-----------|:--:|-------|
| Fabric | 30-50% | The biggest cost driver |
| Labor (cutting/sewing) | 20-35% | Depends on factory location |
| Accessories (zippers, straps) | 5-10% | Quality tiers exist |
| Packaging | 3-8% | Polybag vs printed box |
| Factory overhead + margin | 15-25% | Includes QC, admin, profit |

## Pricing Tiers by Quantity

Standard structure for covers:

| Quantity | Price Level |
|----------|-------------|
| 50-200 pcs | Base tier + 10-20% |
| 300-1,000 pcs | Standard wholesale |
| 2,000-5,000 pcs | Volume discount (-5-15%) |
| 5,000+ pcs | Program pricing, custom negotiation |

Ask for the full tier table — some factories quote a single price that hides better tiers.

## FOB vs Landed Cost

Wholesale quotes are usually FOB. Your real cost per piece:

> Landed cost = FOB + freight per piece + insurance + duty/tariff + port fees + last-mile

A $12 FOB cover can easily land at $18-22. Plan margins on landed cost, not FOB.

## How to Compare Quotes Honestly

- Normalize specs: same fabric weight, same accessories, same packaging
- Ask for the fabric spec in writing — the lowest quote often means thinner fabric
- Compare sample quality, not just price
- Calculate cost per piece including freight for each supplier
- Check MOQ and lead time — a low price with a 9-week lead time may cost you a season

## Negotiation Levers

- **Volume commitment:** multi-SKU programs get better rates than single orders
- **Payment terms:** T/T deposit with balance on shipment is standard; larger advance payments can unlock discounts
- **Packaging simplification:** stock polybags save 2-5%
- **Off-peak production:** slow seasons bring better pricing
- **Annual contracts:** lock prices for 6-12 months

## Pricing Red Flags

- Price 40%+ below market — fabric quality and coating are the hidden cuts
- No fabric spec on the quote
- Price that changes after sample approval
- Freight quoted by a supplier who won't share carton dimensions

## Margin Planning for Resellers

Typical cover resale margins:

- Retail: 100-150% over landed cost
- Amazon FBA: 50-80% after fees and shipping
- Wholesale to distributors: 25-40%
