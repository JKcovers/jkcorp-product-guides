# Roof Rack Covers — Protect Your Cargo From Highway Conditions

Roof rack cargo faces the harshest conditions on any vehicle: 70 mph wind, rain at highway speed, UV exposure, and airborne debris. A roof rack cover is essential for anyone hauling gear up top.

## Why Cover Roof Cargo

- **Weather:** Rain at highway speed finds every seam; snow accumulates; UV degrades fabric cargo
- **Aerodynamics:** A flapping bag or box costs fuel and creates wind noise
- **Security:** Covered cargo is anonymous and less likely to be targeted
- **Protection:** Prevents abrasion between cargo and the rack

## Types of Roof Covers

**Soft cargo carriers (bags):** Flexible waterproof bags that strap to the rack. Best value, easy to store when not in use.

**Hard cargo boxes:** Rigid shells. Superior weatherproofing and security. Expensive.

**Cargo covers (tarps):** Simple waterproof sheets over loose gear — minimal protection, cheap.

## Soft Carrier Features

- **Waterproof with sealed seams:** The #1 feature
- **UV-resistant fabric:** Prolongs bag and cargo life
- **Anti-flap straps:** Cinch the bag tight against the rack
- **Quick-release mounting:** Easy to install/remove
- **Lockable zippers:** Basic theft deterrence

## Installation Tips

1. Clean the rack rails (dirt causes abrasion)
2. Position the bag within the rack's weight capacity
3. Cinch straps tight — a loose bag flaps at speed
4. Check straps at every fuel stop on long trips

## The Weight Warning

Roof loading raises the vehicle's center of gravity. Stay within the rack's rated capacity and the vehicle's roof-load limit — typically 100-165 lbs for passenger vehicles.
