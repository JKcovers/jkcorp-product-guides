# Motorcycle Cover Sizing Made Easy — Never Order the Wrong Size

Most motorcycle cover returns happen for one reason: wrong size. Here's how to get it right the first time.

## Step 1: Measure Your Bike

You need two numbers: overall length (front tire to rear fender) and overall height (ground to top of windshield or fairing).

## Step 2: Check for Obstructions

Do you have a top case, saddlebags, tall windshield, or luggage rack? Each adds inches to your effective size. A bike that measures 92 inches without accessories might need a 100+ inch cover with the top case on.

## Step 3: Choose Your Size

| Cover Size | Length Range | Typical Bikes |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| Medium | Up to 90 in | Sport bikes, naked bikes, small cruisers |
| Large | 90-100 in | Full-size cruisers, adventure bikes |
| X-Large | 100-110 in | Touring bikes, full dressers |
| Scooter | 70-80 in | Vespas, maxi-scooters, mopeds |

## The Golden Rule

When in doubt, size up. A slightly long cover can be cinched with the belly strap. A too-short cover leaves your front tire, fork, and headlight exposed.
