# Canoe Cover Guide — Storage and Trailer Protection for Your Canoe

Canoes are built tough, but years of UV exposure, rain and road debris take their toll. A canoe cover protects the hull, gunwales and interior from sun, dust and weather — whether the canoe lives on a trailer, in the yard or in the garage.

## Why Your Canoe Needs a Cover

- **UV protection** — sun fades and embrittles the hull material over time
- **Interior protection** — keeps rain, leaves and insects out of the cockpit
- **Scratch prevention** — blocks dust and grit from grinding into the hull
- **Trailer safety** — a cover keeps the canoe secure and road-clean during transport
- **Less maintenance** — no scrubbing bird droppings or tree sap off the hull

## Types of Canoe Covers

| Cover type | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Full-length canoe cover | Complete hull protection for storage and trailering |
| Cockpit / interior cover | Keeps rain and debris out of the interior |
| Storage bag | Soft protection for indoor or rack storage |
| Trailer cover | Covers multiple canoes on an open trailer |

## Sizing Your Canoe Cover

Measure the **overall length** (e.g., 12 ft, 14 ft, 16 ft) and the **maximum width** at the center — most canoes are 34-38 in wide. Full-length covers are sold by length class and should fit the beam width comfortably. For cockpit covers, measure the length and width of the opening at the gunwales.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — rugged and waterproof for outdoor storage
- **UV-stabilized** — essential for canoes stored in direct sun
- **Elastic hem or shock cord** — keeps the cover taut in wind
- **Straps and buckles** — secure the cover during trailer travel
- **Ventilation** — reduces trapped condensation and mildew

## Storage Best Practices

1. Rinse the canoe with fresh water and dry it before covering
2. Store on sawhorses or racks — never directly on the ground
3. Loosen straps for long-term indoor storage
4. Clean the cover with mild soap and water each season

## Key Takeaways

- Match full-length covers to canoe length and beam width
- 600D Oxford with straps suits outdoor and trailer use
- Store canoes on racks under a cover for best protection
- A covered canoe keeps its color and strength for decades
