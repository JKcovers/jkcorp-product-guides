# Leather Seat Covers — Luxury Look Without the Luxury Price

Genuine leather seats cost $1,500-3,000 from the factory. Leather seat covers deliver the look for $100-300. Here's what to know before upgrading.

## Leather vs Leatherette (Faux Leather)

**Genuine leather:** Natural material, breathes, develops a beautiful patina, ages well. Expensive and requires conditioning.

**Leatherette (PU leather):** Polyurethane-coated fabric that mimics leather. Cheaper, easier to clean, more consistent color. The majority of "leather" seat covers on the market are leatherette — and for good reason.

For seat covers, **high-quality leatherette is the practical choice** for most owners: it looks premium, costs less, and resists cracking better than cheap genuine leather.

## The Fit Factor

Leather seat covers MUST fit well. A loose leather cover looks terrible and wears quickly at stress points. Choose custom-fit or semi-custom — universal leather covers are almost always disappointing.

## Airbag Compatibility — Critical

A leather cover that blocks the side airbag is a safety hazard. Verify the cover has airbag cutouts and doesn't interfere with deployment. This is non-negotiable for any seat cover, but especially rigid leather.

## Care for Leather Covers

- Wipe with a damp cloth — avoid soaking
- Use leather conditioner 2-3 times per year
- Keep away from direct sun (UV cracks leather)
- Never use ammonia-based cleaners

## The Verdict

Leather covers transform a basic interior for a fraction of factory leather. Choose quality leatherette, custom fit, and airbag-safe construction.
