# Muscle Car Cover Guide — Protect the Legend

Muscle cars are investments — and like any investment, they need protection. A dedicated muscle car cover addresses the specific risks these high-value classics face.

## The Muscle Car Protection Needs

Muscle cars typically:
- Sit for long periods between drives (weekends, shows, winters)
- Have large flat hoods and decks that collect dust and UV
- Live in garages, but travel to shows
- Carry big value — paint condition is worth thousands

## Cover Priorities for Muscle Cars

**Indoor protection is #1:** Most muscle cars live in garages. The cover's job is dust protection, scratch prevention, and humidity control — not weather armor.

**Fleece-lined softness:** Muscle car paint is often custom or show-grade. Only soft inner linings (fleece, microfiber) touch that paint.

**Show-friendly:** The cover must install/remove without leaving marks — critical for show cars.

## Indoor vs Outdoor Muscle Car Covers

| Use | Cover Type |
|-----|-----------|
| Garage storage | Fleece-lined dust cover |
| Outdoor parking | Multi-layer waterproof |
| Car show transport | Travel cover + show cover |

## The Long-Storage Problem

Muscle cars sit for months in winter. A breathable fleece-lined cover is essential — non-breathable covers trap condensation that causes the exact rust and mildew classic owners fear.

## The Resale Math

A show-grade paint job costs $5,000-15,000. A $60-100 cover that prevents one swirl mark, one bird dropping etch, or one season of UV fade pays for itself hundreds of times over.
