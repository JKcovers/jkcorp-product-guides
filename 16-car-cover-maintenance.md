# How to Clean and Maintain Your Car Cover — Extend Its Life to 3+ Years

A quality car cover is an investment. With proper care, a multi-layer Oxford cover can last 3-5 years outdoors. Without it, even premium covers degrade in 12-18 months. Here's how to maintain yours.

## Cleaning Frequency

Outdoor covers: Wash every 2-3 months. Wind-blown dirt, bird droppings, and tree sap accumulate on the outer surface and can eventually bleed through.

Indoor covers: Wash every 6 months. Indoor dust is less aggressive but still builds up over time.

## How to Wash a Car Cover

Most multi-layer covers are machine-washable. Follow these steps:

1. Remove the cover and shake it out thoroughly to dislodge loose dirt and debris
2. Place in a large-capacity front-loading washer (top-loaders with agitators can tear the fabric)
3. Use cold water and mild detergent — no bleach, no fabric softener
4. Gentle cycle only
5. Air dry — never use a dryer. Heat damages waterproof coatings and shrinks elastic hems
6. Do not wring or twist the cover — hang it over a railing or clothesline

## Storage When Not in Use

Most covers come with a storage bag. Always store the cover in its bag when not on the car. A loose cover on the garage floor collects dirt, gets stepped on, and attracts pests.

If the cover is wet when you remove it (morning dew, rain), drape it over a railing or across two chairs to dry before folding and storing. Storing a wet cover leads to mildew and odor that's nearly impossible to remove.

## Signs Your Cover Needs Replacing

- Water no longer beads on the surface (waterproof coating has degraded)
- Fabric feels thin or brittle in spots (UV damage)
- Elastic hem has lost stretch (cover flaps more in wind)
- Persistent musty smell even after washing (mildew in the inner layers)
- Tears or holes larger than a coin (can't be patched reliably for outdoor use)

## Protection Tips

- Never drag the cover across the ground when putting it on or taking it off — grit picked up by the fabric becomes sandpaper on your paint
- Fold the cover accordion-style from front to back for easier solo installation
- Use the mirror pockets as grip points when positioning the cover
- If birds frequently target your car, rinse droppings off the cover with a hose between washes
