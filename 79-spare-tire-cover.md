# Spare Tire Covers — Protect Your Wheel and Show Your Style

Spare tire covers do double duty: they protect the mounted spare from UV, weather, and theft — and they've become a style statement for SUV and off-road owners.

## Why Protect the Spare

A spare tire mounted on the rear of an SUV or Jeep faces constant sun exposure. UV degrades the rubber, causing cracking and dry rot that can make the spare unusable when you need it most. A cover blocks 99% of UV and extends tire life significantly.

## Materials

**Vinyl (standard):** The classic spare cover material. Waterproof, easy to clean, and affordable. Available in a huge range of designs — from blank black to embroidered logos and custom prints.

**Oxford fabric:** Softer, more breathable, less prone to heat buildup. Better for climates with extreme heat. Often used for covers with pockets or padding.

**Canvas:** Heavyweight and durable, popular for premium/retro looks.

## Sizing

Spare covers are sized by tire diameter. To find yours: tire width (e.g., 265mm) ÷ 25.4 = inches (10.4), × aspect ratio (e.g., 0.75) × 2 = wheel height contribution (15.6), + wheel diameter (16) = total diameter (~32 inches). Or simply check the tire sidewall markings — most covers list compatible tire sizes.

| Cover Size | Tire Diameter |
|------------|---------------|
| Small | Up to 30 inches |
| Medium | 30-33 inches |
| Large | 33-36 inches |
