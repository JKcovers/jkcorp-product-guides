# Multi-Category Cover Sourcing — Why One OEM Factory Beats Multiple Suppliers

If your business sells car covers, motorcycle covers, boat covers, and furniture covers, you have two options: multiple suppliers or one multi-category OEM factory. The single-supplier approach wins on cost, quality, and logistics.

## The Multi-Supplier Problem

Four different suppliers means four separate orders, four shipments, four sets of customs paperwork, and four quality standards to manage. A $500 order from each supplier becomes a logistics nightmare with four separate freight costs.

## The Single-Factory Advantage

One ISO 9001 certified manufacturer that produces all four cover types means: one order, one shipment, one quality standard, one point of contact for issues. The manufacturer is incentivized to maintain consistent quality across all your products because you're a larger, more valuable customer.

## Minimum Order Flexibility

Multi-category OEM factories often allow split MOQs. Instead of 100 car covers and 100 motorcycle covers from separate suppliers (200 total), you might get 60 car covers, 40 motorcycle covers, 30 boat covers, and 20 furniture covers from one factory — meeting the 150 total MOQ threshold with better category diversity.

## Quality Consistency

A single factory uses the same sewing standards, fabric sourcing, and quality inspection process across all product lines. Different factories mean different quality philosophies.
