# Seasonal Tire Storage Covers — Protect Your Second Set

Seasonal tire changes are routine for millions of drivers, but the tires that come off the car are rarely stored properly. Unprotected tires stored in a garage, basement, or shed crack, dry out, and deform — losing years of usable life. Seasonal tire storage covers solve this cheaply. Here is the guide.

## Why Tires Need Covers

- **UV damage** — sunlight degrades rubber, causing surface cracking (weather checking)
- **Ozone attack** — ozone in the air attacks rubber even in the dark; covers add a barrier
- **Dust and dirt** — embedded grit grinds into the tread and sidewall
- **Moisture** — damp storage promotes sidewall deterioration
- **Pests** — mice nest in tire stacks; a sealed cover deters them

## Types of Tire Storage Covers

### 1. Individual Tire Bags

- Simple drawstring or zipper bags, one per tire
- Best for stack storage — each tire protected individually
- Usually heavy-duty PE or oxford fabric with a waterproof coating

### 2. Wheel and Tire Bags

For tires still mounted on wheels:

- Larger bags that fit wheel + tire
- Often with handles for lifting
- Protect alloy wheels from scratches during handling and storage

### 3. Full Stack Covers

- One large cover that wraps a stack of 4 tires
- Convenient, but tires inside the stack can still rub each other
- Best combined with individual bags

## Storage Conditions Matter More Than the Cover

| Condition | Ideal | Acceptable | Bad |
|-----------|-------|------------|-----|
| Temperature | 10-25°C | 5-30°C | Below 0°C or above 35°C |
| Light | Dark | Low light | Direct sun |
| Humidity | 40-60% | 30-70% | Damp basements |
| Surface | Clean floor | Off the ground | Concrete (cold, damp) |

### Rules That Save Tires

1. **Store off the concrete floor** — cold concrete sucks heat and moisture; use boards or pallets
2. **Keep tires upright if possible** — long-term stacked storage deforms the bottom tire
3. **Reduce pressure slightly** for unmounted tires (10-15 psi) to reduce stress
4. **Clean tires before bagging** — dirt and oils trapped in a bag accelerate rubber degradation
5. **Never store near sources of ozone** — electric motors, generators, and welding equipment generate ozone

## Choosing a Tire Storage Cover

- **Waterproof with a breathable option** — a fully sealed bag in a damp basement traps moisture
- **Reinforced seams** — bags take handling stress when moved
- **Drawstring or zipper closure** — easy access during seasonal changeover
- **Size labeling** — mark bags by position (FL, FR, RL, RR) so spring changeover is quick

## The Seasonal Routine

1. At changeover, clean tires and check for damage
2. Bag each tire, marking its position
3. Store on a rack or pallet, off the concrete
4. Note the date on the bag — track how many seasons each set has served
5. At the next changeover, inspect for cracking before mounting
