# Outdoor Generator Cover — Weather Protection for Your Power Source

Generators are the most expensive tool most homeowners leave outdoors — and the one with the most weather-sensitive parts. A generator cover protects the control panel, alternator, and fuel system from rain, snow, and sun. This guide covers sizing, materials, and use.

## Why a Generator Needs a Cover

- **Control panels** — water intrusion damages displays, breakers, and outlets
- **Alternator and cooling fins** — dust and grit clog airflow and cause overheating
- **Paint and frame** — rust starts where rain pools on the frame
- **Fuel components** — UV degrades fuel lines and gaskets

## Sizing a Generator Cover

Measure the generator at its widest points:

1. **Width** across the frame
2. **Depth** front to back, including handles
3. **Height** at the highest point (usually the fuel cap or control panel)

Choose a cover slightly larger than these numbers — generators need airflow, and a tight cover traps heat. Never cover a running generator; covers are for storage only.

## Material and Ventilation

Choose a weatherproof fabric with:

- **Top ventilation** — heat and moisture escape while the cover is on
- **Waterproof coating** — rain beads off and never pools on the control panel
- **Side access panels** — some covers open at the front for quick starts without full removal

## Safe Usage Rules

- Let the generator cool completely before covering — hot metal under a cover is a fire risk
- Never run a generator under its cover; exhaust and heat need open air
- Check the cover's straps so wind cannot lift it into the muffler area
- In winter, remove snow from the cover so the weight does not crush the frame vents

## Common Questions

**Can I run the generator with the cover on?** Never. Covers are not rated for operating heat or exhaust. Run the generator uncovered, let it cool, then cover it.

**Do covers prevent rust?** They slow it dramatically by keeping rain and dew off the frame. For full rust prevention, also keep the generator off the ground and away from sprinklers.

**Will a cover fit over wheels and handles?** Most generator covers are designed to fit over the full frame including wheels. Measure the widest point — some models with folding handles need a size up.

**How do I keep the cover on in high wind?** Choose a cover with a drawstring or elastic hem and secure the straps to the frame's lower corners. In extreme wind, add a bungee across the top.

## The Golden Rule

**Cover cool, cover clean, and never cover a running generator.** The right cover protects the generator — the right habits protect the cover.
