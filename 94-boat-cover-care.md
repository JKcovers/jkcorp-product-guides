# Boat Cover Cleaning and Care — Extend the Life to 8+ Years

A marine-grade boat cover is a $300-800 investment. With proper care it lasts 5-8 years. Neglected, it fails in 2-3. Here's the complete care routine.

## The Salt Problem

Salt residue is the #1 boat cover killer. It abrades fibers, attracts moisture, and accelerates mildew. In coastal environments, rinse the cover with fresh water after every use — before salt crystals dry into the fabric.

## Cleaning Frequency

| Environment | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Coastal (salt air) | Rinse after each use, wash monthly |
| Freshwater lakes | Wash every 2-3 months |
| Covered storage | Wash twice per season |

## Washing Steps

1. Rinse with fresh water to remove salt and loose dirt
2. Use a marine fabric cleaner or mild detergent (never bleach)
3. Soft-bristle brush for stubborn stains (never abrasive scrubbers)
4. Rinse thoroughly — soap residue attracts dirt
5. Air dry completely before storing

## Waterproofing Reapplication

Marine fabric coatings wear over time. Reapply fabric waterproofing treatment every 1-2 seasons:

- Clean the cover thoroughly first
- Apply the treatment evenly with a spray bottle
- Let it cure per the product instructions (usually 24-48 hours)

## Seasonal Storage

When the boat goes into storage:
1. Wash and dry the cover completely
2. Repair any small tears with fabric patch tape
3. Fold loosely (tight folds stress the fabric)
4. Store in a dry, ventilated area — never a plastic bag

## Signs of Damage

- Water soaking through (coating failure) — rewaterproof
- Frayed seams — repair with marine-grade thread
- Mildew spots — treat with marine cleaner
- Stiff, brittle fabric — replace; the material has fatigued
