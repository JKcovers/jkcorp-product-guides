# Firewood Rack Cover Guide — Keep Firewood Dry and Ready to Burn

Wet firewood won't light, smokes heavily and creosotes up your chimney. A firewood rack cover is the difference between wood that burns clean all winter and a pile of damp logs. Here is how to choose and use one properly.

## Why Your Firewood Needs a Cover

- **Dry wood** — a cover keeps rain and snow off the top of the stack
- **Cleaner burning** — seasoned wood produces less smoke and creosote
- **Less rot and mold** — covered wood stays healthier for the fireplace
- **Debris control** — leaves and bugs stay off the pile
- **Longer seasoning** — wood keeps its moisture content low

## Firewood Cover Styles

| Style | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| Traditional tarp with straps | Budget, any rack size |
| Fitted rack cover | Neat look, easy on/off, protects the top of the stack |
| Log carrier bag | Short trips from the rack to the fireplace |

## Sizing a Firewood Rack Cover

Measure the **length and width of your firewood rack**, and the **stack height** — most racks hold 2-4 ft high stacks. Covers are sized by rack length (e.g., 4 ft, 8 ft) and should overhang the stack slightly so rain runs off the cover, not into the wood. A cover that's too small leaves the top row exposed.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — heavy-duty and waterproof
- **PVC-backed fabric** — extra waterproofing for exposed locations
- **Elastic hem or straps** — keeps the cover anchored in wind
- **Reinforced grommets** — for tying the cover down securely
- **UV-stabilized** — resists sun damage over years outside

## Proper Firewood Storage

1. Store wood off the ground on a rack — airflow from below matters
2. Cover only the top; leave the sides open for ventilation
3. Face the open side of the cover toward the prevailing wind direction
4. In heavy snow, brush off accumulation to protect the cover and frame

## Key Takeaways

- Measure rack length, width and stack height before buying
- Cover the top only — sides must stay open for airflow
- 600D Oxford with straps suits year-round outdoor use
- Dry, seasoned wood burns hotter and cleaner
