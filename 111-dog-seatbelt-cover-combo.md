# Dog Seat Belt and Cover Combo — The Right Way to Secure Your Dog

A pet seat cover protects your car. A dog seatbelt protects your dog. Together they're the safest setup for dogs who travel loose. Here's how to combine them correctly.

## Why a Seatbelt Is Critical

In a 30 mph crash, an unrestrained 50-lb dog becomes a 1,500-lb projectile. Even without a crash, sudden braking sends dogs flying. A seatbelt-style harness keeps the dog in place — and prevents them from jumping into the front seat.

## The Correct Setup

**Step 1:** Install the seat cover with full anchoring (headrests + seatback straps)

**Step 2:** Have the dog wear a crash-tested harness (NOT a collar — collars can strangle the dog on impact)

**Step 3:** Clip the seatbelt harness tether to the harness's back D-ring and the vehicle's seat belt anchor (or the cover's anchor point if it has one)

**Step 4:** Adjust the tether length so the dog can sit and lie down, but can't reach the front seats or doors

## Common Mistakes

- **Clipping to the collar:** Strangulation risk
- **Using a non-tested tether:** Cheap tethers break on impact
- **Tether too long:** Dog reaches front seat
- **No cover underneath:** The tether system doesn't protect the seat from claws

## The Layered System

| Layer | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Crash-tested harness | Restrains the dog safely |
| Seatbelt tether | Connects dog to anchor |
| Anchored seat cover | Protects seats, gives the dog grip |
| Door flaps | Protects panels from paws |
