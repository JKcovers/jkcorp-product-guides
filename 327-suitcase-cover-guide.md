# Suitcase Cover Guide — Keep Your Suitcase Looking New

A suitcase is an investment in travel comfort — and the roughest part of travel is the baggage belt. A suitcase cover protects the shell, corners, and handles from scuffs, scratches, and dirt. This guide covers cover types, sizing, and what to look for.

## What a Suitcase Cover Protects

- **The shell** — hard cases get scratched and scuffed; soft cases get punctured
- **Corners and edges** — the highest-impact zones on every flight
- **Handles and zippers** — the most stressed components, shielded from abrasion
- **Interior contents** — an extra layer of water resistance for the bag itself

## Types of Suitcase Covers

| Type | Best For | Durability |
|---|---|---|
| Stretch spandex | Quick protection, soft cases | 1-3 years |
| Neoprene | Impact and water resistance | 3-5 years |
| PVC-coated nylon | Heavy-duty airline travel | 3-5 years |
| Custom-printed | Branding and easy identification | 2-4 years |

## Sizing a Suitcase Cover

Measure the suitcase standing upright: height, width, and depth. Covers are sold by luggage size (small/cabin 20", medium 24", large 28"). Bulging soft-sided bags need one size up. Check that the cover leaves the telescoping handle free — a cover that binds the handle makes the suitcase awkward to roll.

## Fit and Care Tips

1. Put the cover on with the bag empty or lightly packed — it stretches over the frame.
2. Align the cover's openings with the handles and zippers.
3. Do not force a too-small cover; it will tear at the corner seams.
4. Wash covers by hand in cold water; machine washing breaks down the elastic.

## Common Questions

**Will the cover fit a packed suitcase?** Packed bags expand. If your bag is normally full, size up — a tight cover strains the seams and can tear at the corners on the baggage belt.

**Do covers work with spinner wheels?** Yes, most covers leave the wheels exposed for smooth rolling. Check that the bottom opening matches your wheel configuration.

**Can I personalize a suitcase cover?** Absolutely — printed and custom covers also serve as instant luggage identification at the carousel.

**How long does a cover last?** With normal use, 2-4 years. Replace it when the elastic loses tension or the fabric starts tearing at the handle openings.

## The Golden Rule

**Buy for the loaded size, and protect the handle openings.** A suitcase cover that fits well disappears into the journey — that is exactly what it should do.
