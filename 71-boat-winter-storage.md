# Boat Winter Storage — Shrink Wrap vs Custom Cover

Boat winterization is the biggest decision boat owners face. Shrink wrap is the classic choice; custom fitted covers are increasingly popular. Here's an honest comparison to help you choose.

## Shrink Wrap: The Standard

Heat-shrunk plastic film that encloses the entire boat.

**Pros:**
- Complete enclosure — blocks all weather, UV, and pests
- No structural stress on the boat
- Relatively inexpensive material ($200-400 for a typical runabout)

**Cons:**
- Single-use — must be cut off in spring, new wrap every winter
- Not breathable — moisture problems unless vents are installed correctly
- Requires professional installation for large boats
- Environmental concerns — thousands of tons of shrink wrap go to landfill annually

## Custom Boat Cover: The Reusable Option

A fitted fabric cover designed for your hull.

**Pros:**
- Reusable for 5-7 years — pays for itself in 2-3 winters
- Breathable fabrics prevent mildew
- Repairable
- Environmentally friendlier

**Cons:**
- Higher upfront cost ($300-800)
- Requires proper support structure (poles/frame) so water doesn't pool
- May not cover every accessory without customization

## The Decision Matrix

| Factor | Shrink Wrap | Custom Cover |
|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Upfront cost | ✅ Lower | ❌ Higher |
| 5-year cost | ❌ ~$1,000-2,000 | ✅ ~$400-800 |
| Breathability | ❌ Vents needed | ✅ Fabric breathes |
| Reusability | ❌ Single-use | ✅ 5-7 years |
| Storage space | ❌ Wasted each spring | ✅ Compact |
| DIY feasibility | ⚠️ Hard for large boats | ✅ Easy |

## The Verdict

If you store your boat for 3+ winters, a custom cover is the better investment. For one-off storage or extreme climates where you need a complete seal, shrink wrap still wins.
