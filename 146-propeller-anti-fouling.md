# Marine Propeller Protection — Anti-Fouling and Corrosion Defense

Propeller corrosion and fouling cost boat owners real money. A dedicated propeller cover is the simplest defense between trips.

## The Two Threats

**Fouling:** Marine growth (barnacles, algae) attaches to props sitting in water. Fouled props lose thrust, burn more fuel, and require aggressive cleaning that damages blades.

**Corrosion:** Saltwater accelerates galvanic corrosion on bronze, aluminum, and stainless props — especially when the boat sits unused for weeks.

## How a Cover Helps

A propeller cover blocks both threats at the source:
- Prevents growth by cutting off the attachment surface while docked
- Reduces salt contact during storage
- Protects blades from accidental dock/trailer strikes

## Sizing

Measure propeller diameter (tip to tip) and hub size. Covers typically fit prop sizes from 10 to 20+ inches. For twin-engine setups, order per-prop.

## When to Use

- **Dry storage / trailering:** Always — protects blades from strikes
- **Wet mooring:** Use an anti-fouling-treated cover
- **Off-season storage:** Essential — longest exposure period
