# OEM Motorcycle Cover Customization — Logo, Size, and Material Options

Motorcycle cover OEM manufacturing is a straightforward process once you understand the customization options. Whether you're a brand, a dealer, or a distributor, here's what you can customize.

## The Customization Menu

**Logo printing:**
- Silkscreen: Most durable, best for solid logos
- Embroidery: Premium look for text or simple graphics
- Heat transfer: Best for complex multi-color designs

**Colors:** Most factories offer a standard palette (black, silver, grey, blue, red, orange). Premium factories match custom colors to your brand (Pantone matching available).

**Sizes:** S-XL for standard bikes, plus custom sizing for:
- Tall windshields
- Top cases and luggage
- Fairings and side cases
- Specific models (custom templates)

**Packaging:** Custom-printed poly bags, branded boxes, or folded-with-band + hang tag options.

## The OEM Process

1. **Request samples** (3-5 materials, $50-100 credited to order)
2. **Approve pre-production sample** — the factory produces 1 unit of your exact spec before bulk
3. **Bulk production** — 15-25 days for standard, 25-35 days for fully custom
4. **QC inspection** — seams, straps, heat panels, printing accuracy
5. **Shipping** — sea freight for bulk, air for samples

## MOQ Reality

| Customization | Typical MOQ |
|---------------|:--:|
| Blank cover | 50 pcs |
| Logo printing | 100 pcs |
| Custom color | 100-200 pcs |
| Full custom sizing | 200+ pcs |

## Quality Checklist for OEM Buyers

- ISO 9001 certified factory
- 5+ years on Alibaba, Trade Assurance eligible
- Heat panel spec stated in writing
- Fabric GSM/denier specified in contract
