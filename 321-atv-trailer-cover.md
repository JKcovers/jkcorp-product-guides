# ATV Trailer Cover — Keep Your Hauler and Quad Protected

An ATV trailer works hard — hauling quads through mud, dust, and rain — then sits outside waiting for the next trip. Without protection, the trailer rusts and the quads' plastics fade. An ATV trailer cover solves both problems at once. Here is the guide.

## What an ATV Trailer Cover Protects

- **The trailer bed and rails** — steel scratches and rusts where cargo scrapes it
- **The quads themselves** — UV fades plastics and dry-rots tires while parked
- **Winches and tie-downs** — hardware corrodes in the weather
- **Wiring and lights** — moisture kills trailer lighting fast

## Sizing an ATV Trailer Cover

Measure the trailer with the quads loaded — that is how it will be covered:

1. **Length** including the tongue
2. **Width** over the fenders or rails
3. **Loaded height** from the ground to the top of the tallest quad's handlebars or rack

Order the size that covers the loaded height, not the empty bed height. A cover that only fits the empty trailer will strain over the quads and tear at the seams.

## Key Features

- **Elastic hem** — holds the cover against wind without flapping
- **Vent panels** — let condensation escape from under the cover
- **Reinforced corners** — the cover stresses where the quads' handlebars push upward
- **Tie-down loops** — extra security for high-wind areas

## Maintenance Tips

- Dry the quads before covering — trapped moisture under the cover equals rust and mildew
- Check the cover after hauling; road vibration can shift it loose
- Clean the cover with mild soap seasonally to keep the waterproof coating active

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Sizing to the empty trailer.** The most common error. Measure with the quads loaded — handlebars and racks are the tallest points, and a cover that fits the empty bed will rip over them.

**Covering muddy quads.** Mud and moisture against the cover create rust on the trailer and mildew on the fabric. Pressure-wash the quads and let them dry before covering.

**Skipping the tie-downs.** Trailers vibrate during travel, loosening covers mid-haul. Always secure all loops before driving, and check them at every fuel stop.

**Forgetting vents.** Quads off-gassing fuel vapors and moisture need an escape path. Choose a cover with ventilation panels or leave a strap loose at the rear on dry days.

## The Golden Rule

**Size for the loaded trailer, and always cover dry.** An ATV trailer cover works only when it fits the real shape of the load.
