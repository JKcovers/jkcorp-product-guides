# Patio Table Cover Sizing — Measure Right, Buy Once

The most common patio table cover mistake is buying by "round table" or "umbrella hole" without measuring. The result is a cover that either splits at the seam or pools water on top. This guide walks through exact patio table cover sizing.

## Step 1: Measure the Tabletop

- **Round tables:** measure the diameter at the widest point, edge to edge
- **Rectangular tables:** measure length and width at the widest points
- **Include the base:** if the base is wider than the top, the cover must fit the base

## Step 2: Measure the Height

Run a tape from the floor to the top edge of the table. Covers are sized by diameter/width plus height — a tall bistro table needs a taller cover than a low patio table even with the same top size.

## Step 3: Add Clearance

Add 5-10 cm to every dimension for:
- Fabric shrinkage over time
- Easy installation over a set table
- Room for the elastic hem to sit below the tabletop

## Patio Table Cover Size Chart

| Table Top | Recommended Cover Size |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Up to 60 cm round | 60-70 cm |
| 70-90 cm round | 90-100 cm |
| 100-130 cm round | 130-150 cm |
| 150-180 cm round | 180-200 cm |
| 6-seat rectangle (180x100 cm) | 200x120 cm |

## Umbrella Hole Covers

If your table has an umbrella hole, buy a cover with an umbrella slot or a separate umbrella hole cover. This keeps the table protected while the umbrella stays mounted.

## Fit Tips

- A snug fit with the elastic hem under the tabletop is ideal
- Loose covers should be secured with the built-in straps
- Never buy "one size fits all" — they fit none well

## Glass and Stone Tables

Glass and stone tops are heavier than wood — the cover must be padded or fleece-lined to avoid scratching. Confirm the liner type before purchase.

## Key Takeaways

- Always measure the widest point plus the full height — never guess from the chair count
- Add 5-10 cm of clearance so the cover sits over the tabletop instead of straining
- Use an umbrella slot cover if your table keeps its umbrella mounted
- A snug elastic hem that tucks under the tabletop is the sign of a correct fit
