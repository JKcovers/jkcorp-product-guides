# Container Shipping for Covers — How Many Fit in a Container?

How many covers fit in a container? It is the first question that turns a product decision into a business decision — because freight cost per piece can swing your margin by 10-20%. Here is how container loading works for covers.

## Container Basics

| Container | Internal Volume (usable) | Max Payload |
|-----------|:--:|:--:|
| 20ft (20GP) | ~28 m³ | ~28 t |
| 40ft (40GP) | ~58 m³ | ~26 t |
| 40ft High Cube (40HQ) | ~68 m³ | ~26 t |

Covers are volume-driven, not weight-driven — the container fills by space before weight.

## How Many Covers Fit

Loading depends on packaging. Representative numbers for flat-packed covers in polybags:

| Packaging | 20GP | 40HQ |
|-----------|:--:|:--:|
| Car covers, flat pack | ~3,000-4,000 pcs | ~8,000-10,000 pcs |
| Car covers, vacuum pack | ~5,000-6,000 pcs | ~13,000-16,000 pcs |
| Motorcycle covers, flat | ~8,000-10,000 pcs | ~20,000-25,000 pcs |
| Seat covers (per set) | ~2,500-3,500 sets | ~7,000-9,000 sets |

The range reflects carton efficiency — tight cartons and full carton loads push numbers up.

## The Math That Matters

Freight cost per piece:

> (Freight rate ÷ pieces per container)

Example: $3,800 freight on a 40HQ holding 10,000 covers = $0.38 per piece. The same container at 6,000 covers = $0.63 per piece. **The difference is your packaging design.**

## How to Maximize Pieces Per Container

1. **Vacuum or flat packing** — removes trapped air, the single biggest win
2. **Optimize carton dimensions** — cartons that divide evenly into container width (e.g., 58-60 cm wide) eliminate wasted lanes
3. **Uniform carton sizes** — mixed sizes create dead space
4. **Ask for a loading plan** — good factories provide a computed loading count
5. **Consider CBM-based quotes** — some forwarders charge by volume; smaller cartons pay off

## Weight Checks

A loaded 40HQ of covers typically weighs 6-12 t — well under payload limits. But heavy-duty covers (600D, large boat covers) can reach 15-18 t; always confirm total weight for trucking and destination handling.

## Shipping Configurations

- **Full container (FCL):** cheapest per piece for 2,000+ covers
- **Less than container (LCL):** for 500-1,500 pcs — pay per cubic meter
- **Sea + air:** for urgent small orders

## Getting the Numbers Before You Order

Ask the supplier for:

- Carton dimensions and weight
- Pieces per carton
- Computed loading count for 20GP and 40HQ
- A mock loading plan

This data lets you compare freight scenarios before committing.
