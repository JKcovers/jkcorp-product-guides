# Magnetic Car Covers — Instant Protection Without Straps

Magnetic car covers are the fastest-installing protection on the market. Instead of mirror pockets, straps, and anchors, a row of magnets along the hem grips the steel body panels — cover on in under a minute, off in seconds. Here is what you need to know before buying.

## How Magnetic Covers Work

Neodymium magnets sewn into the hem attach directly to the steel roof, hood, trunk, and doors. The magnetic grip holds the cover in place against wind up to a point — no straps, no mirror pockets, no underbody hooks.

**Important:** the magnets only work on steel panels. Aluminum-bodied cars and fiberglass body panels will not hold a magnetic cover.

## The Big Advantages

### 1. Fastest Installation and Removal

- Install in 30-60 seconds — no adjusting straps
- Remove in 10 seconds — grab the hem and lift
- Perfect for daily use where time matters

### 2. No Risk of Scratching

Straps and buckles can scratch paint when they flap in wind. Magnetic covers have no loose hardware touching the body.

### 3. Clean Fit

No mirror pockets and no anchor straps means a smoother profile — less fabric flapping and better wind behavior.

## The Trade-Offs

| Consideration | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| Wind resistance | Magnets hold well but a strap system holds better in high winds |
| Body material | Steel only — no aluminum or fiberglass panels |
| Magnetic strength | Quality varies — cheap covers use weak magnets that release easily |
| Paint protection | Add a soft backing layer so magnets never touch paint directly |

## Wind Performance

Magnetic covers are tested for everyday wind — parking lots, garages, light storms. In sustained high winds, the fabric can billow and break magnet contact. Premium covers add:

- Stronger magnets at corners (where wind lifts first)
- Reinforced hems that keep magnets aligned
- Optional tie-down loops for storm conditions

## Choosing a Magnetic Cover

1. **Check your body panels** — confirm steel construction (test with a fridge magnet if unsure)
2. **Magnet count and strength** — more magnets and stronger magnets mean better grip
3. **Soft backing** — fabric between magnets and paint prevents scratches
4. **Weather rating** — waterproof fabric for outdoor use
5. **Size** — measure your car; magnetic covers must sit flat to grip evenly

## Care and Maintenance

- Wipe the magnets clean occasionally — dirt reduces grip
- Fold without creasing the magnet rows
- Store flat or loosely rolled; tight rolling stresses the magnet pockets
- Check magnet retention after washing — loose magnets need resewing

## Who Should Buy One

- Daily drivers who cover and uncover often
- Garage users who want a dust cover without install hassle
- Aluminum/fiberglass owners — look elsewhere (straps required)
