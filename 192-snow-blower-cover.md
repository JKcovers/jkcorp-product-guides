# Snow Blower Cover — Winter Protection That Pays Off

A snow blower earns its keep in the worst weather — and then sits idle for nine months. Rain, rust, and mice can wreck it in that idle time. A snow blower cover is one of the smartest off-season purchases a homeowner makes.

## What Happens Without a Cover

- **Rust** forms on the chute, auger, and impeller
- **Belts and cables** corrode and seize
- **Fuel lines** degrade from UV exposure
- **Mice** nest in the engine housing and chew wires
- **Paint** on the housing fades and chips

## Snow Blower Cover Sizing

Measure the blower's total footprint:

| Blower Type | Typical Size |
|-------------|--------------|
| Single-stage | 40-48" L x 20" W |
| Two-stage | 52-62" L x 24-26" W |
| Three-stage / wide | 65-72" L x 26-28" W |

Measure length, width, and height — including the handles, chute, and any attachments. Add 10 cm per dimension.

## Fabric Requirements

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Waterproof (2000mm+) | Melting snow and rain |
| Heavy-duty (600D) | Rough handling, garage storage |
| Breathable backer | Prevents condensation rust |
| UV-stabilized | Garage windows and sun exposure |

## Key Features

- **Elastic hem** for a snug fit
- **Tie-down straps** if stored outdoors
- **Reinforced corners** — the chute is sharp
- **Washable fabric** — salt and grime accumulate

## Preparation Before Covering

1. Run the blower dry — empty the fuel tank or add stabilizer
2. Clean the auger and chute of packed snow
3. Wipe down the housing to remove road salt
4. Loosen the auger control cables
5. Cover only a dry, cool machine

## Indoor vs Outdoor Storage

- **Garage/shed:** a dust cover keeps mice and grime out
- **Outdoor:** full waterproof cover — but always fuel-stabilized and dry

## Maintenance Under the Cover

- Check monthly for mouse droppings and nesting
- Re-clean if the cover develops condensation
- In spring, wash and air-dry the cover before storing it folded

## Key Takeaways

- Run the blower dry and add fuel stabilizer before covering for the season
- Waterproof heavy-duty fabric with reinforced corners handles the chute's sharp edges
- A breathable backer stops the condensation that rusts augers and cables
- Check under the cover monthly for mouse nests and moisture
