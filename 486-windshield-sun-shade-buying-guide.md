# Windshield Sun Shade Buying Guide

The same panel that keeps snow off a windscreen in January keeps sun off the dashboard in July, and that dual-season logic is why the category sells year-round in most markets. A windshield sun shade is also a highly visible product from outside the car, which makes it a natural fit for branded programmes.

## What a sun shade actually achieves

Parked in direct sun, a car interior absorbs heat through the glass. The dashboard, steering wheel, seat surfaces and any plastic trim all reach temperatures well above ambient, and they stay hot after the cabin air has cleared. Two consequences follow.

First, the driver returns to a vehicle that is uncomfortable to enter. Second, materials degrade. Plastics and vinyls are the parts most exposed to that heat and to the ultraviolet component of sunlight, and they fade, harden and crack over years of it. A shade addresses both by intercepting the light before it reaches the interior surfaces.

## Types and how they differ

**Folding accordion shades.** A pleated panel that unfolds to cover the windscreen. Compact and inexpensive, and the most common format in retail.

**Roll-up shades.** Stored on a roller, deployed by pulling across the screen. Neater in use but bulkier to store.

**Wire-frame or hoop shades.** A sprung frame that holds the panel flat against the glass. Good coverage, larger to store.

**Custom-fit shades.** Cut to a specific windscreen shape, including cut-outs for mirror mounts and camera housings. Best coverage, highest SKU count.

**Full-set kits.** A shade plus side and rear window panels. Higher ticket, and the format most often used for branded programmes because the kit covers the whole vehicle.

## Sizing

Universal sizes dominate because one SKU covers many vehicles, but the fit is approximate: gaps at the corners let light past. Vehicles with large, steeply raked screens and mirror-mounted sensors are the hardest to cover with a universal panel.

The practical approach mirrors what works for other fitted textile products — specify by windscreen dimensions, and publish a table that maps common vehicle models to the correct size. That removes the buyer's main source of uncertainty.

## Specification points

| Point | Consideration |
|---|---|
| Reflective outer face | Returns light instead of absorbing it |
| Stiffness | Determines whether the panel stays in place against the glass |
| Frame or edging | Holds the shape and protects the glass from the panel's edges |
| Storage format | Determines packability and therefore shipping cost |
| Attachment | Some designs rely on sun visors, others on suction or friction |

## A note on performance claims

Be wary of specific UV or temperature reduction percentages unless the supplier can describe how the figure was measured. A claim without a stated test method is not evidence, and it invites both customer disputes and competitor challenge. Describe the mechanism — interception of light before it reaches interior surfaces — and state care instructions accurately.

## Branded shades

The panel is large, flat and highly visible through the glass, which makes it a popular dealer, rental and fleet item. Keep artwork away from the fold lines on accordion designs; print that crosses a fold will crack before the rest of the panel does.
