# Car Cover for Coastal Areas — Salt, Sand, and Sun Protection

Living near the ocean is great for your lifestyle but hard on your car. Salt-laden air corrodes metal, sand acts like sandpaper on paint, and there's often less tree cover for shade. A car cover designed for coastal conditions addresses all three threats.

## The Coastal Triple Threat

**Salt corrosion:** Sea spray travels miles inland. It settles on your car's surface and accelerates rust on exposed metal, brake rotors, and underbody components. A waterproof cover prevents salt-laden moisture from reaching your car.

**Sand abrasion:** Wind-blown sand scratches clear coat like fine-grit sandpaper. A cover with a soft inner fleece lining absorbs sand impact without transferring it to your paint.

**Intensified UV:** Coastal areas often have less shade and more reflected UV off water. A 99% UV rejection cover is essential — UV damage happens faster near the ocean than inland.

## Recommended Cover Specs

- **600D Oxford** or heavier — salt accelerates fabric degradation in lighter materials
- **3000mm+ waterproof rating** — salt spray penetrates lower-rated covers
- **Soft fleece inner lining** — essential for paint protection in sandy environments
- **Full under-body straps** — coastal winds are stronger and more consistent than inland
- **Machine-washable** — rinse salt residue from the cover monthly
