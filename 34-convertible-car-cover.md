# Car Covers for Convertible Owners — Special Considerations

Convertibles present unique challenges for car covers: soft tops that trap moisture, vulnerable rear windows, and complex folding mechanisms. A generic cover can cause more harm than good if it doesn't account for these factors.

## Soft Top Protection

The biggest risk with convertible covers is moisture trapped against the soft top fabric. Canvas and vinyl tops absorb water, and a non-breathable cover seals that moisture in, leading to mildew and stitching rot. Always choose a breathable cover with a waterproof outer shell — not a solid plastic sheet.

## Window Protection

Plastic rear windows scratch easily. The inner lining of the cover must be soft fleece or non-woven fabric. Never use a cover with a rough polyester inner layer on a convertible with a plastic rear window.

## Water Pooling

Convertible tops are flatter than hardtops, making water pooling a real problem after rain. A properly fitted cover — ideally semi-custom — should have a slight roof peak or drainage channels to shed water rather than collect it.

## Indoor vs Outdoor

Indoor storage is ideal for convertibles. If you must park outdoors, invest in a premium multi-layer cover with 99% UV rejection, 3000mm+ waterproof rating, and a breathable middle membrane.
