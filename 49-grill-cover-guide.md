# BBQ Grill Cover Buying Guide — Protect Your Grill Year-Round

A quality grill costs $300-2,000. A cover costs $15-40. The math is simple. Here's how to choose the right grill cover for your setup.

## Material Comparison

Oxford fabric with PVC backing is the best all-around choice — waterproof, UV resistant, and durable enough for 2-3 years outdoors. Vinyl is heavier and more waterproof but cracks in freezing temperatures. Polyester canvas looks premium but requires re-waterproofing annually.

## Sizing

Measure your grill's width (with side shelves down), depth, and height. Add 2-3 inches for easy on/off. Universal covers with adjustable drawstrings fit most standard grills.

## Must-Have Features

- **Air vents:** Prevent wind lift and condensation
- **Velcro straps or click buckles:** Secure the cover in storms
- **Handles:** For one-person removal
- **Dark color:** Hides grease stains and weathering
