# Cross-Border E-Commerce Covers on Amazon — The FBA Playbook

Car covers on Amazon FBA are a proven cross-border category: steady year-round demand, a seasonal spike before winter, and room for differentiated private labels. But bulky products have FBA-specific economics. Here is the playbook for selling covers on Amazon profitably.

## The Market Signal

- Search volume for car cover keywords is strong year-round, peaking Sep-Nov in the US
- Competition exists but quality and fit complaints leave room for better products
- Seasonal variants (winter, snow, hail) extend the category year-round
- Review quality matters — fabric weight and fit drive ratings

## FBA Economics for Covers

Covers are size/weight-heavy, so fees matter:

| Cost Item | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| FBA fulfillment fee | $5-10 per unit depending on size tier |
| Storage fee | Higher for bulky items — watch cube |
| Referral fee (15%) | Standard |
| Inbound shipping | Bulk LCL/FCL to Amazon warehouses |

A $12 landed cover typically needs a $39.99-49.99 retail price to clear ~25-30% net margin.

## Size Tier Strategy

- **Smaller covers** (motorcycle, wheel, mirror) — lowest fees, faster turns
- **Standard car covers** — the sweet spot if packaging keeps dimensions in lower tiers
- **Avoid oversized tiers** — a cover in the "oversize" tier can add $15+ in fees
- **Vacuum packing** reduces volume for storage fees, but test the fit of compressed covers

## Listing Optimization for Covers

- Title: vehicle type + size + material + key feature (e.g., "Waterproof Car Cover for Sedans, 420D Oxford, UV Protection")
- Bullets: fabric weight, waterproof rating, fit details, storage bag, straps
- Images: white background main image + size chart image + lifestyle shots + comparison
- A+ Content: fabric cross-sections, sizing guide, warranty info
- Backend keywords: fit variations (sedan, SUV, truck, hatchback), climates, seasons

## Seasonal Planning

| Season | Strategy |
|--------|----------|
| Q2-Q3 | Build reviews on all-weather covers |
| Q3-Q4 | Stock winter/snow variants before demand peaks |
| Q1 | Hail season SKUs (southern US) + spring cleaning demand |

## Sourcing for Amazon Sellers

- **MOQ flexibility** — start at 100-300 pcs per SKU
- **Vehicle-specific sizes** — Amazon buyers search by model; support multiple sizes
- **UPC/EAN barcodes** — confirm the factory applies correct labels
- **Certifications** — REACH for EU, general compliance for US (CPSC textiles)
- **Private label** — your brand on the cover and package is the moat

## The Winning Pattern

1. Pick 2-3 vehicle segments with review gaps
2. Launch all-weather + one seasonal SKU
3. Use Amazon Vine and early ads to seed reviews
4. Expand sizes after the first wins
5. Feed return data back to the factory (see return reduction guide)
