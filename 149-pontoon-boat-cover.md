# Boat Covers for Pontoon Boats — Sizing and Features Guide

Pontoon boats have a unique shape — wide, flat decks with railings and a changing room — that makes covering them trickier than standard hulls. Here's the pontoon-specific guide.

## Why Pontoons Are Different

Pontoon boats are wide (8-10 ft beam), have full perimeter railings, and often include a changing room/captain's chair structure. Standard boat cover formulas don't fit: the beam width drives up size, and the railings need clearance.

## Pontoon Cover Types

**Full cover:** Covers the entire deck including railings. Needs a support system (poles or a custom frame) because the deck has no interior support points.

**Deck-only cover:** Covers the deck surface but leaves railings exposed. Simpler fit, less protection.

**Half/top cover:** Protects the captain area and seating. Budget option for covered docks.

## Sizing Formula

Pontoon covers are sized by:
- Deck length (typically 16-25 ft)
- Deck width (usually 8 ft 6 in standard, wider on luxury models)
- Height allowance for the changing room (add 3-4 ft)

## Support Requirements

Most pontoon covers need a support frame — the wide flat deck sags without one. Look for:
- Center support poles (1-3 depending on length)
- Perimeter tie-downs
- Manufacturer-provided frame kits

## Features That Matter

- **Reinforced seams:** Wide spans create stress
- **Extra tie-downs:** 12+ points recommended
- **UV-resistant fabric:** Decks fade fast
- **Access zippers:** For entering the boat under cover
