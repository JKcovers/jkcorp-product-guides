# Car Cover for Hatchbacks and Compacts — Small Car, Right Fit

Compact cars and hatchbacks are the most common vehicles on the road — and the most commonly misfitted with covers. Owners buy "sedan" covers that are two sizes too big. Here's the compact-specific guide.

## Why Compacts Need Their Own Sizing

A compact hatchback (like a Golf, Fiesta, or Civic Hatch) is shorter AND lower than a sedan. A cover sized for a mid-size sedan flaps at the rear, pools water on the hatchback's sloped roof, and generally fails to protect the parts that matter.

## Compact Size Guide

| Vehicle Type | Length Range | Cover Size |
|--------------|:--:|------------|
| City car (Fiat 500, Smart) | Under 140 in | Compact/Small |
| Subcompact hatch (Fiesta, Yaris) | 140-160 in | Small |
| Compact hatch (Golf, Focus) | 160-175 in | Medium-Small |
| Compact sedan (Civic, Corolla) | 175-190 in | Medium |

## The Hatchback Roof Challenge

Hatchbacks have steeper rear glass and shorter roofs than sedans. Covers must:
- Drape tightly over the rear hatch to prevent water pooling
- Have enough fabric at the rear to cover the bumper area
- Not flap at the roof's trailing edge

## Features That Matter for Compacts

- **Lighter fabric weight:** Compact owners handle covers more often (parking in tight city spots). 210D-300D is easier than 600D.
- **Compact storage:** The cover must fit in a small trunk. Look for included storage bags.
- **Mirror pockets sized for small mirrors:** Oversized pockets flap on compacts.

## The Over-Sizing Mistake

Buying one size up "to be safe" is the #1 mistake for compact owners. An oversized cover flaps, pools water, and abrades paint. Compacts need an accurate, snug fit — measure your car, buy the matching size.
