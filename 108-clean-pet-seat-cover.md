# How to Clean a Pet Seat Cover — Step-by-Step for Fur, Slobber, and Mud

Pet seat covers take a beating: fur, drool, mud, and the occasional accident. Proper cleaning extends the cover's life and keeps your car smelling fresh.

## The Weekly Routine (5 Minutes)

1. Shake the cover outside to remove loose fur
2. Vacuum the surface with a brush attachment (a lint roller works for quick touch-ups)
3. Spot-clean drool or mud with a damp cloth and mild soap
4. Let it air dry before folding

## The Monthly Deep Clean (30 Minutes)

**Machine washing:**
- Remove the cover and shake out loose debris
- Pre-treat stains with a gentle pet-safe stain remover
- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle
- Use mild detergent — avoid bleach and fabric softener (softener breaks down waterproof backing)
- Air dry completely — never machine dry (heat destroys the waterproof layer)

**Hand washing (delicate covers):**
- Fill a tub with cold water and mild detergent
- Gently scrub with a soft brush, focusing on stained areas
- Rinse thoroughly and squeeze (don't wring) to remove water
- Hang to dry completely

## The Accident Protocol

For urine or vomit:
1. Remove the cover immediately
2. Rinse with cold water (hot water sets protein stains)
3. Wash with an enzymatic cleaner — it breaks down the odor molecules
4. Air dry in the sun (UV neutralizes remaining odor)
5. If the smell persists, repeat or replace — odor embedded in the backing can't be fully removed

## Storage

Store the clean, fully dry cover folded loosely. Never store damp — mildew is nearly impossible to remove from waterproof-backed fabric.
