# Car Cover for Pickup Trucks — Special Sizing and Features

Pickup trucks are the hardest vehicles to fit with a car cover. Long beds, tall cabs, roof racks, and toolboxes all complicate sizing. Here's what pickup owners need to know.

## Measure Everything

Most "truck" covers assume a standard cab. If you have an extended cab, crew cab, or long bed, measure the actual overall length bumper-to-bumper including the tailgate.

## Bed Access

Do you need access to the truck bed with the cover on? Some covers have rear zippered access panels or two-piece designs that let you uncover just the cab or just the bed.

## Toolbox and Rack Compatibility

If you have a bed-mounted toolbox, ladder rack, or headache rack, a standard cover won't fit. OEM manufacturers can build covers with cutouts or raised sections to accommodate permanent accessories. Provide the accessory height and position when ordering custom covers.

## Weight Matters

Pickup covers are larger and heavier than car covers — a full-size truck cover can weigh 8-12 lbs. Look for covers with grab handles or a two-piece design for easier solo installation.
