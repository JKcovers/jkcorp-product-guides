# Air Conditioner Window Cover — Stop Drafts and Save Energy

A window AC unit sits half inside, half outside — and the outside half takes the weather. An air conditioner window cover blocks cold drafts in winter, keeps rain and debris out of the unit, and extends its life. Here is the complete guide.

## Why a Window AC Needs a Cover

- **Winter drafts** — cold air pours through and around the unit, raising heating bills
- **Rain and snow** — moisture corrodes the housing and the evaporator fins
- **Debris** — leaves and dust clog the exterior intake
- **UV damage** — plastic housings fade and become brittle

## Types of Window AC Covers

| Type | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fabric cover with drawstring | Winter storage | Breathable, fits most units |
| Vinyl cover | Heavy rain areas | Fully waterproof, check vents |
| Insulated cover | Cold climates | Adds an R-value layer |
| Ventilated summer cover | Year-round use | Protects while the AC runs |

## Sizing

Measure the exterior housing: width, height, and depth, including any protruding handles or the side louvers. A cover that is too small will not seal the bottom edge; one too large flaps in the wind. Most units need a cover sized by the housing, not the BTU rating.

## Installation and Care

1. Turn the unit off and let it dry before covering.
2. Slide the cover over the exterior housing from below.
3. Cinch the drawstring or strap above the top louver.
4. Tuck the bottom hem under the housing so rain runs off, not in.
5. Remove and air the cover in spring before storage.

## Common Questions

**Should I cover the AC in summer?** Only with a ventilated summer cover designed for running units. Standard winter covers block airflow and cause overheating.

**Do covers stop all drafts?** They stop most exterior drafts, but interior gaps around the window still leak. Pair the cover with weatherstripping inside for the best winter result.

**Will a fabric cover survive heavy snow?** Fabric covers shed light snow but can sag under deep accumulations. Brush snow off promptly, or choose an insulated rigid-style cover for heavy snow regions.

**How do I know my size?** Measure the housing width and height in inches — most covers list the housing dimensions they fit. BTU ratings alone do not determine fit.

## The Golden Rule

**Match the cover to the housing, and let the unit breathe.** Ventilation holes prevent the condensation that corrodes the very unit the cover protects.
