# Ebike Cover Comparison — Neoprene vs Oxford vs PEVA

Choosing the wrong material for your ebike battery cover means replacing it twice as often. Here's the real-world breakdown.

## Neoprene: The Insulator

Closed-cell foam construction that insulates better than any other cover material. Neoprene stretches for a snug fit and holds up well in rain. The trade-off: it costs 30-50% more than Oxford.

**Best for:** Cold-weather riders, year-round outdoor parking in variable climates.

## Oxford with PU Coating: The All-Rounder

Woven polyester with a waterproof PU layer. Available in 210D to 600D densities. The 420D version is the sweet spot — tough enough for daily use but not so heavy it's a hassle to put on.

**Best for:** Most riders, all-season use, bulk wholesale orders.

## PEVA: The Budget Pick

Lightweight plastic that's 100% waterproof and costs the least. The catch: it degrades in 6-8 months of direct sun exposure and isn't breathable, so condensation can build up underneath.

**Best for:** Temporary use, indoor storage, riders on a tight budget who park in covered areas.
