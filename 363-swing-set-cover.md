# Swing Set Cover Guide — Keep Kids' Outdoor Playsets Safe and Clean

A backyard swing set is a significant purchase, but rain, sun and bird droppings take a toll fast. Metal frames rust, wooden sets splinter and plastic seats fade. A swing set cover protects the whole structure — and keeps the equipment safe for kids.

## Why Cover a Swing Set

- **Metal rust prevention** — rain and humidity corrode A-frame joints and chains
- **Wood protection** — keeps moisture from seeping into seals, cracks and bolts
- **UV fade reduction** — sun bleaches plastic seats and slides
- **Less maintenance** — no more scrubbing bird droppings before playtime
- **Safety** — covered sets have no slippery wet seats or rusty chain links

## Sizing Your Swing Set Cover

Measure the **full footprint**: the length of the top beam, the width between the A-frame legs, and the **total height** from the ground to the top bar. Covers are typically sold in small (4-6 ft beam), medium (6-8 ft) and large (8-10 ft+). If your set has a slide or trapeze, measure the widest overall footprint — the cover must clear it.

## Material and Construction

| Material | Use |
|----------|-----|
| 600D Oxford, PU-coated | Best for all-season, snow areas |
| 420D Oxford, waterproof | Standard backyard use |
| Mesh / breathable | Partial shading, not weatherproof |

Look for **reinforced corners**, **double-stitched seams** and **elastic hem or tie-down straps** so the cover stays anchored in wind. Ventilation grommets reduce trapped heat and condensation.

## Installation Tips

1. Remove or fold down accessories like swings and trapeze bars if possible
2. Center the cover over the top beam and drape evenly
3. Secure the hem with straps or stakes — a loose cover flaps and wears faster
4. In heavy snow, brush off accumulation to protect the frame

## Key Takeaways

- Measure beam length, leg width and total height before buying
- 600D Oxford with tie-downs is the all-season standard
- Secure the hem — wind damage is the #1 cover killer
- A covered swing set lasts years longer and stays safer for children
