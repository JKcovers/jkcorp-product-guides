# Boat Cover Material Comparison — Solution-Dyed vs Coated Polyester

Choosing the right boat cover fabric is the difference between 3 years and 8 years of service. Two materials dominate the market — solution-dyed polyester and coated polyester — and they're not interchangeable.

## Solution-Dyed Polyester: The Long-Term Champion

Solution-dyed means the color pigment is mixed into the polymer before the fiber is extruded, rather than applied to the surface afterward.

**Strengths:**
- Color is integral to the fiber — it cannot fade, ever
- Excellent UV resistance (blocks 95%+ UV)
- Breathable — critical for preventing mildew on boats stored outdoors
- 5-7 year outdoor lifespan
- Repairable

**Weaknesses:**
- Costs 30-50% more than coated polyester
- Heavy (12-18 oz/yd²)

**Best for:** Year-round outdoor storage, saltwater environments, boats stored for months at a time.

## Coated Polyester: The Value Option

A polyester base fabric with a PVC or polyurethane coating applied to one side.

**Strengths:**
- 100% waterproof
- Lower cost
- Lightweight and easy to handle

**Weaknesses:**
- Coating cracks and peels after 2-3 years of UV exposure
- Not breathable — condensation forms under the cover
- Color fades when the coating wears

**Best for:** Short-term storage, covered marinas, budget-conscious owners.

## Which Should You Choose?

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| Outdoor slip/mooring, year-round | Solution-dyed |
| Trailer storage, seasonal | Solution-dyed |
| Covered dock, short-term | Coated polyester |
| Budget priority | Coated polyester |
| Saltwater environments | Solution-dyed (always) |
