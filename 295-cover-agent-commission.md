# Cover Agent Commission Model: Earning by Sourcing

The cover agent commission model lets individuals and small agencies earn from the cover trade without holding inventory: connect buyers with factories, manage the deal, and take a commission. It works well in the protective cover industry because buyers need exactly what agents provide — supplier vetting, communication, and QC oversight.

## How the Model Works

- **The agent's role:** find or qualify buyers (importers, distributors, e-commerce sellers), match them with vetted factories, manage samples and orders.
- **Who pays:** commission is typically paid by the factory (3-8% of order value) or split with the buyer, depending on the agreement.
- **The asset:** relationships and trust, not capital or warehouse space.

## Commission Structures

| Structure | Typical Terms |
|-----------|---------------|
| Factory-paid | 3-8% of FOB value, paid after order confirmation or shipment |
| Buyer-paid | 2-5% consultancy fee, often monthly or per project |
| Hybrid | Factory commission + buyer retainer for QC and sourcing services |
| Volume bonus | Extra 0.5-1% above annual order thresholds |

## What Agents Actually Do

- **Supplier sourcing:** shortlist factories by capability, price, and QC level
- **Negotiation:** MOQ, pricing, payment terms, lead time
- **Sampling:** manage sample rounds and fit verification
- **QC:** schedule and interpret mid-production and pre-shipment inspections
- **Logistics support:** Incoterm advice, container loading oversight
- **Problem solving:** returns, disputes, and rework coordination

## Building Credibility Fast

- Specialize: car covers, motorcycle covers, or boat covers — not everything
- Build a supplier scorecard (QC pass rates, lead time adherence, response quality)
- Document every deal: sample approvals, inspection reports, payment milestones
- Ask factories for references from existing agents

## Risks to Manage

- **Payment disputes:** define the trigger point (order confirmation vs. shipment) in writing
- **Buyer bypass:** protect yourself with a buyer-supplier exclusivity clause per deal
- **QC blame:** never certify quality without a documented inspection
- **Market shifts:** diversify across countries and product lines

## Is It Right for You?

The model suits people with sourcing, QC, or logistics experience in textiles. It scales from a side income (a few deals a year) to a full agency when you standardize your process.

## First-Deal Checklist

- Sign a written commission agreement with clear payment triggers
- Document every sample approval and inspection report
- Confirm exclusivity terms so buyers cannot bypass you
- Deliver value beyond price: QC oversight is your moat
- Start with one product line and win it before expanding
