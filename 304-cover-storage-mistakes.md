# Cover Storage Mistakes — Top 10 That Ruin Covers

The cover is off the car — now what? How you store a car cover between uses determines whether it lasts three seasons or three years. These 10 cover storage mistakes are the most common ways owners destroy their covers without ever using them.

## Mistake 1: Storing the Cover Damp

The #1 storage killer. Moisture trapped in a folded cover breeds mildew that eats fabric and leaves permanent stains and smell.

**Fix:** always dry the cover completely — ideally in the shade — before folding.

## Mistake 2: Storing It Dirty

Dirt ground into the fabric during folding becomes permanent grit. Next season, that grit scratches the paint it is supposed to protect.

**Fix:** shake out, brush, or wash the cover before storage.

## Mistake 3: Folding the Same Way Every Time

Folding along the same creases every time weakens the fabric along those lines until it splits.

**Fix:** vary the fold pattern each time, or roll loosely instead of folding.

## Mistake 4: Crushing It Under Weight

Storing covers under boxes, tires, or tools compresses the fabric, flattens padding, and damages seams.

**Fix:** store covers on a shelf or in a bin where nothing sits on top.

## Mistake 5: Storing in Direct Sun

UV degrades fabric even in storage. A cover stored in a sunny garage or on a windowsill fades and embrittles without ever touching the car.

**Fix:** store in a dark, cool place — the storage bag helps.

## Mistake 6: Storing in Heat

Attics and unvented garages in summer cook the coating and elastic. High heat accelerates fabric breakdown.

**Fix:** choose a cool storage spot — a basement, closet, or shaded shelf.

## Mistake 7: Storing With the Straps Tangled

Elastic straps left tangled or stretched stay deformed and lose tension. Next install, the cover does not hold.

**Fix:** coil straps loosely and tuck them inside the fold.

## Mistake 8: Not Using the Storage Bag

Without the bag, covers collect dust, absorb garage odors, and catch snags. The bag is not optional packaging — it is part of the care system.

**Fix:** use the provided bag or a breathable fabric sack (avoid sealed plastic that traps moisture).

## Mistake 9: Storing in Sealed Plastic

A sealed plastic bag traps any residual moisture and speeds mildew. Airflow matters even in storage.

**Fix:** use a breathable cotton or non-woven storage bag.

## Mistake 10: Storing for Years Without Checking

Covers in long storage get nibbled by rodents, attacked by moths, or damaged by moisture — silently.

**Fix:** check stored covers every few months; air them out and re-fold.

## The Golden Rule

**Clean, dry, cool, dark, breathable, and uncompressed.** Follow that and a quality cover will still protect after years in storage.
