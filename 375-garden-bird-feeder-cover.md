# Bird Feeder and Garden Cover Guide — Protect Feeders, Planters and Garden Gear

Bird feeders and garden supplies sit outside year-round, where rain soaks the seed, sun fades the plastic and frost cracks the planters. A few well-chosen garden covers protect both your gear and your birds' health.

## Why Cover Bird Feeders and Garden Items

- **Dry seed** — wet seed molds and spreads disease among visiting birds
- **Feeder longevity** — UV and rain degrade plastic, wood and metal feeders
- **Planter protection** — covers shield pots from frost and overwatering in heavy rain
- **Garden tool care** — keeps moisture off tools and kneeling pads
- **Cleaner garden** — covers keep pollen, leaves and dust off equipment

## What to Cover in the Garden

| Item | Cover recommendation |
|------|----------------------|
| Bird feeder | Feeder roof cover or a full feeder bag for winter storage |
| Seed storage | Waterproof seed bucket or bag |
| Planters and pots | Frost blanket or planter cover for winter |
| Garden tools | Tool bag or cover for shed and cart storage |
| Kneeling pads | Lightweight cover or bag |

## Choosing the Right Cover

- **Bird feeder covers** — look for a raised roof design that sheds rain while leaving feeding ports accessible; a clear or mesh window lets you see seed levels
- **Frost protection** — insulated or fleece-lined covers give plants a few extra degrees of protection
- **Garden storage** — waterproof bags with drawstrings keep tools and seed dry in sheds and garages

## Material Notes

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — durable all-season choice
- **Fleece or insulated fabric** — frost protection for plants
- **PVC-backed** — fully waterproof for exposed locations
- **UV-stabilized** — essential for anything that stays in the sun

## Care and Placement Tips

1. Clean feeders regularly — a cover protects seed but doesn't replace hygiene
2. Brush snow off covers to protect both fabric and frames
3. Store covers dry when not in use
4. Check covers seasonally for UV wear and tears

## Key Takeaways

- Match the cover to the job: dry seed, frost protection or storage
- Feeder covers should shed rain without blocking feeding access
- 600D Oxford and PVC-backed fabrics suit year-round garden use
- Regular cleaning plus a cover keeps birds and equipment healthy
