# Paddleboard Cover Guide — Protect Your SUP from Sun, Scuffs and Sand

A paddleboard is a precision piece of equipment — and it spends most of its life exposed to UV, dust and accidental knocks. A paddleboard cover is the simplest way to keep your SUP's hull in top shape for years of paddling.

## Why Cover Your Paddleboard

- **UV protection** — sun degrades EPS foam cores and epoxy or PVC skins
- **Scuff prevention** — protects rails and hull from knocks in the garage or on racks
- **Dust and debris defense** — sand and grit won't grind into the surface
- **Transport protection** — covers shield the board on roof racks and in truck beds
- **Heat control** — a light cover reflects sun and keeps the board cooler in storage

## Types of SUP Covers

| Cover type | Best for |
|------------|----------|
| Full-length cover | Long-term storage, transport |
| Nose/tail protector | Partial protection during short trips |
| Inflatable board bag | Protection plus easy carrying for iSUPs |

## Sizing Your Paddleboard Cover

Measure the board's **overall length** (e.g., 10 ft 6 in, 11 ft) and **maximum width** across the center. Full-length covers are sized by length with a width allowance — most fit boards up to about 32 in wide. For inflatable boards, add a little length for the rolled-up bundle. Always check the cover's length range against your board before buying.

## Material and Features

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — durable for outdoor storage and roof racks
- **UV-stabilized** — prevents fading and fabric breakdown in sun
- **Padded lining** — extra impact protection for transport
- **Zipper or elastic openings** — easy on/off without lifting the board
- **Carry handles or straps** — simplify moving the board

## Care and Storage

1. Rinse and dry the board before covering — salt and sand cause wear
2. Loosen fins or use a fin box protector where needed
3. Store covered boards flat or on racks, never leaning on rails
4. Wash the cover occasionally with mild soap and water

## Key Takeaways

- Match covers to board length and width — check the size range
- 600D Oxford with padding suits transport and outdoor storage
- Always cover a clean, dry board
- A covered SUP keeps its shape, color and resale value
