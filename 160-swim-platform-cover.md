# Swim Platform Boat Covers — Extending Protection Over the Platform

Swim platforms are the most overlooked part of a boat when it comes to covers. Many covers stop at the transom, leaving the platform exposed to UV, bird droppings, and the same weather that damages the rest of the boat.

## Why Swim Platforms Need Coverage

The swim platform is:
- **Constantly exposed** — it extends past the hull, catching sun all day
- **Often walked on** — bare feet, sunscreen, and moisture
- **Hard to replace** — a damaged platform can cost $500-1,500

UV fades the non-skid surface, and bird droppings etch the gelcoat. Platforms also collect water and debris when uncovered.

## Cover Options for Platforms

**Full-length covers that include the platform:** The best option — the cover extends past the transom to fully enclose the platform.

**Platform-specific covers:** A separate small cover for the platform only. Useful when the main cover doesn't reach, or when you access the boat often and want quick platform coverage.

## Sizing Considerations

When measuring for a cover that includes the platform:
- Add the platform's protrusion (usually 18-30 inches) to your centerline measurement
- Measure platform width separately (some extend wider than the hull)
- Account for ladder clearance

## The Trailer Factor

If you trailer the boat, a platform-inclusive cover needs extra reinforcement — the platform bounces and stresses the extended fabric at highway speeds.

## Material Matters

Platform sections need the same marine-grade fabric as the rest of the cover — solution-dyed polyester resists the UV and moisture the platform faces constantly.
