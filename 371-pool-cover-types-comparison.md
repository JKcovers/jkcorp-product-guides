# Pool Cover Types Comparison — Which Pool Cover Should You Buy?

Pool covers do very different jobs. Some keep leaves out, some save heat, some stop children and pets from falling in. Choosing the wrong type wastes money — here is how the main pool cover types compare.

## The Main Pool Cover Types

| Type | Job | Cost | Best for |
|------|-----|------|----------|
| Solar cover | Heat the water, reduce evaporation | Low | Warmer swimming in spring and fall |
| Leaf net | Catch debris on the water surface | Low | Pools near trees |
| Winter cover | Protect the pool all off-season | Medium | Cold climates, seasonal closing |
| Safety cover | Support a child or pet's weight | High | Families with kids and pets |
| Automatic cover | Open/close by motor, dual duty | Very high | Convenience, heat and safety |

## Solar Covers

Thin bubble-wrap sheets that float on the surface. They raise water temperature by a few degrees and cut evaporation and chemical loss. Expect to replace one every 2-3 seasons. Not for leaf protection — debris stays on top of the sheet.

## Leaf Nets and Mesh Covers

Leaf nets are open mesh that drapes over the pool to catch falling leaves while letting water through. Mesh winter covers do the same with stronger fabric, keeping the pool clean while rain and snow drain through. They don't stop evaporation or hold heat.

## Solid Winter Covers

Solid covers block sunlight entirely, preventing algae growth during the closed season. Water must be pumped off the top, which adds maintenance. Solid covers are the classic choice for northern winters where pools close for months.

## Safety Covers

Safety covers are engineered to support the weight of a child or pet — usually anchored with straps to the pool deck. They meet strict safety standards (ASTM F1346 in the US). More expensive, but the only cover type that genuinely adds fall protection.

## Automatic Covers

Motorized covers roll out at the touch of a button. They combine heat retention, debris exclusion and safety in one system, which is why they dominate high-end pools. The trade-off is installation cost and ongoing mechanical maintenance.

## Key Takeaways

- Match the cover to the job: heat, debris, off-season protection or safety
- Safety covers are the only type designed to support a child's weight
- Solid winter covers need a pump; mesh covers drain naturally
- Automatic covers combine heat, debris and safety benefits at a premium price
