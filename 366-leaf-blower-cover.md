# Leaf Blower Cover Guide — Proper Outdoor Storage for Your Blower

Leaf blowers are often left leaning against the shed wall, where dust, damp and accidental knocks shorten their life. A leaf blower cover is a small purchase that protects a costly tool — here is what to consider.

## Why Store Your Blower Covered

- **Dust and debris protection** — keeps intake vents and filters clean
- **Moisture defense** — prevents rust on metal housings and corrosion in electrical connections
- **Impact protection** — a cover cushions the blower against knocks and scratches
- **Organized storage** — no more tangled cords or misplaced nozzles

## Types of Blowers and Their Covers

| Blower type | Cover consideration |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Gas handheld | Compact cover; check the tube length |
| Electric corded | Slim cover; store the cord with it |
| Battery / cordless | Keep dry to protect the battery contacts |
| Backpack | Taller cover with shoulder-strap allowance |

## Sizing and Fit

Measure the **overall length from nozzle tip to handle** and the **widest point**. Most leaf blower covers are universal (roughly 20-30 in long). A cover with an **elastic hem or drawstring** stays on during gusts and fits a range of models. If you store the blower with its tube attached, add a few inches for the tube length.

## Material Recommendations

- **600D Oxford, PU-coated** — durable all-season choice for outdoor sheds
- **210D polyester** — lightweight dust protection for garage shelves
- **PVC-coated** — for fully exposed outdoor storage

## Storage Best Practices

1. Clean the blower and empty the fuel tank before long-term storage
2. Coil and store the cord or battery separately if possible
3. Hang or place the covered blower off the damp floor
4. Check the cover occasionally for holes from rodents or sharp tools

## Key Takeaways

- Measure length including the nozzle, and size up if the tube stays attached
- 600D Oxford with drawstring suits shed and outdoor storage
- Cover a clean, fuel-empty blower for the off-season
- A covered blower stays reliable for many seasons
