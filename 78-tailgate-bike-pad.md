# Tailgate Bike Pads — The Pickup Owner's Guide to Hauling Bikes

For pickup truck owners who ride, a tailgate bike pad is the missing link between the bike rack and the truck bed. It's the simplest, safest way to carry bikes without a roof rack or hitch mount.

## What a Tailgate Pad Does

A padded protector that drapes over the tailgate, protecting both the truck's paint and the bike's frame while hauling bikes upright in the bed. The pad has slots where the bike frames rest, keeping them separated and stable.

## Why Riders Choose Tailgate Pads

- **No hitch required:** Works on any pickup with a standard tailgate
- **Faster than a rack:** Load/unload in under a minute per bike
- **Carries more:** 3-6 bikes depending on pad width
- **Protects both vehicles:** The truck's tailgate paint and the bikes' frames

## What to Look For

**Density of padding:** 3/4 to 1 inch of closed-cell foam is the sweet spot — thick enough to protect, thin enough to still latch the tailgate.

**Fabric:** 600D+ Oxford with waterproof backing. The pad faces the weather whenever the truck is parked.

**Strap system:** Should cinch tight under the tailgate with quick-release buckles.

**Frame slots:** Pre-cut slots keep frames from touching each other. Some pads have removable dividers for flexibility.

## Sizing

Standard pads fit full-size and mid-size trucks (60-70 inch tailgates). Compact truck owners should measure and check for specific short-bed versions.
