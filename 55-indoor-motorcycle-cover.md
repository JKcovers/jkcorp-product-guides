# Indoor Motorcycle Cover Guide — Garage and Showroom Protection

Indoor motorcycle covers serve a different purpose than outdoor covers. Here's what matters most for garage storage.

## Why Indoor Covers Are Different

A garage-protected bike doesn't face rain, UV, or wind. The threats are more subtle: fine dust that settles and scratches paint when wiped, accidental contact from bicycles and tools, insect droppings, and temperature fluctuations that cause condensation.

## Material Choice

- **Fleece-lined polyester:** Softest option, zero scratch risk. Best for bikes with custom paint or chrome.
- **Cotton canvas:** Classic look, highly breathable, absorbs minor moisture. Good for unheated garages.
- **Lightweight nylon:** Compact and packable. Good for bikes stored in shared garages where space is tight.

## Features That Matter Indoors

- **Full elastic hem:** Keeps dust from creeping up from the floor
- **Breathable fabric:** Prevents condensation on cold metal surfaces
- **Lightweight:** You'll take this cover on and off frequently
- **Machine washable:** Dust buildup means regular washing

## Don't Pay for Outdoor Features You Don't Need

Skip the waterproof membrane, UV coating, and heavy-duty straps. An indoor cover costs $15-30 versus $40-80 for outdoor. Spend the savings on a better indoor material (fleece over polyester).
