# Smoker Cover Buying Guide — Size, Material and Fit for Your Smoker

Smokers are exposed to the harshest conditions of any backyard appliance: high heat, grease, rain and sun. Without a cover, the steel shell rusts, the firebox corrodes and the thermometer and controls degrade. A smoker cover is essential maintenance, not an accessory.

## Why Your Smoker Needs a Cover

- **Rust prevention** — moisture on a hot-then-cold steel body accelerates corrosion
- **Firebox protection** — keeps rain and ash out of the firebox and vents
- **Controller protection** — shields digital thermometers and probes from water
- **Paint and finish preservation** — UV and rain fade the powder coat
- **Longer lifespan** — a covered smoker can last years longer

## Smoker Types and Sizing

| Smoker type | How to measure |
|-------------|----------------|
| Vertical / bullet | Height and widest diameter of the body |
| Offset / barrel | Total length, width and height including the firebox |
| Pellet | Height, width and depth, including the hopper |
| Kamado | Diameter of the dome at its widest point |

Measure the **overall footprint and height**, including handles, vents and side shelves if they stay attached. Smoker covers are sold in size classes (small, medium, large, extra-large) — compare your measurements against the listed dimensions and size up when in doubt.

## Material Comparison

| Material | Best for |
|----------|----------|
| 600D Oxford, PU-coated | All-season outdoor storage |
| PVC / vinyl | Rainy climates, fully waterproof |
| Canvas | Classic look, breathable, heavier |

## Features That Matter

- **Elastic hem or drawstring** — holds the cover on during storms
- **Ventilation panels** — reduce condensation that rusts steel from inside
- **Reinforced seams and corners** — withstand repeated fitting over sharp edges
- **Handles** — make removal easier on large covers
- **Heat tolerance** — let the smoker cool fully before covering

## Care and Maintenance

1. Always let the smoker cool completely before covering
2. Empty the ash pan and clean the grates first
3. Brush off snow in winter so weight doesn't damage the cover
4. Wash the cover with mild soap and water each spring

## Key Takeaways

- Measure the full footprint and height, including the firebox and shelves
- 600D Oxford with drawstring is the all-season standard
- Never cover a hot smoker — always cool and clean first
- A covered smoker keeps its heat retention and resale value
