# Commercial Seat Covers — Restaurant, Hotel, and Venue Protection

Commercial seating takes abuse that residential furniture never sees. Restaurant booths, hotel lobby chairs, and event venue seating need covers that balance durability, cleanability, and appearance.

## Commercial-Grade Requirements

- **Stain resistance:** Food, drinks, and bodily fluids are daily threats. Solution-dyed polyester resists staining better than printed fabrics.
- **Machine washable at high temperatures:** Commercial laundry cycles run at 140-160°F. Covers must survive weekly hot washes without shrinking or fading.
- **Fire retardant options:** Many commercial spaces require NFPA 701 or CAL 117 compliance.
- **Quick-change design:** Staff should be able to swap covers in under 2 minutes during service.

## Material Options

- **Polyester with PU backing:** Waterproof, stain-resistant, holds up to commercial laundry.
- **Vinyl:** Wipe-clean surface, best for high-turnover settings. Can feel hot and sticky in warm climates.
- **Performance fabric blends:** Look premium while resisting stains. Higher upfront cost but longer lifespan.
