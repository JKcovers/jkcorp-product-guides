# Grill Cover Maintenance — Double the Lifespan With 10 Minutes a Month

A grill cover faces some of the harshest conditions of any protective product: direct sun, grease splatter, and weather extremes. With monthly care, a good cover lasts 3+ years instead of 18 months.

## Monthly Cleaning

1. Shake off loose debris and ash
2. Rinse with a garden hose (grease and smoke residue wash off)
3. Wipe with mild dish soap and a soft cloth for stubborn grease
4. Rinse thoroughly and air dry completely before reinstalling

## Why Grills Are Hard on Covers

Grill covers sit close to heat sources and absorb grease that degrades fabric coatings. UV exposure between grilling sessions weakens the fabric. The combination of heat, grease, and sun makes grills one of the fastest killers of outdoor covers.

## Signs It's Time to Replace

- Water no longer beads on the surface (coating gone)
- Fabric feels stiff or brittle
- Grease stains won't wash out (coating compromised)
- Velcro straps no longer hold

## Pro Tips

- Let the grill cool completely before covering (30-45 minutes)
- Wipe the grill surface before covering to remove fresh grease
- In winter, brush snow off promptly to prevent fabric stress
- Store the cover indoors during the off-season (after cleaning)
