# Air Conditioner Outdoor Unit Cover — Protection That Keeps AC Running

The outdoor condenser unit is the most exposed — and most expensive — part of an air conditioner. Rain, leaves, and sun attack it year-round. An AC unit cover protects the condenser, but the wrong cover can trap moisture and damage it. Here is the balanced guide.

## Does an AC Unit Really Need a Cover?

The condenser contains the compressor, coils, and fan motor. It is designed to handle rain, but winter ice, falling debris, and nesting animals cause real damage. A cover adds protection in the off-season — and should be removed while the AC is running.

## The Golden Rule: Cover Off While Running

Never run the AC with the cover on. Condenser units need airflow to reject heat; a cover during the cooling season restricts airflow, raises pressure, and can trip the unit or damage the compressor.

## When to Cover

| Season | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Summer (in use) | No cover — keep the unit clear |
| Fall | Light dust cover |
| Winter (off) | Full waterproof cover |
| Coastal/all-year | Breathable UV cover |

## Choosing an AC Unit Cover

| Feature | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Fabric | 420D/600D Oxford, waterproof |
| Breathability | Vents or breathable backer — critical |
| Fit | Snug with elastic hem and straps |
| Size | Match the unit's height, width, depth |

## Sizing

Measure the unit's height, width, and depth. Add 5 cm per dimension. A too-large cover flaps and holds water; a too-small cover strains the seams.

## Maintenance That Matters More Than the Cover

- Trim nearby branches so leaves don't pile on the unit
- Clear debris from the top grille before winter
- Check the fins are not bent after storms
- Keep the cover clean so it doesn't become a pest home

## The Anti-Condensation Argument

Condenser coils rust fastest in humid coastal air. A breathable cover protects the fins from salt spray while allowing moisture to escape. Fully sealed covers — even "breathable" claims — should always have vents.

## Key Takeaways

- Cover the condenser only when the AC is off — airflow is critical while running
- Breathability beats pure waterproofing for condenser units
- Size by the unit's dimensions plus 5 cm; a snug, vented cover protects best
