# Motorcycle Cover Size Chart: Find the Right Fit

A motorcycle cover size chart looks simple until you try to match a bike with a cover class. Bikes vary wildly: a scooter and a touring motorcycle can differ by 40 inches in length. This guide explains size classes, how to measure your bike, and how to read manufacturer charts correctly.

## Size Classes Explained

| Class | Typical Length | Bike Examples |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Small | Under 70 inches | Scooters, mopeds, mini bikes |
| Medium | 70-80 inches | Naked bikes, standard motorcycles |
| Large | 80-90 inches | Sport bikes, cruisers |
| XL | 90-100 inches | Touring bikes, adventure bikes |
| XXL | Over 100 inches | Full-dress tourers with luggage |

Length is the primary dimension, but width and height matter for mirror and screen clearance.

## How to Measure Your Motorcycle

1. **Length:** front tire edge to rear tire edge, straight line
2. **Height:** ground to the highest point (screen, mirrors, or top case)
3. **Width:** handlebar tip to handlebar tip
4. **Accessories:** note side cases, top cases, and tall screens

Measure with the bike on its center stand if possible; add accessory dimensions to the base figures.

## Fit Features on the Cover

- **Mirror pockets:** protect mirrors and reduce strain
- **Screen clearance:** adventure and touring screens need headroom
- **Antenna allowance:** for bikes with tall antennas
- **Elastic hem with lock loop:** fits the base tight and accepts a lock
- **Side case room:** wider cutouts for pannier-equipped bikes

## Sizing Rules of Thumb

- If the bike sits at the top of a class, buy one class up
- Universal covers run large; measure before buying
- A cover that is too small stresses the hem and leaves the wheels exposed
- A cover that is too large flaps and can scratch the tank

## Quick Takeaways

- Measure length, height, and width including mirrors, screens, and side cases
- Scooters use small classes; touring bikes with luggage need XL-XXL
- If the bike sits at the top of a class, move up one size
- Check for mirror pockets, screen clearance, and a lock loop in the fit

## Class-Specific Advice

- **Scooters:** small or medium; look for shorter, wider patterns
- **Sport bikes:** medium-large; lower height, clip-on mirrors
- **Adventure bikes:** large-XL; tall screens and panniers need room
- **Touring bikes:** XL-XXL; include top cases in the length figure
