# Car Cover for Long-Term Storage — The Months-Long Protection Plan

Storing a car for weeks or months is different from daily parking. The cover becomes your car's only line of defense against dust, moisture, UV, and pests. Here's the long-term storage playbook.

## The Long-Term Storage Threats

| Threat | How It Damages |
|--------|---------------|
| Dust | Settles into paint pores, scratches on removal |
| Condensation | Mildew on seats and carpet, corrosion |
| UV through windows | Fades dash, cracks leather |
| Rodents | Nest in engine bay, chew wiring |
| Flat spots | Tires deform from sitting |

## Preparing the Car BEFORE Covering

1. **Wash and dry thoroughly** — dirt under a cover becomes sandpaper
2. **Apply wax or sealant** — a sacrificial layer under the cover
3. **Inflate tires to max rated pressure** — reduces flat spotting
4. **Fill the fuel tank** — prevents condensation in the tank
5. **Add fuel stabilizer** — protects the fuel system
6. **Battery maintainer** — or disconnect the battery

## The Right Cover for Long-Term Storage

**Breathable is critical:** A non-breathable cover traps condensation over weeks. Choose multi-layer construction with a breathable middle membrane.

**Full coverage:** The cover must seal the bottom edge (elastic hem) to keep dust and pests out.

**UV-rated fabric:** 99% rejection protects paint and slows interior damage through windows.

**Secure straps:** Even indoor storage — drafts and temperature swings move loose covers.

## The Maintenance Schedule

- **Monthly:** Remove the cover on a dry day, air out the car and cover for an hour, check for moisture or pests
- **Quarterly:** Wash the cover and inspect for damage
- **Before returning to service:** Full detail and tire pressure check

## Indoor vs Outdoor Long-Term

**Indoor (garage):** A breathable dust cover suffices — the structure blocks weather, you need dust and scratch protection.

**Outdoor:** Full multi-layer cover required, plus periodic checks after storms.
