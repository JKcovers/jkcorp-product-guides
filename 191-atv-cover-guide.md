# ATV Cover Guide — Protecting Your Quad Year-Round

ATVs live outdoors — in truck beds, under carports, and at the back of the property. Rain, dust, and UV take a toll on plastics, seats, and electricals. An ATV cover is a small purchase that protects a big one. Here is the complete guide.

## Why ATVs Need Covers

- **Plastic fades** and becomes brittle in UV
- **Seats crack** and hold water
- **Handlebars, grips, and switches** corrode
- **Engine and exhaust** collect dust and debris
- **Theft protection** — a covered ATV is less visible

## ATV Cover Sizing

ATVs vary widely. Measure the full footprint:

| ATV Type | Typical Size |
|----------|--------------|
| Youth ATV | 60" L x 35" W |
| Utility ATV | 75-85" L x 45-48" W |
| Sport ATV | 72-78" L x 44-46" W |
| ATV with plow/racks | Add length for accessories |

Measure length, width, and height including mirrors, racks, and the winch. Add 10 cm per dimension.

## Fabric and Features

| Feature | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Fabric | 420D/600D Oxford, waterproof |
| UV coating | Essential for plastics |
| Straps | Elastic hem + 2-4 tie-down straps |
| Lining | Soft inner lining to protect plastics |
| Vents | Breathable backer to release condensation |

## Storage Scenarios

- **Outdoor year-round:** full waterproof cover with straps
- **Under carport:** light weather cover
- **Garage:** dust cover is enough
- **Snow regions:** heavy-duty cover; remove snow weekly

## Preparation Before Covering

1. Let a hot engine cool completely — covering a hot muffler can melt fabric
2. Dry the seat and floorboards
3. Inflate tires to recommended pressure
4. Disconnect the battery for long-term storage or keep a trickle charger
5. Treat the seat with UV protectant before covering

## Common Mistakes

- Covering a hot ATV after a ride
- Buying "universal" covers that pool water on the seat
- Skipping straps in windy areas
- Forgetting vents — condensation rusts handlebars and racks

## Key Takeaways

- Measure the full footprint including racks, mirrors, and winch — then add 10 cm
- Let the engine cool completely before covering
- UV-stabilized fabric is non-negotiable for fenders and seats
- A covered ATV stays cleaner, runs cooler, and resells for more
