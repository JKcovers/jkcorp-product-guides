# Motorcycle Cover Buying Guide — Protect Your Bike from Weather and Theft

Motorcycles are more exposed than cars. Without a roof, doors, or windows, every component faces direct assault from sun, rain, and dust. A quality motorcycle cover is essential gear, not an optional accessory.

## Why Motorcycle Covers Are Essential

Riders who park outdoors without a cover face: faded paint and plastics from UV exposure, rusted chains and fasteners from rain, clogged air filters from wind-blown dust, cracked seats from temperature swings, and bird droppings that etch into paint and chrome.

A good cover prevents all of this for $15-40 — less than the cost of one chain replacement.

## Material Options

**Oxford fabric (210D-420D):** Best for outdoor use. Waterproof with PU coating, UV resistant, breathable. Most popular choice for year-round protection.

**Polyester with PVC backing:** Decent outdoor protection, slightly less breathable than Oxford. Good value option for covered parking.

**Nylon:** Lightweight and compact, best for touring and travel covers. Not for long-term outdoor use.

**Cotton blend:** Indoor only. Soft and breathable but absorbs water.

## Essential Features

- **Heat-resistant lower panels:** The exhaust area stays hot for 30+ minutes after riding. Panels rated to 300°F+ prevent melting.
- **Lock chain opening:** An opening at the bottom allows a chain lock to pass through, securing both the bike and the cover.
- **Windproof belly strap:** A clip strap under the bike prevents the cover from blowing off in wind.
- **Reflective strips:** Safety for bikes parked on streets at night.
- **Compression bag:** For touring riders, a compact stuff sack that fits in a saddlebag.

## Sizing

Measure your bike's overall length from front tire to rear fender. Most manufacturers use these categories:

| Size | Length | Fits |
|------|--------|------|
| Medium | Up to 90 inches | Sport bikes, standard bikes |
| Large | 90-100 inches | Cruisers, touring bikes |
| X-Large | 100-110 inches | Full dressers, large touring bikes |
| Scooter | 70-80 inches | Vespas, maxi-scooters |

Motorcycles with tall windshields, top cases, or luggage racks should size up. A cover that's too short leaves the front wheel and fork exposed.
