# How to Clean a Motorcycle Cover — Simple Steps for Longer Life

A dirty cover scratches your paint. A wet, mildewed cover smells bad and degrades faster. Here's how to keep your motorcycle cover clean in 10 minutes.

## When to Wash

Outdoor covers: Every 2-3 months. Bird droppings, tree sap, and road grime accumulate and can bleed through the fabric.

Indoor covers: Every 6 months. Indoor dust is less aggressive but still builds up.

## Washing Steps

1. Shake the cover out to remove loose dirt
2. Machine wash on gentle cycle with cold water and mild detergent (no bleach, no fabric softener)
3. Air dry — never use a dryer. Heat damages waterproof coatings
4. Do not wring or twist — hang over a railing

## Spot Cleaning

For bird droppings or sap, rinse the spot with a hose and gently rub with mild soap. Don't scrub aggressively — it damages the waterproof coating.

## Storage

Always store in the included storage bag. A crumpled cover on the garage floor collects dirt that scratches paint the next time you use it. If the cover is damp, drape it to dry completely before bagging.
