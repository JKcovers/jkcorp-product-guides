# Outdoor Water Heater Cover — Protecting the Tank That Works All Year

Outdoor water heaters — tank-style and tankless — run 24/7 in rain, sun, and frost. Their jackets and electronics take a beating that shortens their life. An outdoor water heater cover adds protection where it matters most.

## Why Cover a Water Heater?

- **Corrosion:** rain and salt air attack the jacket and fittings
- **Freeze risk:** exposed pipes and drain valves freeze in winter
- **UV damage:** sun degrades plastic housings and wiring
- **Dust and debris:** vents clog, burners run dirty
- **Longevity:** protected units fail later and need fewer repairs

## Tank vs Tankless Covers

| Type | Shape | Cover Requirements |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| Tank (40-80 gal) | Tall cylinder | Tall cover, handles for fit |
| Tankless | Compact box | Vent clearance, slim fit |

## Venting Is Non-Negotiable

Gas water heaters need combustion air and proper venting. The cover must not block intake vents, exhaust vents, or the pressure relief valve. Look for covers with vent openings or breathable fabric — and check your unit's manual for clearance requirements.

## Material Guide

| Material | Use |

| 420D Oxford | Standard climates |
| Vinyl/PVC | Heavy rain, mild winters |
| Reflective fabric | Sunny climates — reduces heat buildup |

## Sizing Tips

- **Tank units:** measure height and diameter of the tank jacket
- **Tankless units:** measure width, height, and depth of the housing
- **Add 5-8 cm** for easy installation
- **Leave room** for the drip pan and drain valve access

## Features to Look For

- Elastic hem or drawstring bottom
- Quick-release straps
- Access flaps for controls and valves
- UV-stabilized fabric
- Anti-condensation vents

## Placement Matters Too

Even with a cover, tilt the unit slightly or raise it off the ground so standing water doesn't pool at the base. Keep the cover pulled down fully in winter so frost cannot reach the drain valve.

## Key Takeaways

- Never block vents, exhaust, or the pressure relief valve — clearance is a safety requirement
- Waterproof Oxford with vent panels is the best balance for outdoor heaters
- Size by tank or housing dimensions plus 5-8 cm, and keep the drain valve accessible
- Raise the unit and keep the cover fully down in winter to protect pipes and valves
