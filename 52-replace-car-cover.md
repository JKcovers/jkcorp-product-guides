# How Often Should You Replace a Car Cover?

Even the best car cover has a finite lifespan. Here's how to know when it's time for a replacement, organized by cover type and usage.

## Outdoor Covers: 2-4 Years

A quality multi-layer Oxford cover used year-round outdoors lasts 2-3 years in moderate climates, 3-4 years if used only seasonally. Replace when: (1) water no longer beads on the surface — the waterproof coating has degraded, (2) fabric feels thin or brittle when pinched, (3) elastic hem has lost significant stretch, (4) tears or holes larger than a dime appear.

## Indoor Covers: 5+ Years

Indoor covers face none of the weather stress. Dust is the only threat. An indoor fleece or polyester cover regularly machine-washed (every 6 months) can last a decade.

## PEVA Covers: 6-12 Months

Budget PEVA covers degrade fast. In direct sun, expect 6 months. In covered parking, 12 months. They're disposable protection — budget for replacement when you buy.

## Extending Cover Life

- Machine wash gently every 2-3 months (outdoor) or 6 months (indoor)
- Never use a dryer — heat destroys waterproof coatings
- Store in the included bag when not in use
- Repair small tears immediately with fabric patch tape
