# Cover Sampling Checklist — 12 Checks Before You Approve

The sample is the single most reliable predictor of bulk order quality. A cover sample that passes these 12 checks will almost always perform in production; a sample that fails any of them signals problems ahead. Use this cover sampling checklist before approving any cover sample.

## Check 1: Fabric Weight and Feel

Confirm the sample fabric matches the quoted weight. A 600D cover should feel dense and substantial — not flimsy.

**Test:** weigh the sample if possible; compare hand-feel against a known reference.

## Check 2: Fabric Construction

Check the weave pattern and finish. Tight weave with a smooth finish indicates quality; loose, shiny fabric suggests cost-cutting.

**Test:** inspect under bright light and stretch the fabric lightly — it should spring back.

## Check 3: Water Resistance

Test the water repellency immediately. Water should bead and roll off.

**Test:** pour water on the surface and tilt — observe beading and runoff.

## Check 4: Seam and Stitching Quality

Seams are where covers fail first. Check stitch density, straightness, and loose threads.

**Test:** pull seams gently at stress points — no gaps or broken stitches.

## Check 5: Zipper and Closure Function

Zippers and buckles must operate smoothly and close securely.

**Test:** open/close each zipper and buckle several times.

## Check 6: Size Accuracy

Measure the sample against the spec. Sizes shrink or stretch in production — the sample sets the baseline.

**Test:** measure length, width, and height; confirm within spec tolerance.

## Check 7: Logo and Print Quality

Custom logos must be sharp, correctly placed, and durable.

**Test:** check edges for bleed, alignment, and heat transfer quality.

## Check 8: Color Accuracy

The sample color must match the approved Pantone or reference.

**Test:** compare under daylight and indoor light; note any discrepancy.

## Check 9: Packaging

The sample's packaging previews bulk packaging. Check for protective wrapping, branding, and labeling.

**Test:** evaluate packaging condition and printed information.

## Check 10: Accessories Included

Straps, storage bags, and instruction cards must match the order spec.

**Test:** inventory every included accessory against the order.

## Check 11: Odor and Chemical Smell

Strong chemical odors signal poor materials or processing.

**Test:** smell the fabric, especially after it has been sealed in packaging.

## Check 12: Real-World Fit (If Possible)

The best test is on an actual vehicle.

**Test:** if you have the vehicle, install the sample and check fit, coverage, and wind behavior.

## After the Sample: Document Everything

- Photograph the sample from all angles
- Record measurements and test results
- Note every deviation from spec in writing to the supplier
- Keep the approved sample as the production reference

## The Golden Rule

**Test the sample as if it were the finished product — because it is.** Approve only what you would ship to your own customers.
