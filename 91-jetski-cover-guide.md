# Jetski (PWC) Cover Guide — Protect Your Watercraft

Personal watercraft (PWC) — Jetskis, Sea-Doos, WaveRunners — are expensive machines exposed to the worst of sun and water. A dedicated PWC cover is the cheapest protection you can buy.

## Why PWCs Need Specific Covers

PWC covers differ from boat covers in critical ways:
- **Lower profile:** Need covers that fit tight over the handlebars and seat
- **Trailering:** Covers must stay secure at highway speeds
- **UV exposure:** The deck and seat plastics fade faster than fiberglass hulls

## Cover Types

**Storage cover:** For off-season storage. Full coverage, heavy-duty fabric, maximum UV and weather protection.

**Trailering cover:** Reinforced for highway speeds — extra tie-downs, reinforced stress points, and material rated for wind abrasion.

**Day cover:** Lightweight, for between-ride protection at the dock or campsite. Compact and quick to install.

## Key Features

- **Elastic hem:** Keeps the cover snug over the watercraft's rounded shape
- **Tie-down loops:** For trailering security
- **UV-resistant fabric:** 95%+ rejection — the deck plastic is the most UV-sensitive part
- **Breathable middle layer:** Prevents condensation on the seat and engine cover
- **Snap or buckle attachment:** Keeps the cover from blowing off

## Sizing

PWC covers are sized by watercraft model and hull length:

| Size | Length | Typical Models |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Small | Up to 8' | WaveRunner VX, Jet Ski SX |
| Medium | 8-10' | Sea-Doo GTI, Jet Ski STX |
| Large | 10-12' | Sea-Doo GTX, Jet Ski Ultra |

## The Trailer Consideration

If you trailer your PWC, choose a trailering-specific cover or verify the storage cover has reinforced tie-downs. A storage cover flapping at 60 mph tears within one trip.
