# Car Cover vs Garage — The Complete Comparison for Paint Protection

The garage-versus-cover debate has one right answer for most people: use both. But understanding the differences helps you allocate your protection budget correctly.

## The Protection Comparison

| Threat | Garage | Quality Cover |
|--------|:--:|:--:|
| Rain | ✅ | ✅ |
| UV | ✅ | ✅ (99% rejection) |
| Bird droppings | ✅ | ✅ |
| Tree sap | ✅ | ✅ |
| Dust | ⚠️ (garages get dusty) | ✅ |
| Scratches (physical) | ⚠️ | ⚠️ |
| Temperature control | ✅✅ | ⚠️ |
| Theft visibility | ✅ | ✅ (hides car) |
| Condensation | ⚠️ (unheated garages) | ⚠️ (depends on breathability) |

## The Surprising Finding

A quality cover in a garage beats either alone: the cover adds dust protection, scratch prevention, and temperature moderation on top of the garage's weather protection.

## When a Garage Is Enough

- Climate-controlled garage
- Clean garage (no tools stored against the car)
- Daily driving (cover on/off is a chore)

## When a Cover Is Essential

- No garage (apartment, shared parking)
- Long-term storage (weeks+)
- Outdoor parking in harsh climate
- Classic/collector cars (every detail matters)

## The Optimal Setup

1. **Indoor cover + garage:** Dust and scratch protection
2. **Outdoor cover + no garage:** Weather protection
3. **Both garage + outdoor cover:** For long-term storage

## Cost Reality

A garage bay costs $50-200/month. A quality cover costs $50-150 once. For most owners, a cover delivers 70-80% of a garage's paint protection at 1% of the cost.
