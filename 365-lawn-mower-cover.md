# Lawn Mower Cover Guide — Outdoor Storage for Gas and Electric Mowers

Most lawn mowers live outside or in an unheated shed, exposed to rain, dew and dust. Moisture rusts the deck and corrodes electrical components, while debris clogs the air intake. A simple lawn mower cover keeps your machine starting reliably season after season.

## Why Use a Lawn Mower Cover

- **Rust prevention** — a covered steel deck stays dry between cuts
- **Engine protection** — keeps rain and debris out of the air filter and exhaust
- **Electrical safety** — protects batteries, wiring and spark plugs from moisture
- **Cleaner storage** — grass clippings and pollen don't bake onto the machine
- **Resale value** — a well-kept mower holds its value

## Sizing Your Mower Cover

Measure your mower's **length, width and height**, including the handlebar and grass catcher if fitted. Push mowers typically take a small cover (up to 50 in long); ride-on mowers need a larger cover matched to their deck width and overall dimensions. When in doubt, size up — a slightly loose cover is better than one that stretches over sharp edges.

## Material Options

| Material | Best for |
|----------|----------|
| 600D Oxford, PU-coated | All-season outdoor storage |
| PVC / vinyl | Fully waterproof, heavy rain |
| 210D polyester | Indoor shed or garage dust protection |

## Features That Matter

- **Elastic hem** — keeps the cover on in wind
- **Ventilation** — reduces condensation, which can rust the deck from beneath
- **Cut-out or loose fit over the handle** — prevents fabric tearing on corners
- **Reinforced seams** — handles repeated removal and refitting

## Storage Tips

1. Let the mower cool completely before covering — hot engines degrade fabric
2. Clean grass clippings off the deck first
3. For long storage, disconnect the battery and add fuel stabilizer
4. Secure the cover with a bungee or strap in windy areas

## Key Takeaways

- Measure length, width and height including handles and catcher
- 600D Oxford suits all-season outdoor storage best
- Always cover a cool, clean mower
- A covered mower starts reliably and lasts years longer
