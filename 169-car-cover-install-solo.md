# Car Cover Installation Solo — Step-by-Step for One Person

Putting a car cover on alone is frustrating if you don't know the technique. Most people fight with flapping fabric for 5 minutes. With the right method, it's a 60-second job.

## The 60-Second Solo Installation

**Step 1 — Prepare the car (10 seconds):** Fold the windshield mirrors in (if possible). This prevents the cover from snagging and gives a cleaner fit.

**Step 2 — Orient the cover (10 seconds):** Find the front marker (usually a label or the mirror pockets). Hold the cover so the front aligns with the car's front.

**Step 3 — Drape the front (15 seconds):** Stand at the front bumper. Drape the cover's front section over the hood, letting the fabric fall over the mirrors.

**Step 4 — Walk and release (15 seconds):** Walk toward the rear, releasing the cover along the roofline. The fabric settles over the car as you move.

**Step 5 — Pull the rear down (10 seconds):** Pull the remaining fabric over the trunk/rear. The elastic hem should now sit at the bumper line.

**Step 6 — Seat the mirrors and hem (5 seconds):** Push the fabric into the mirror pockets, then run your hand along the bottom edge to seat the elastic hem.

## Common Solo Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Starting at the roof | Fabric bunches at the front |
| Cover inside-out | Check the inner fleece faces the paint |
| Pulling the hem too tight | Snug, not stretched — stress tears seams |
| Ignoring mirror pockets | Loose fabric flaps and scratches |

## The Windy-Day Technique

On windy days, install the cover front-first and immediately anchor the front straps or hem. Then work backward. Letting the whole cover flap in the wind before anchoring invites it to blow off and drag on the pavement.

## Removal

Removal is the reverse: pull the hem up from the rear, fold the cover over itself as you walk forward (accordion-style), and bag it immediately. Never drag the cover across the ground — it picks up grit that scratches paint next time.
