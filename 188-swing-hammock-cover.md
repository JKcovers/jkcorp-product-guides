# Swing and Hammock Cover — Keep Your Relaxation Spot Dry

A porch swing or backyard hammock is built for relaxation — not for weather. Rain soaks the cushions, sun fades the fabric, and birds leave their mark. A swing cover and hammock cover protect both the furniture and your comfort.

## Why Swings and Hammocks Deteriorate

- **Fabric fading:** UV rays bleach swing cushions and hammock webbing
- **Mold and mildew:** damp cushions develop black spots quickly
- **Rust:** chains, hooks, and frames corrode in rain
- **Wear:** wet fabric stretches and frays faster

## Swing Cover Basics

A swing cover should fit the swing's full dimensions — seat, backrest, and arms. Key specs:

| Feature | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Fabric | 420D/600D Oxford, waterproof |
| Coating | PU or PVC backing |
| Closure | Elastic hem + straps |
| Lining | Breathable backer to prevent condensation |

## Hammock Cover Basics

Hammocks need covers that work with their unique shape:

- **Stand hammocks:** a rectangular cover over the whole stand
- **Hanging hammocks:** a hammock-specific cover that drapes over the bed, or a full shelter cover with grommets
- **Canvas hammocks:** dry thoroughly before covering — canvas retains water

## Sizing Your Cover

| Item | How to Measure |
|------|----------------|
| Swing | Seat width x depth x back height (add 10 cm) |
| Hammock | Bed length x spread (add 15-20 cm) |
| Stand | Total width x height of the A-frame |

## Installation Tips

- Cover only dry swings and hammocks
- Use the elastic hem to tuck under the seat or frame
- Secure straps against wind — a flapping cover wears through fast
- For hanging hammocks, take the cover down in strong storms

## Cushion Care Under Covers

Swing cushions are the first casualty. If your swing has cushions, either remove them in winter or use a cushion storage bag. The cover protects the frame; the cushions need their own care.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

- Brush off leaves and bird droppings promptly — droppings are acidic
- Wash covers with mild soap and water each season
- Check grommets and straps for wear
- Let everything dry fully before long-term storage
