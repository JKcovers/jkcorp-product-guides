# Pet Seat Cover Buying Guide — Protect Your Car and Your Dog

A pet seat cover is one of the highest-value car accessories you can buy. It protects your upholstery from claws, fur, drool, and accidents — and keeps your dog secure and comfortable on every ride.

## Why Pet Seat Covers Matter

Dogs shed, drool, and dig. A single long ride without protection can embed fur into fabric seats permanently, and claws can puncture leather in minutes. A quality cover costs $20-60 — a fraction of the $500-1,500 it costs to replace or deep-clean seats.

## Key Features

**Waterproof backing:** Non-negotiable. Dogs drool, have accidents, and shake off rain. A waterproof backing layer prevents moisture from reaching the seats.

**Non-slip surface:** The cover should grip the seat so it doesn't slide when your dog moves. Look for rubberized backing or anchor straps.

**Secure attachment:** Straps that hook around headrests and buckles. A cover that slips is dangerous for both dog and driver.

**Machine washable:** You WILL wash this monthly. Waterproof-backed covers are easier to clean than hammock-style covers.

## Hammock vs Flat Cover

| Type | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| Hammock (attached front/rear) | Blocks dog from front seat, protects seatbacks | More expensive, heavier |
| Flat cover | Cheaper, lighter, easier install | Doesn't protect seatbacks |

## Sizing

Measure the rear seat width. Most covers fit sedans and SUVs; truck rear seats and extended cab pickups need larger sizes. Universal covers with adjustable straps fit most vehicles.
