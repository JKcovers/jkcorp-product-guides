# Outdoor Furniture Cover for Winter — The Full Guide

Winter is the season that destroys outdoor furniture faster than any other. Rain, snow, frost, and UV can warp teak, crack plastic resin, and fade aluminum frames. A quality winter furniture cover is the cheapest insurance you can buy — here is the full guide to choosing and using one correctly.

## Why Furniture Dies in Winter

The damage cycle is predictable:

- **Moisture absorption** — unprotected wood soaks up rain and freezes, splitting seams
- **UV degradation** — winter sun is weaker but still fades fabrics and plastics
- **Freeze-thaw cycling** — water expands inside joints, loosening glue and screws
- **Mold and mildew** — trapped dampness under a poor cover breeds black stains

## What to Look for in a Winter Furniture Cover

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Waterproof (2000mm+) | Sheds rain and melting snow |
| Breathable fabric | Releases condensation, prevents mildew |
| UV-stabilized | Survives months of winter sun |
| Wind straps | Handles winter storms without lifting |
| Padded corners | Protects sharp table and chair edges |

## Sizing Matters

A cover that is too small strains the zippers and leaves furniture exposed. One that is too large pools water and flaps in the wind. Measure the widest point of the set (table + chairs as a group) and add 10-15 cm. Most manufacturers publish size charts — match your measurement to the chart rather than guessing by "6-piece set."

## Indoor vs Outdoor Storage

- **Covered patio or garage:** a breathable dust cover is enough
- **Open deck or yard:** a full waterproof winter cover with straps
- **Extreme snow regions:** choose a heavy-duty cover and clear snow weekly to avoid fabric collapse

## Maintenance During Winter

- Remove snow promptly with a broom — never a shovel
- Check straps after storms and retighten
- If the cover freezes to the furniture, let it thaw before removing
- Air-dry the cover fully in spring before folding it away

## Key Takeaways

- Winter furniture covers must be both waterproof and breathable — one without the other invites rot
- Measure the full set and add 10-15 cm; a properly sized cover protects better than a baggy one
- Clear snow weekly and check straps after storms
- A quality winter furniture cover lasts 3-5 seasons; a cheap one rarely survives one
