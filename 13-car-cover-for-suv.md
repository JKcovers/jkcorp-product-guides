# Best Car Cover for SUVs — What to Look For and Why Size Matters

SUVs present unique challenges for car covers. Their taller profile catches more wind, their longer bodies need more fabric coverage, and roof racks add complexity. Here's what to look for specifically in an SUV car cover.

## Why SUV Covers Are Different

SUV covers need stronger wind resistance than sedan covers simply because of the larger surface area. A cover that flaps on a sedan will be torn off an SUV in the same wind. Look for at least 3 under-body straps and a heavy-duty elastic hem. A 4-layer or higher construction also adds the weight needed to stay in place.

## Roof Rack Compatibility

If your SUV has factory or aftermarket roof rails, standard covers won't fit. You have two options: remove the racks before covering (inconvenient), or order a cover with roof rack allowance. Many OEM manufacturers accept custom dimensions that include roof rack clearance — just provide the rack height when ordering.

## Size Guide for Popular SUV Categories

Compact SUVs (RAV4, CR-V, CX-5): Typically 175-185 inches. A Medium SUV cover works for most.

Mid-size SUVs (Explorer, Highlander, Grand Cherokee): 185-200 inches. Large SUV size.

Full-size SUVs (Tahoe, Expedition, Escalade): 200-220 inches. X-Large SUV or custom.

## Mirror Pockets Are Essential

SUVs have larger side mirrors than sedans. A cover without mirror pockets will have loose, flapping fabric at the widest point of the vehicle. This is both annoying and damaging to your paint. Always choose a cover with pre-shaped mirror pockets sized for SUV mirrors.

## Bottom Line

For SUV covers, prioritize: multi-layer construction for weight and wind resistance, mirror pockets, at least 3 under-body straps, and roof rack accommodation if needed. A quality SUV cover should last 2-3 years outdoors.
