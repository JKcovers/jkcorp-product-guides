# Patio Umbrella Cover Buying Guide — Sizing, Fabric and Fit

Your patio umbrella takes a beating: UV rays fade the canopy, wind shreds the seams, and rain leaves mildew stains that never come out. A patio umbrella cover is the cheapest insurance you can buy for it — here is how to choose the right one.

## Why Use a Patio Umbrella Cover

- **UV protection** — covers block 90%+ of UV, keeping canopy colors vivid
- **Water resistance** — stops pooling water from stretching and sagging fabric
- **Wind protection** — a secured cover stops the canopy from whipping and tearing
- **Less cleaning** — bird droppings, pollen and leaves wipe away instead of grinding in

## Measuring Your Umbrella

Measure the canopy diameter **while the umbrella is fully open**, from one edge across the center to the opposite edge. Covers are sold in sizes like 6 ft, 7.5 ft, 9 ft, 10 ft and 11 ft. Choose the size that matches or slightly exceeds your canopy. Also check the pole length — the cover should reach far enough down the pole to protect it too.

## Fabric Choices

| Material | Best for | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| 600D Oxford, PU-coated | All-season | Heavy-duty, resists wind and snow |
| Polyester, UV-treated | Mild climates | Lightweight, affordable |
| PVC / vinyl | Rainy areas | Fully waterproof, wipe-clean |

## Features That Matter

- **Adjustable drawstring hem** with toggles — keeps the cover on in gusty weather
- **Ventilation vents** — reduce trapped heat and condensation inside
- **Pole strap or buckle** — wraps around the pole for a secure fit
- **Reinforced seams** — double stitching prevents tears at stress points

## Care Tips

- Close and secure the umbrella before covering, or remove it if storing for winter
- Shake off water and debris after storms
- Clean with mild soap and water twice a year
- Store the cover dry in its carry bag when not in use

## Key Takeaways

- Measure the open canopy diameter and match the nearest cover size
- 600D Oxford with drawstring hem handles all seasons best
- Ventilation vents prevent condensation and mildew
- A covered umbrella lasts several seasons longer than an exposed one
