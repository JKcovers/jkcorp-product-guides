# Car Cover Gift Guide — The Unexpected Present Car Guys Actually Want

Car covers make great gifts. They're practical, show thoughtfulness, and work for every budget. Here's how to pick the right one.

## Gift by Relationship

- **New car owner:** A quality outdoor cover protects their biggest purchase. Budget $50-80.
- **Classic car enthusiast:** A fleece-lined indoor cover shows you understand their passion. Budget $80-120.
- **Recent grad or first apartment:** A compact outdoor cover for apartment parking. Budget $30-50.
- **Motorcycle rider:** A touring cover that packs small for road trips. Budget $25-40.

## Gift by Budget

- **Under $30:** PEVA outdoor cover or soft indoor dust cover
- **$30-60:** Mid-range Oxford outdoor cover or fleece indoor cover
- **$60-100:** Multi-layer outdoor cover with UV protection
- **$100+:** Premium custom-fit cover with storage bag and cable lock

## Customization Makes It Special

OEM manufacturers can add embroidered names, custom colors, or personalized storage bags. A cover with the recipient's name or car model adds a personal touch that off-the-shelf covers can't match.
