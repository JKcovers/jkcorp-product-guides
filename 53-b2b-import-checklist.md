# Custom Car Cover Import Guide — A B2B Buyer's Checklist

Importing custom car covers from China can cut your per-unit cost by 40-60% compared to domestic wholesale. But the process has pitfalls. Here's a B2B buyer's checklist.

## Pre-Order Checklist

- **Request fabric samples** in 3-5 materials before placing a bulk order. Colors and textures look different in person.
- **Verify certifications:** ISO 9001 is the minimum. REACH and RoHS are required for EU imports. OEKO-TEX is relevant for indoor covers.
- **Check Alibaba history:** 5+ years on the platform, Trade Assurance eligible, and at least 4.0 star rating.
- **Clarify MOQ:** Ask for the exact minimum per design, per color, and per size. Some factories count each variation as a separate MOQ.

## During Production

- **Approve a pre-production sample:** One physical sample shipped to you before the full run begins. Costs $50-100, credited toward your order.
- **Specify packaging:** Custom packaging takes 3-5 extra days. Confirm box dimensions for shipping cost calculations.
- **Confirm lead time:** Standard is 15-25 days. Custom-printed covers add 5-7 days.

## Shipping

- **Sea freight:** 25-35 days to US West Coast, $500-1,500 per pallet. Best for orders of 500+ units.
- **Air freight:** 5-7 days, 3-5x the cost. Best for samples and urgent restocks.
- **Door-to-door service:** Many Alibaba suppliers offer DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) pricing that includes customs clearance.
