# How to Start a Car Cover Business — OEM Sourcing and Profit Margins

The car cover market is $10.5B globally and growing at 5% annually. Starting your own brand with OEM sourcing is one of the most accessible paths into automotive e-commerce. Here's the playbook.

## Step 1: Define Your Niche

Generic "car covers" face the most competition. Pick a niche:
- Luxury/exotic car covers (higher margins, smaller customer base)
- SUV/truck covers (largest market segment)
- Indoor display covers for dealerships (B2B recurring revenue)
- Eco-friendly/biodegradable covers (differentiation angle)
- Custom-printed promotional covers (corporate gifting)

## Step 2: Source OEM

Find an ISO 9001 certified manufacturer on Alibaba with 5+ years on the platform. Order samples in 3-4 sizes to verify quality. Negotiate MOQ — most factories start at 50-100 pieces per design.

## Step 3: Set Your Pricing

A $15-25 wholesale cover retails for $50-90 on Amazon or your own site. That's a 200-400% markup. Factor in shipping ($5-10/unit for bulk sea freight) and platform fees (15% on Amazon, 3% on Shopify).

## Step 4: Choose Your Channel

- **Amazon FBA:** Fastest path to customers, highest competition
- **Shopify + Google Ads:** Higher margins, requires marketing investment
- **Alibaba wholesale:** Sell B2B to other retailers
- **Dealership direct:** Pitch local dealers on branded covers
