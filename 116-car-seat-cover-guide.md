# Car Seat Covers — The Complete Guide for Car Owners

Car seat covers protect one of your vehicle's most-used and most-expensive surfaces. Whether you want to protect factory upholstery, refresh worn seats, or add comfort, here's everything you need to know.

## Why Seat Covers Matter

Seat upholstery absorbs sweat, spills, UV damage, and daily wear. Factory seats cost $800-2,000+ to replace. A quality seat cover costs $20-80 and lasts 3-5 years — the cheapest insurance for your interior.

## Types of Seat Covers

**Custom-fit:** Made from your exact vehicle template. Perfect fit, OEM look. More expensive.

**Semi-custom:** Fits a range of vehicles in the same class. Good balance of fit and price.

**Universal:** One-size-fits-most. Cheapest but with loose fit and bunching.

## Material Choices

| Material | Best For | Characteristics |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Neoprene | Water sports, outdoor | Waterproof, durable, cushioned |
| Leather/leatherette | Luxury look | Premium appearance, easy wipe |
| Polyester | Daily commuting | Breathable, affordable |
| Canvas | Working vehicles | Tough, stain-resistant |
| Sheepskin/faux | Comfort | Soft, temperature-regulating |

## Key Features

- **Airbag compatibility:** Critical! Covers must have side-airbag cutouts (never block airbag deployment)
- **Headrest/armrest access:** Shouldn't interfere with function
- **Seat heater compatibility:** Check if the cover blocks heat transfer
- **Washability:** Removable and machine-washable covers are far easier to maintain

## Installation Tips

1. Remove headrests, slide the cover over, reinstall headrests
2. Tuck edges between the seatback and bottom cushion
3. Smooth and adjust for wrinkles — bunching causes premature wear
4. Check airbag cutouts align with the seat's airbag location
