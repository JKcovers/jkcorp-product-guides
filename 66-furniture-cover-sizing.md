# Furniture Cover Size Guide — Measure Right, Protect Right

Outdoor furniture covers fail in two ways: too small (doesn't fully protect) or too large (flaps, pools water, and causes damage). Getting the measurement right is the most important step.

## What to Measure

**Width:** Left to right at the widest point (including armrests)
**Depth:** Front to back at the deepest point
**Height:** Ground to the tallest point (backrest or cushions)

## Add the Coverage Allowance

Add 4-6 inches to each dimension. This allows:
- Easy installation and removal
- Air circulation to prevent condensation
- A drape that covers legs and feet completely

## Sectional and Multi-Piece Sets

L-shaped sectionals need careful measuring. Measure each section individually — the two pieces may require separate covers. A single oversized cover over both pieces creates pooling pockets where water collects.

## Special Cases

| Furniture | Measurement Note |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Umbrella table | Measure with umbrella REMOVED, add 6 inches height |
| Lounge chairs | Measure with backrest at full recline |
| Gas fire pit | Measure the widest part including controls |
| Deep sofa | Measure cushion depth — covers sized for standard sofas won't fit |

## Feature Checklist

- Adjustable drawstrings or click straps
- Ventilation flaps (prevent mildew)
- Handles for one-person removal
- Dark color (hides stains)
