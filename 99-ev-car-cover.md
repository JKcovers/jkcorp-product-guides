# EV-Specific Car Covers — What Electric Vehicle Owners Should Know

Electric vehicles have specific protection needs that gas cars don't. Battery thermal management, charging ports, and sensitive electronics make cover choice more consequential.

## What's Different About EVs

**Battery temperature:** EV batteries work best in a temperature range (60-90°F). Extreme heat degrades battery chemistry and can trigger thermal management systems to run longer, draining range. A reflective cover reduces heat soak.

**Charging port exposure:** The charge port door is a weather entry point. Rain and dust near the port can cause charging issues. Covers designed for EVs include charge-port access.

**Regenerative braking electronics:** Sensitive electronics in the undercarriage benefit from reduced moisture exposure.

**Range and energy use:** A car that's been sitting in 150°F sun requires energy to cool the cabin AND the battery before driving. A cool car under a reflective cover uses less range on startup.

## EV Cover Features

- **Reflective outer layer:** Reduces interior AND battery bay temperatures
- **Charge port access:** A zippered or magnetized opening so you can charge without removing the cover
- **Breathable construction:** Battery thermal management creates heat; the cover must not trap it
- **99% UV rejection:** Protects the large glass roofs common on modern EVs

## What EV Owners Should Avoid

- **Non-breathable covers:** Trap heat from battery thermal activity
- **Covers without port access:** Charging requires removal every time
- **Unsecured covers:** EVs have smooth roofs; wind lift is a bigger issue than on cars with roof channels
