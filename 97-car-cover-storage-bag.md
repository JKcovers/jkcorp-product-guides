# Car Cover Storage Bag — The 5-Minute Habit That Doubles Cover Life

The most underused feature of any car cover is its storage bag. Most owners toss the bag aside and leave the cover crumpled in the garage — then wonder why it fails after 12 months instead of 3 years.

## Why the Bag Matters

A loose cover on the garage floor:
- Collects dust and grit that scratches your paint next use
- Gets stepped on and punctured
- Develops mildew if stored damp
- Acquires creases that weaken fabric folds

The bag solves all four — IF you use it consistently.

## The Accordion Fold Method

The fastest way to fold a cover for the bag:

1. Stand at the front of the car, fold the cover in half lengthwise
2. Starting from the front, fold in 18-24" accordion sections toward the rear
3. When you reach the rear, you have a neat stack
4. Slide into the storage bag — no forcing, no cramming

## When to Bag It

**Daily use:** Fold and bag every time you remove the cover. It takes 60 seconds and keeps the cover ready-to-use.

**Wet cover:** NEVER bag a wet cover. Drape it over a railing or the car's roof for an hour, then fold. A damp bagged cover develops mildew within a week.

**Seasonal:** Wash, fully dry, bag, and store in a closet or garage cabinet — not an attic or unheated shed.

## If Your Cover Didn't Come With a Bag

- Any breathable fabric bag works (cotton laundry bag, mesh duffel)
- Avoid plastic bags — they trap moisture
- The bag itself should be washable
