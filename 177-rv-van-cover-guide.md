# Car Cover for RVs and Vans — Large Vehicle Protection

RVs and vans are the largest vehicles owners try to cover — and the most likely to be left uncovered due to cost and hassle. Here's what large-vehicle protection actually requires.

## The Large Vehicle Challenge

A 24-foot RV or full-size van needs a cover the size of a small boat cover:
- 20-30 feet of fabric
- Heavy (20-40 lbs)
- Wind-catching surface area
- Tall roof line

## RV/Van Cover Specs

| Feature | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Fabric | 600D+ heavy-duty Oxford |
| Waterproof | 3000mm+ |
| UV rejection | 95%+ (large painted surfaces) |
| Wind | Multiple straps + elastic hem |
| Access | Zippered door/compartment panels |

## The Access Problem

RVs and vans need access while covered: doors, compartments, roof vents, and hookup points. Quality covers include 2-4 access zippers. Without them, you're removing the cover constantly.

## Size Guide

| Vehicle | Length | Cover Size |
|---------|:--:|:--:|
| Minivan/van | 16-20 ft | Large |
| Class B camper | 16-20 ft | Large |
| Class C motorhome | 20-30 ft | X-Large |
| Class A / fifth wheel | 25-40 ft | XX-Large |

## The Storage Decision

- **Roofed storage:** A breathable dust cover suffices
- **Open storage:** Full weather cover
- **Snow regions:** Heavy-duty cover + periodic snow removal

## Cost-Benefit for RV Owners

An RV cover costs $150-400 — about 1-2% of a typical RV's value. It prevents:
- Roof sealant degradation (leaks are the #1 RV repair)
- Decal fading (expensive to replace)
- Fiberglass oxidation (permanent dullness)
